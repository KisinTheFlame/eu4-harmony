# 2026-10-30 Play-through (remote control Z2, tested by me)

Two short sessions of the game, played through `eu4-harmony game` alone, run from `eu4-harmony\eu4-harmony.exe` in the game folder, where `dev-install.ps1` now installs it with the rest of the player's files: nothing else drove the game, no click and no key, and `eu4-harmony game help` was read first, as an AI would. The first session's build was this branch at `Install and pack eu4-harmony.exe with the framework`, the second's at `Say in the game command's help what each error means and how a game goes`, with what the first session led to; both on the requirement branch at Z1's merge, 567 dependency points matching, 104 hooks and 4 writes, after the build, the tests and the offline checks passed. `eu4.exe` was started directly. Chinese playset with the unchanged regional-combat extension, system fonts. Before each session the files AGENTS.md lists were backed up, with every other file at the top of the user folder and the framework's whole folder. The sessions ran 8 and 4 minutes, each closed once its checks were done; nothing grew slow: a one-day advance at speed 5 took 0.10 to 0.16 s in the engine (0.25 to 0.31 s through the command line), ten days 0.5 to 2.9 s, and no request answered at once took more than 25 ms (a recruit refused; `menu.saves` 15 to 18 ms).

- **The main menu**: at startup `menu` answered after 41 s, `starting`, and `main_menu` 5 s later. `menu.saves` listed 139 saves, 25 with `missing_mods`, as in Z1's run. A test save, `z2-binary-missing-mod` (an ironman save with one mod's descriptor and name changed in its binary `meta`), was listed with that mod missing and refused by `menu.load`, `rejected` at once; the 92 ironman saves were listed with their players and dates and no missing mod.
- **A new game**: `menu.start FRA` answered after 9.6 s with session 1, 1444.11.11. The start-of-game introduction window stayed on screen in the screenshots until time ran. France's opening question (`flavor_fra.206`) was listed and answered; `time.advance 1` at speed 1 took 2.5 s, as speed 1 runs.
- **Observing**: `country.get FRA` (allied to Provence, five subjects), `province.get 183` (巴黎, infantry and cavalry allowed, ships not), `province.list` (4,941 provinces; France 20, England 40), `province.neighbors`, `army.list` (two armies and two fleets, the fleets with `province` null), `army.can_enter`, `army.in_province`, `events`.
- **Orders while paused**, the first session's main finding: after every `time.advance` the game is paused, and `army.find_route`, `army.move` and `army.stop` were refused `not_ready`, "game is paused". The only way on was `time.resume`, the order and `time.pause`, during which the game, at speed 5, ran on 11 days in 0.85 s. With the second session's build, at 1444.11.11 with the game paused, `army.find_route` answered `[4388]`, `army.move` and `army.stop` `null`, three `army_ordered` events came, the date stayed 1444.11.11, and the army reached 185 as soon as time ran.
- **Recruiting**: infantry in Paris answered `null`, and the next read had 10 ducats and 1,000 men less. In 1475, artillery was refused "the player has no unit type of the kind artillery" and a heavy ship "建立需要51¤。".
- **Questions and offers**: two questions answered (`flavor_fra.206`, `consort_events.51`), one in 1475 with two options (`incidents_bur_inheritance.301`); royal marriage offers from Scotland accepted and from Castile declined, both gone from `offer.list` at once. A question that came up on the last day of an advance was answered `date`; a later advance answered `question` at once.
- **Diplomacy**, first session: an alliance proposed to Scotland `accepted` (57 to 70 ms after it ran), one to Castile the same day refused "你必须等待一天才可以提出另一个结盟提议。" and `accepted` the next day; on 1444.12.12 `diplomacy.war_goals ENG` listed seven `cb_core`/`take_core` war goals, one claimed for Armagnac (`@AMG 收回核心省份拉布尔`), then `none`; `declare_war ENG cb_core --province 167` answered `declared` with "法兰西收复科之战" and `called: []`, France's three allies passed over with nothing said; the war had France and its subjects attacking, England, Leinster and Portugal defending. A white peace was refused during the diplomat's wait and later `declined` (74 ms).
- **Diplomacy**, second session: Castile refused an alliance with its reasons (rival; opinion -78); the declaration on England answered `passed_over` for Scotland and Provence, each with the call box's tooltip ("估计他们不会接受。", for 50 and against -1004, "苏格兰没有欠法兰西至少10点人情：-1000"). In the 1475 save, after the game console's `favors` with each ally, `diplomacy.war_goals HAB` offered `cb_humiliate_rotw`/`humiliate_rotw` with `target` HAB; declaring it with `--call-allies BOH,PAP` was `rejected` with the Papal State's tooltip and nothing sent (`country.get` had no war); with every ally, `declared` "法兰西羞辱奥地利之战", `called` Bohemia, `passed_over` the Ottomans ("正在打另一场战争：-1000") and the Papal State; two days later Bohemia and its subjects were among the attackers. The declarations ran in 66 and 93 ms.
- **The game console**: `cash 100` "Gold changed by 100"; an unknown command `rejected` "Command not found. Did you mean 'run_commands'?"; in the ironman save `rejected` "Command not available in multiplayer or ironman mode."; `favors <tag> 30` and `observe` answered what they print.
- **Frames the player cannot play**: in the second session `time.advance 30 --speed 1` was under way when a second client ran `observe`; the advance answered `not_in_game`, "the player can no longer play the game (no controllable player)", 1.41 s after `observe` ran (Z1's run, before the 1 s wait: 0.75 s), and `session` then `not_in_game`, `menu` `game`.
- **Camera and screenshots**: `camera.look_at_province 167` looked at 2820, 1506; `camera.zoom 600` kept it there; four screenshots at 25% (836 to 932 KB, encoded in 36 to 54 ms) showed the game as the screen did.
- **Back and into a game**: `menu.return` answered `ending` twice (once while observing); `menu` answered `not_ready` from the old process, then nothing until the new process presented its first frame, 42 s later, `starting`, then `main_menu` 3 s after. `menu.load Castilla-5` (ironman, binary `meta`, mods all in the playset) answered after 8.2 s with session 2, 1470.3.13, `CAS`; `menu.load 法兰西1475_05_07` (compressed text) after 4.5 s.
- **A plain save's long list of mods**: `z2-many-mods`, a copy of the plain autosave with 1,000 more entries in its list of mods, the list ending at byte 78,023, was listed with player `ETH`, 1445.1.1 and no missing mod. It was not loaded.
- **The help, read as an AI**: the descriptions and help changed after the first session were read from the running game in the second (`describe`: 46 capabilities, `declare_war`'s result naming `passed_over`).
- **Not seen in the running game**: a named ally that the declare war window no longer lists once another is called (unit tests); a failure at a game's first frame that passes, for the 1 s wait (unit test of the wait); a waiting load or start answered at a frame the player cannot play (no load came to one: the load makes the save's country the player); an accepted peace in this run (C8's run saw one); `camera`, `camera.look_at`, `army.strait_open`, `army.can_attack` and `army.selected`, not called; `FakeGame`'s new order of refusals, `Date::months_later` and `game_string::written` on their own (unit tests, and the capabilities that use them answered as before); `tools/release.ps1`'s zip installed in a game folder (it was made and unpacked, and its `eu4-harmony.exe` ran, answering exit code 3 with no game).
- **Cleanup**: each session's game was closed by ending its process; no crash folder was made. After each, the save games folder and the framework's folder were replaced by the backup's and every file at the top of the user folder put back, which removed the test saves and whatever the game had rewritten; all 162 files compared equal to the backup. The framework was uninstalled, which put back the earlier `version.dll`; the backups, logs and screenshots of the run were deleted.
