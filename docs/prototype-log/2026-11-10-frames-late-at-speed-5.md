Where the stutter the owner saw at speed 5 comes from, and what deferring the map's owner mark and making the map's names at the update gives ([research/drawing-beside-the-day.md](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md), "Frames late at speed 5"). Hidden test games from this branch's core with `dev-test-games`, the 1780.7.1 save ([research/late-game.md](../research/late-game.md), "Saves to test with") in observe mode, the camera on Constantinople (151) at a height of 700, a year or two at speed 5 each (`time.advance`); the player's playset and settings, 3840×2160 borderless, a display of 144 Hz. The drawing thread's log line counts the frames late in ten seconds by their cause.

- **Before** (the deferred calls already at the day's end): 50 to 122 frames a second; 24 to 74 late in ten seconds, 20 to 43 held back, 0 to 9 waiting for the lock, the longest gap 118 to 206 ms; 11 to 22 frames drawn by the main thread.
- **Attributed**: a one-off count of the stacks that let mesh objects go, built in for these runs and not kept, put them in the map's markers' update, while the main thread held the lock; the frames they seemed to hold back were the log's old count. The late frames held back with the drawing thread holding the lock came first from `0x10af990` (the map told of a province's new owner), 7 to 28 in ten seconds, then from mesh objects let go and from scenes begun.
- **The owner mark deferred, first as a function of the framework's**: the game crashed at `0xa0e748` within the first game year, an access through `r10` after the call, which the replacement had changed. Through a stub that keeps every register: two game years without a crash; `0x10af990` no longer among the causes; 94 to 105 frames a second, 44 to 54 late, 30 to 39 held back, by mesh objects (19 to 20) and scenes begun (10 to 16).
- **The map's names made at the update** besides: no frame drawn by the main thread; 114 to 128 frames a second, 26 to 58 late, 11 to 31 held back, the longest gap 160 to 187 ms. The remaining mesh objects let go came from the commands' step before the day (18 of the last 36 counted); those in frames inside a day hold nothing back, as those frames are prepared.
