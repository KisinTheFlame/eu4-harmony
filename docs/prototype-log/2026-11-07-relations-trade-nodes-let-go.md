# 2026-11-07 The relations and the trade nodes let go (tested by me)

The relations the diplomatic histories leave and the trade nodes each world clear replaces (research/main-menu.md, "The relations a diplomatic history keeps", "The trade nodes"), found with `cargo xtask scenario leak`: four rounds watching sixteen sites from the first, `0xb6654f`, `0xb6abfa`, `0x1bebf2`, `0xa5c2f9`, `0xa5c368` and others. Then the repairs (`core/src/repairs/histories.rs`, `trade_nodes.rs`), four rounds of `leak` with the quarantine, and `growth`. Every run within the runner's claim.

- **Before**: each new game and load from the main menu left the relations, `0xa5c4b1` 29 KB a generation, `0xa5c2f9` 14, `0xa5c368` 10, `0xa5c50b` 5, last pointed at by the diplomatic history's records (1,313 of 2,092 and 1,010 of 1,647 that nothing pointed at, the rest unrecorded or blocks that pointed at them for a moment); each generation left the trade nodes and their routes, `0xb6654f` 18 KB, `0xb6abfa` 19, `0x1bebf2` 23, `0x42f92e` 16, `0x1bc17e` 6, and `0xcc2a3` 35, none of them held by nothing.
- **The quarantine, the repairs deleting at once** (the relations at the world's next clear, the trade nodes as their database went): four rounds, no crash; each new game's and load's history left 911 to 917 relations, none kept as given to the diplomacy, and no "DELETING RELATION OUTSIDE OF PERMITTED CONTEXT" in the game's `error.log`; each world reset deleted 80 trade nodes. The quarantine kept 6,395 blocks of the relations (0.6 MB) and 15,564 of the trade nodes (1.3 MB), and nothing was written to after a repair let it go. The relations' and the trade nodes' sites were in no later generation's leftovers, and `0xcc2a3` left 2 KB a generation against 35.
- **Without the quarantine, the same**: ten rounds of `growth` ended at the eighth, `menu.load` of the in-game load answering "call 0x7ff6558d0580 faulted: 0xc0000005" (the in-game load, `0x5d0580`), the game running on; twelve rounds ended at the seventh, the game crashing in a return after a new game, at the switch that deletes the map manager's popups (`0x81e953`), in a sprite's hide (`0x15bd50f`) and the texture code under it (`0x1654af0`, `0x1655425`), read from the dump's stack. The quarantine frees nothing and so hid what read them. Not found which window read what; the framework's call now says where a fault was and what it read or wrote.
- **The repairs waiting for a return**: fifteen rounds of `growth`, no crash, every answer right, each round 26 s; each new game's and load's history's relations and each world reset's trade nodes (80, or 160 when two had waited) deleted at the world's clear after the next return. Over rounds 2 to 15: the game's heap held 0.78 MB a round more, against 1.18; the private bytes grew 8.2 MB a round, 6.9 MB the heaps' unused pages.
- **Not verified**: the quarantine with the repairs waiting for a return; more than fifteen rounds; ironman; sessions with in-game loads and no return, where they wait as long as the game left them; what in the interface read the trade nodes or the relations deleted at once.
- **Cleanup**: each release put back the player's files and the game folder's framework files and found them as the backup had them; the reports, the dump and the logs the releases kept were deleted.
