# 2026-11-10 A hidden game at full speed (tested by me)

Whether Windows' power throttling slows a game started hidden, and what opting out of it gives ([hidden-start.md](../plans/hidden-start.md)). Two cores built with `dev-test-games` from this branch's commit, one with `hidden` as on master and one opting the process out of execution-speed and timer-resolution throttling; two test games (`hidden-before`, `hidden-after`) held at once and started hidden one after the other, each with its core; the 1780.7.1 save ([research/late-game.md](../research/late-game.md), "Saves to test with") copied into each one's `save games`, `menu.load`, `console run observe`, `time.set_speed 5`, both resumed together; the player's playset and settings, 3840×2160 borderless, vertical sync on. Beside them ran another session's test game and a game of no test game's; the machine's processors were 41% busy.

- **Found first**, the evening before, in other sessions' runs: hidden test games at speed 5 used 5 to 35% of a core, where a game needs one for its main thread alone; the `long` scenario slowed from 86 s a game year to more than ten minutes and was ended as hung. Setting the running `growth` game out of execution-speed throttling (`SetProcessInformation`, `ProcessPowerThrottling`) raised it from 5% to 84% of a core at once.
- **The log**: the changed core logged "hidden start: opted out of Windows' power throttling" at its start; the other, nothing.
- **Side by side**, in 60 s after 20 s of play: the changed game went from 1781.2.1 to 1782.9.4, about 580 days; the other from 1780.7.9 to 1780.8.1, 23 days. Twice over 30 s after that: the changed game used 168% and 188% of a core and passed 267 and 245 days; the other 11% and 14%, and 12 and 11 days.
- **Not verified**: which of the two opt-outs gives what (only both together were tried); a game shown with `desktop.show` and then behind other windows; a hidden game alone, against the speed of one shown; other machines, power plans and Windows versions than this Windows 11 on AC power; the graphics card's share, which one of the throttled games was seen to use little of.
- **Cleanup**: both test games released, their logs kept; the cores' copies were in the worktree's `target\`, removed with it.
