# 2026-11-07 What the heap's free pages are, after the windows (tested by me)

Why the game's heap still commits 8 to 12 MB a round it does not use (sessions.md, "Where the private bytes go"), now that what the game holds grows 2.1 to 2.4 MB. Master at `05022e8`, the memory account's build with an experiment, not committed: `memory` answered too with a walk of the game's heap (`HeapWalk` under its lock: its regions, its busy blocks, and its free blocks by size, with the whole pages in them), the heap's own fields (its decommit thresholds), and in the first two runs what `HeapSetInformation` with `HeapOptimizeResources` and then `HeapCompact` gave back right then. Three runs of `cargo xtask scenario growth`, 6, 3 and 10 rounds, each within the runner's claim.

- **Giving back later gives back nothing**: 5 s after each return, at the main menu, giving back the free pages again took 0 ms and the private bytes fell 0 to 3 MB. What the first frame after the world reset gives back is all there is, though the battles and the panels' windows go after it.
- **What the free bytes are**, at the main menu after a round: 2.4 to 2.9 million free blocks, 190 to 320 MB. Of them, 110 to 160 MB in blocks under 4 KB, growing 6 to 7 MB a round with about 30,000 blocks, beside the 24,000 blocks and 2.3 MB a round the game holds more; 13 to 18 MB in blocks of 4 to 64 KB; and 60 to 155 MB in blocks of 64 KB to 1 MB, nearly all over 256 KB, which grew in the first three rounds and then stayed. The heap's thresholds are 16 KB for a free block and 64 KB for its free bytes in all (`0x400` and `0x1000` units of 16 bytes); blocks from about 1 MB are its own allocations (`0xff00`).
- **`HeapCompact`** of the default heap, after the pages were given back, decommitted 40 to 76 MB of those large free blocks at once, in 0 ms (the private bytes fell 33 to 38 MB). Done at the first frame after every world reset, ten rounds: no crash, the whole pages in free blocks 3 to 4 MB at every main menu against 60 to 160, the heap's free bytes about 140 MB lower; but the private bytes grew 13.7 MB a round over rounds 2 to 10, the heaps' unused pages 8.3 MB and outside them 3.1 MB, as without it (13.3 to 14.1 MB, 2026-11-06). The next session takes the pages again: the large free blocks are a level the sessions share, not a growth, and compacting lowers the main menu only. Not committed.
- **So**: what grows a round of the heap's unused pages is the small free blocks among the blocks the sessions leave, about three times their bytes; the framework cannot give them back, and letting go of what the sessions leave is what lowers them.
- **Not verified**: whether the small free blocks are the low-fragmentation heap's or its back end's, which the walk does not tell apart; what the private bytes outside the heaps, going up and down by 40 MB from round to round, do over more rounds.
- **Cleanup**: the experiment was put back before anything was committed. Each release put back the player's files and the game folder's framework files and found them as the backup had them; the reports were in the session's scratch folder, deleted after.
