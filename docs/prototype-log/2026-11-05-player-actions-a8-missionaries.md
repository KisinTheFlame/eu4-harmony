# 2026-11-05 Player actions A8: missionaries, colonists, trade companies, centers of trade, merchants (tested by me)

A8 of [player-actions.md](../player-actions.md), developer builds of this branch with the command log (and, for the native policy, the test switches' posts), within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset, a 3840x2160 window. A new game as the Ottomans at 1444.11.11, then `tag POR` from the game console; then new games as Portugal and its autosave loaded again after each build. Everything through remote control; the console's `add_colonist`, `own`, `cash` and `tag` set the cases up. Each command the layer posted showed in the command log as the game executed it, in the written form [research/envoys.md](../research/envoys.md) gives.

- **Missionaries** (the Ottomans, one missionary): `missionary.options` allowed Edirne (149) and other Orthodox provinces, each at its own upkeep (0.584 ducats a month for Edirne), refused Anatolia's with "这些民众已经信奉了正统信仰。" and Constantinople (151, Byzantium's) with "你不能将传教士派往你尚未控制的省份。". `missionary.send 149` was executed (`send_missionary={ … country="TUR" owned_by="TUR" location=149 }`); then 149 read `working` with progress 0 and its send refused with "一名传教士已在埃迪尔内工作。 于1444.11.11完成。"; a second send elsewhere was refused with "没有可供派遣的传教士。"; `missionary.recall 149` posted `cancel=yes` and 149 was free again; a second recall was refused by the framework ("the player has no missionary there"); province 99999 was `not_found`. `missionary.automatic` read 1 missionary, 0 automatic; raise made it 1, a second raise and a second lower were refused by the framework, lower made it 0.
- **Centers of trade** (Portugal, Lisbon 227 at level 2, 38 ducats): upgrade refused with "升级到世界级港口需要25.00发展度，但是该省份仅有16.00" and "升级此贸易中心需要1000.00¤，而你只有38.10¤。"; downgrade (10 prestige) was executed: level 1, prestige 4.963 → -5.037; a second downgrade refused with "无法降级此贸易中心，已经是最低级别！" and "负威望时无法降级贸易中心！"; after `cash 2000` the upgrade (200 ducats) was executed, level 2, treasury down by 200. Stockholm (1, Sweden's) was refused with the game's "所有者变更时贸易中心将被降一级。" among its reasons; a province without one was refused by the framework.
- **Merchants** (Portugal, two merchants steering in Tunis and Safi): `merchant.list` and `merchant.options` gave the missions and the nodes downstream; `steer_to tunis valencia` was executed and Tunis then steered to Valencia; steering in the home node Sevilla was refused with "你不能在你获取贸易收入的节点引导贸易。" and a steer there with "你在本埠节点永远只能获取贸易收入，你无法转移这里的贸易价值。"; `recall tunis` was executed and the merchant left the list; sending with none free was refused with "必须拥有一个可用商人。"; `steer genua` was executed and read `arriving` until it got there; `collect genua` while it travelled changed its mission (`type=0` for the same merchant, as the game's own button does); recalling it on its way was refused by the framework. A first build counted a merchant on its way as not there, and a recalled one as still going; both were fixed (the mission's node `+0x80` and phase `+0x98`) and tried again.
- **Colonists** (Portugal, after `add_colonist`): every province was refused with "在派遣殖民队之前，我们需要选择原住民政策。" until a native policy was set (a `set_native_policy` posted by the test switches, which the game's check takes only once the country has a colonist, so the game was saved and loaded); then the Azores (367) and Madeira (368), not yet discovered, were refused with no reason (the panel shows nothing there), and Arguin (1111) was allowed. `colonist.send 1111` was executed; 1111 then read `working`, its send refused with "一支殖民队将在1445年1月04日到达。其成功率为1.40%。" and "殖民者正从葡萄牙赶来。"; with none left another send was refused with "没有可供派遣的殖民队。". `colonist.recall` was executed, but the colonist stays until the game's next day, then the province is free again.
- **Trade companies** (Portugal, Goa 2030 given with `own`): `trade_company.add 2030` was executed and Goa read `in_trade_company`; a second add was refused with no reason (the panel shows the remove button then); Lisbon was refused with "贸易公司只能在自治领地上建立。". The investments of Goa's area listed 15 with their names in the player language ("葡萄牙定居点"), each refused for want of money with "你负担不起200¤" and the like; with money, `trade_company.invest 2030 local_quarter` took 200 ducats and the investment then read `made`, refused again with "你已经在卡纳达区域投资过当地驻防了。"; an unknown key was `not_found`; `trade_company.remove 2030` was executed, and adding again was refused with "1449年12月07日前不能将这个省份添加给贸易公司。". A first build read the investments' array as a vector (it is a pointer and a count) and kept the condition lines that hold and their heading; both were fixed and tried again.
- **Not tried**: the panels on screen (only the reasons' texts were compared with what the game writes for its tooltips); a colony growing to a city; a missionary's conversion finishing; a merchant collecting outside its home node with a trade post; an extension calling these capabilities; a subject's center of trade; a save other than the run's own autosave.
- **Cleanup**: the release put the player's files back (every file as the backup had it, the run's autosaves among them) and the game folder's framework files; `dev.toml` and the screenshots were deleted.
