# 2026-11-08 A country's knowledge refreshes, counted (tested by me)

How often the game refreshes a country's knowledge (`0x93fe30`), from where, and whether a refresh changes anything ([research/country-knowledge.md](../research/country-knowledge.md)). An experiment build of master at `1578f775`, not kept: the release build with the test games' pipes, and a module of its own called around the game's refresh in the framework's observer of it (`core/src/repairs/supporters.rs`), on the main thread only. For each refresh it timed the game's call, told one asked for by `needs_refresh` by its label (the country's `+0x2858`, or `country.cpp:9209`), and compared the country before and after: its 0x9b78 bytes word by word (but `+0x2858`, `+0x2880` and the heads of the lists and vectors compared), a digest of the items of 15 lists and of the bytes of 6 vectors. The first 300 refreshes were done twice, and the words that differed between the two left out of the comparisons after. Every 250 game days it logged the counts by label.

On a test game: the 1780.7.1 save copied into its `save games`, `menu.load`, `console run observe`, `session.view TUR`, `time advance --days 2600 --speed 5`.

- **First run**, 1780.7.1 to 1787.8.15: 33,574 refreshes in 2,500 days, 14,661 of them asked for, 283 µs each, 3.80 ms a day. The double refreshes ran during the load, on the load's thread, and found no word that changes, so this run's counts of what changed are not used; its counts by label are in the note.
- **Second run**, on the main thread only, the changes counted word by word and list by list: 33,851 refreshes in 2,501 days, 14,313 asked for, 8,703 changed nothing, 266 µs each, 3.60 ms a day; 9,466 of a country refreshed already that day, 4,233 of them changed nothing; 11 of 4,975 words changed at every refresh. The words and lists that changed most are in the note. Its log was replaced by the later runs' before its counts by label were copied out.
- **Third run**: the experiment did not build (a type), and the old build ran 500 days; not used.
- **Fourth run**, 300 days: the world's array at `+0x1d08`, which the refresh walks, held 118 countries, each with a valid tag. Its first 250 days, 7,014 refreshes, 2,813 of them changing nothing, 2,089 of a country refreshed already that day, stand for the second run's first 250 days in the note.
- **Not verified**: what changed, label by label; a game a player plays; other saves.
- **Cleanup**: the experiment's code removed from the checkout; the release removed the test game's folder but `kept\` and said none of its four games crashed.
