- **Deferred in a day only**, a first build: three game years in 141 s, with the computer's load at 73% from other work (the days' pace not comparable); a port's model still held frames back, called in the commands' step, which is not in a day. Deferred by caller instead.
- **By caller**, the next build: **the game crashed** after about four months, at `0x1540f20` on the drawing thread, in the 3D map's shadow pass (`0x10a57ad` → `0x1540c70`): a mesh object's parts (`+0xa0`) read as null. A port's model done with the deferred calls at the preparation had let its mesh object go at once, as the deferred calls' own calls were not deferred again, after the update that takes a model off the markers' lists: the lists held it, and the drawing thread drew it. The crash's folder is in its claim's `kept\crashes\`; the place and the stack read from its minidump.
- **Fixed**: a mesh object the main thread frees waits for the next preparation also when a deferred call frees it. Five game years in 102 s (56 ms a day, the computer's load at 53%): no crash, no error in the log; 104 to 136 frames a second, 20 to 61 late in ten seconds, 0 to 1 held back, by the monthly refresh only, 8 to 29 waiting for the lock, the longest gap 40 to 179 ms.
