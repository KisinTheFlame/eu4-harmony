# 2026-11-10 The tooltips updated before the frame is prepared (tested by me)

Where the renderer's scenes that held the drawing thread back came from, and what updating the tooltips before the frame is prepared gives ([research/drawing-beside-the-day.md](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md), "Frames late at speed 5"). Hidden test games from this branch's core with `dev-test-games`, the 1780.7.1 save in observe mode, the camera on Constantinople, at speed 5 (`time.advance`); the player's playset and settings, 3840×2160 borderless, a display of 144 Hz.

- **The stacks**: a count of the stacks that began a scene with the frame not yet marked changed, built in for this run and not kept, over three game years: every stack but the loading screen's went through the in-game frame's call of the tooltips' update after its drawing (`0x81594e`, the return from `0x6eb6e0`), to the tooltip's text drawn into its texture (`0x15979a4` in `0x1596660`). 59 came from frames between days, the rest from frames inside a day (`0x769fb7`).
- **The tooltips updated before the frame is prepared**, the game's call after the drawing taken out: two game years at speed 5, no crash, no error in the log; no frame held back by a scene (before: 2 to 8 in ten seconds in the same hidden game, where the cursor stays still); 116 to 136 frames a second, 29 to 83 late in ten seconds, 0 to 11 held back, by mesh objects let go (`0x153e560`) and the monthly refresh (`0x81e280`), 0 to 3 waiting for the lock, the rest (26 to 82) neither.
- **The tooltips still work**: a screenshot showed Constantinople's tooltip under the cursor; the cursor moved to a province of Poland-Lithuania (`input.move`), the next showed that province's tooltip there.
- **Not verified**: a game shown, the map dragged with the cursor over provinces and units, where the owner's game had the scenes hold back 18 to 39 frames in ten seconds; tooltips over windows and buttons; the frames late with neither cause, which are now the most.
- **Cleanup**: every test game released, their logs kept in their claims' `kept\`; the stack count taken out of the code.
