# 2026-11-09 The drawing thread's measures, its crossings and its first runs (tested by me)

This branch ([drawing-thread.md](../plans/drawing-thread.md), phases 0 to 2), built with `dev-test-games` and as each run says with the call trace or `dev-crossings`, on one test game held throughout; the 1780.7.1 save ([research/late-game.md](../research/late-game.md), "Saves to test with") copied into its `save games`, `menu.load`, `console run observe`, `session.view TUR`, `time.set_speed 5`, `time.resume`; the player's playset and settings, "Smooth map", 3840×2160 borderless, hidden. Other sessions' test games ran on the computer at the same time. What came out is weighed in [research/drawing-beside-the-day.md](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md).

- **M1, pages changed**, a diagnostic of the call trace's for this run only, since dropped: every committed private read-write page and `eu4.exe`'s data hashed at the daily tick's entry and return. Ordinary days (1780.7.1, 7.29, 7.30, 8.1): the heap 21.4 to 37.5 MB of 1,798 to 1,808 MB, `eu4.exe` 0.2 MB, elsewhere 19.5 to 38.2 MB, most of it in two regions of 14,228 and 13,410 pages; the month's turn (7.31): 93.0 MB, 0.2 MB and 74.0 MB. A hash of the whole took 20 to 90 s, so only the ticks around a month's turn were measured.
- **M2 and M3**, the call trace's `times` on the interface's steps and the crossings' entries, 55 days: the day's body 50.1 ms (median), a frame's markers 1.7 ms, alerts 1.3, the GUI's update 0.8, input 0.5, the outliner 0.4, a drawing 8.6; 3.5 application frames and 2 drawings a day; a unit's model 51 times a day, the first 17.6 ms into the body; a message 1.9 times; the daily refresh and the minimap once, 33.8 ms into the body at the earliest.
- **The crossings' tool**, 1,050 days, after a first run of 325 with narrower ranges: 326 stacks, 105,408 calls in 66,786 stretches; the table is in the research note.
- **The drawing thread**, `drawing_thread=yes`. Runs that failed, in order: the framework stopped at startup, a global not checked by a dependency point; the map's markers' update crashed at the first day (`0x1323ee1`) until the observer on a text floating on the map (`0x6e9860`), which reads six arguments, forwarded them all instead of four; the drawing thread's frame faulted at `0x1540f20` reading 0 after 30 s, until it waited after a crossing for the next update; with that, once more in 60 s, a frame left out and the game going on.
- **Days a second**, 60 s from the load each: 19.6 with the drawing on the main thread (1780.7.2 to 1783.9.20); 16.5 and 18.7 with the drawing thread (to 1783.3.21 and 1783.8.1). The drawing thread drew 68 to 99 frames a second at speed 5; the main thread waited 1.1 to 1.6 s in 10 s for the lock, at 230 to 490 crossings in 10 s. Paused, with no limit and without vertical sync (`vsync=no` in the test game's `settings.txt`): 196 frames a second, the main thread waiting 69% of the time.
- **Not verified**: the camera at the drawing thread's rate, a hidden test game moving no mouse; a game the player sees, with vertical sync; the frames' pixels against the game's; saves other than 1780.7.1; more than about four minutes of play with the drawing thread; a game started, loaded or left with it on beyond the one load each run.
- **A startup crash** in the LoadingThread (`0xa402ee`, before the main menu) in one start, on master's mesh types loaded beside the databases, which master has since taken back (#150).
- **Cleanup**: the release kept the test game's logs; the diagnostics' copies and the crash dumps' readings are in the session's scratchpad.
