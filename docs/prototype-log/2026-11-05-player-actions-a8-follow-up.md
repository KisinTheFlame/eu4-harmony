# 2026-11-05 Player actions A8 follow-up: trading policies, colonial upkeep, expelling minorities, settlement growth (tested by me)

What A8 left undone ([player-actions.md](../player-actions.md), A8). Developer builds of this branch with the command log and the test switches' posts, within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset. A new game as Portugal at 1444.11.11, then a new game as Castile, its autosaves of 1445.1.1 and 1446.1.1 loaded so that the test switches' `set_native_policy` was posted at the load (the game's check takes it only once the country has a colonist). Everything through remote control; the console's `add_colonist`, `add_idea_group` (expansion and exploration, which came in whole and gave two colonists that last), `discover`, `own`, `add_core` and `population` set the cases up. Each command the layer posted showed in the command log in the written form [research/envoys.md](../research/envoys.md) gives.

- **Trading policies** (Portugal, its merchant steering in Tunis): `merchant.policies tunis` listed the database's nine policies in the game's order with their names (利润最大化, 渗透式贸易, …), `maximize_profit` current, `hostile_trading` and `establish_communities` allowed, the others refused with the game's lines: the upgraded ones with their country flag ("拥有国家标志'enabled_trading_policy_maximize_profit_upgraded'"), `improve_inland_routes` with "突尼斯:" and "葡萄牙的贸易竞争力达到 50%。", `propagate_religion` with its whole failing condition list. So the policy's trigger holds its `potential` and `can_select` together. In Sevilla, where Portugal has no merchant, every policy was refused with "葡萄牙在塞维利亚有一个商人团", and `set_policy sevilla maximize_profit` with it. `set_policy tunis hostile_trading` was executed (`select_trading_policy={ … members={ dummy="POR" dummy=58 dummy=2 } }`); then `hostile_trading` read current, and every other policy, `maximize_profit` among them, was refused with the game's cooldown text "你必须等到 1445年11月11日 才能在这个贸易节点选择一个新的贸易政策。", which `set_policy tunis establish_communities` gave as its refusal; choosing `hostile_trading` again was refused by the framework ("the player already follows hostile_trading there"), an unknown key was `not_found`.
- **Colonial upkeep**: `colonist.options` gave a colonist's work 0.333 ducats a month with no colonist, 2.00 with one or two (the share beyond the country's colonists falls away). With one console colonist promoting settlement growth in Lisbon, the budget's 殖民地维护费 was 2.00 a month, where the first build had given a twelfth of it: the sum is a month's, not a year's, and the build was fixed. Not as the budget later shows: Havana, given 2.00 before the colony was founded, cost with Madrid's settlement growth 8.00 a month together once it had settlers, and 10.00 alone a month later: a settled colony's own modifiers come on top of what the game counts for a province without settlers, which is what `colonist.options` gives.
- **Expelling minorities** (Castile, the Bahamas given with `own`, `add_core` and `population` as a port within range): Havana (484, a colonial region) listed 19 of Castile's provinces (Andalusian and others), each refused with the send's own reasons while Havana was out of range ("这个省份太远了。" and the range lines); in range, 15 were allowed and 4 refused with the game's "我们只能把少数族群从我们目前控制的省份中驱逐出去。", which `expel_minorities 484 206` gave as its refusal. Arguin and Bermuda listed none (no colonial region). `expel_minorities 484 224` was executed (`send_colonist={ … country="CAS" location=484 settlement_growth_construction=no expulsion_province=224 }`); Havana then read `working`, its province list the game's "这里目前有一个殖民地正在建造中。", and the colony grew with a `minority_expulsion` construction; Madrid (217), Castilian and not listed, was refused by the framework ("the colonize button lists no minority to expel from province 217").
- **Settlement growth**: Lisbon (Portugal, a console colonist) and Madrid (217, Castile) were allowed and `start_settlement_growth` was executed (`send_colonist={ … settlement_growth_construction=yes expulsion_province=0 }`); the province then read `growing`, and a second start was refused with the game's "一个殖民队正在马德里提高省份发展。 于1446.07.10完成。" (the first build answered no reason there, as it did not ask the game while growing; fixed). Sevilla, from which the minority was being expelled, was refused with "我们正在将塞维利亚的少数族群驱逐到哈瓦那。", Arguin (not Portugal's) with no reason, as the game's check writes none for the panel's own rules, and with no colonist free with "没有可供派遣的殖民队。". `stop_settlement_growth 217` was executed (`cancel_colonist={ … location=217 settlement_growth_construction=yes }`); the province still read `growing` until the game's next day, as a recalled colonist does, so a second stop that day was posted too; then it was free. Stopping in Sevilla, where there was none, was refused by the framework. Lisbon's console colonist went at the next month and the growth with it.
- **Not tried**: the panels on screen (the trade view's policy buttons, the colonize button's expel list and the settlement growth button were not compared with what they show); a unique policy another country follows; a policy chosen again after its cooldown; whether the expel list's provinces are those the window shows, by minority; a colony founded by an expulsion finishing; the modifier `expel_minorities_cost`; an extension calling these capabilities.
