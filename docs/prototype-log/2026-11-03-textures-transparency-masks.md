# 2026-11-03 Textures' transparency masks (tested by me)

What the census's largest site, `0x950ce` (the vectors' generic allocation), stood for, and the repair of the textures' transparency masks (`core/src/repairs.rs`; research/font-textures.md, "Transparency masks"). Experiment builds, not committed: the census counting a block from the generic allocation at the calls above it, two then four calls up; the texture loader's calls traced with their files (the `trace` feature, `EU4HARMONY_TRACE=0x16c2980`); the texture manager's masks read at each world reset; the window holders the game makes and lets go, listed at the main menu after each return; the quarantine of what the repairs free, as on 2026-11-03 before. Nine processes under the game lock, which backed up and restored the player's files; the framework files in the game folder copied aside first. `eu4.exe` started directly, the playset as installed, driven by `eu4-harmony game`, rounds as in the acceptance.

- **The census, four rounds**: over ten generations the sessions left 3 to 6 MB each; `0x950ce` 9 MB of it, 353 blocks; counted further up, 7.4 MB of those came from the texture loader (`0x16c2980`) resizing a mask. Most of the rest was the interface's (`0x155c294`, `0x1529f85`, `0x156acc9` and others, about 2.5 MB a generation) and the history's (`0x2976f0`, about 0.8 MB in the generations that read one). A generation's size two resets on was within 0.4 MB of its size later: these stay.
- **The textures loaded**: about 600 a session, the main menu's and the interface's loaded again at every one. The masks' array held 4,947 to 4,991 IDs, 620 to 670 with a mask, their buffers 6.2 MB after the first session and 11.6 MB after ten world resets, where their textures needed 1.5 to 2.1 MB.
- **The repair**, four rounds with the census: each world reset let go 0.2 to 4.7 MB of masks, nothing logged about one not let go; `0x950ce` left 3.8 MB over ten generations (9 MB before), and the generations 2.0, 3.7 and 4.7 MB a round where they were 2.2, 4.7 and 5.7.
- **The quarantine**, six rounds: 688,977 blocks the repairs freed, the masks' buffers among them, 111.5 MB, kept; none written to.
- **Twenty rounds, twice**, release builds: every answer right, no crash, handles 1,050 to 1,071; private bytes 3,952 MB after the first round and 4,339 MB after the twentieth, 20.4 MB a round; then 3,938 and 4,335 MB, 20.9 MB (21 and 21.6 MB without the repair).
- **Long and ironman**: France from 1495.11.19 to 1499.8.18 (the questions answered with their first option), an in-game load of England and two years and four months, a return; Castile's ironman save 30 days, the same save loaded again in the game, 10 days, a return; Austria's ironman save of 1724 45 days and a return. Every answer right, no crash; 0.5 to 5.2 MB of masks let go at each reset; private bytes 4,160 MB at the end.
- **The window holders left**: after each return, the holders made before the last reset still live grew by about 300 a round (864 after ten resets): list entries of panels gone, the country diplomacy view's shields (`countrydiplomacyview_shielditem`, 225), the map modes' entries (216, their windows killed since 2026-11-03), policies, institutions, the Empire's golden bull, filters and others. Of their windows only about 50 were alive (`filter_list_item` 18, `golden_bull_entry` 17, the lobby's chat 9, the navy templates 3 and the like); the rest were killed. Not repaired.
- **Not seen in the running game**: a click on a sprite whose texture's mask was let go and made again (the mask is written whole before any use, as before); multiplayer.
- **Cleanup**: each game was closed by ending its process. The three framework files in the game folder were put back and compared equal to the copies taken before. Releasing the game restored the player's files, the ironman saves and the autosaves the runs rewrote among them, and found every file as the backup had it. Scripts and copies of the runs were deleted.
