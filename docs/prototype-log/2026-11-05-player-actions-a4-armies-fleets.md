# 2026-11-05 Player actions A4: armies and fleets (tested by me)

A4 of [player-actions.md](../plans/player-actions.md), a developer build with the command log of this branch, within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset (a Chinese localisation mod among it). Three new games as England at 1444.11.11, driven through remote control only: the first to 1444.12.2 (it ended at the first `siege.options`, below), the second to 1445.11.26, the third only for the leaders' reasons.

- **The first `siege.options` ended the game**, by the framework's own guard: "the framework used 0x1c55480, which no dependency point checks", the spending's vtable the framework puts in the spending it asks the cost of. A dependency point now checks it (the siege view's button that makes the same spending), and the run went on in a new game.
- **Leaders**: `leader.options` gave the general and the conquistador "你需要至少50mil。" with 0 military points (the number was missing until the text was read unformatted, `game_string::unformatted`: the game's lookup without values leaves `$COST$` out), the admiral `can` at 50 dip, the conquistador and the explorer "你需要「探索新世界」国家理念才能招募探险家和征服者。", the conquistador the points as well. `leader.recruit admiral` recruited "安东尼 德 克利福德" (a name the game picked) and took 50 dip; `general` and `explorer` were refused with those reasons. `leader.assign` gave John Talbot (175) to the army in London and the admiral to a fleet, which `leader.list` then showed as their `army`; the general to a fleet was refused by the framework; `leader.dismiss` of a leader leading a unit was refused; `leader.remove` took both off, and `leader.dismiss 4209` then removed the admiral from the list.
- **Mercenaries**: `mercenary.list` listed the companies with their content keys (`merc_routiers`, `merc_gallowglasses`, …), names, regiments, cost (115.056 ducats for 10 regiments) and upkeep, each refused "我们现在承担不起115¤的费用。" with 71 ducats; with 500 more, `mercenary.hire merc_routiers 236` took 115 ducats and the list then said "大路佣兵已经被我们雇佣了！"; in Paris it was refused "英格兰不是巴黎的控制者。"; an unknown key was not_found. In the second game `merc_gallowglasses` hired in Cumbria stood there as an army of 8 regiments within 40 days.
- **Forts**: `fort.list` listed England's six forts with their levels, all `can`; `fort.mothball 246` mothballed Northumberland's (the list then said `mothballed`), `fort.reopen` reopened it; reopening an open fort, mothballing a mothballed one and a province without a fort of the player's were refused by the framework.
- **The army panel**: `army.options` for London's army: split and disband `can`, merge "仅当选中两个单位时才能进行重组。", consolidate "所有的军队都已满员。", drill "你不能在军队没有领袖时进行操练。" (the bullet the game starts each line with is left out since); after a battle in Scotland, consolidate `can`, and `army.consolidate` took the army from 14 regiments to 12; disband in occupied Dumfries "不能在被敌人占领的领土或者临近敌人的军队时解散单位。". `army.split` split 14 regiments into 7 and 7 and answered the new army; `army.merge` of the two made one of 14; `army.disband` removed a split one; `army.drill` with a leader started drilling (options then offered `stop_drill`), `army.stop_drill` stopped it, and each a second time was refused. Fleets in a naval battle: merge "仅当选中的单位都未处于战斗状态时才能进行重组。", disband "此单位在战斗中。"; after it, `army.merge` of two fleets made one of 32 ships and `army.split` two of 16.
- **Fleets moving**: `army.route_to` for a fleet gave `[1270,1271,1272,1274,1275,1276]`; `army.move` set the route, the fleets sailed from London to the Irish Sea (1284) within a month, and `army.get` showed their sea zone and ships. Missions: after a move, `army.options` offered patrol and evade, and `army.mission … patrol` and `protect_trade --target 5` were taken; evade after `army.stop_mission` (which leaves no route) was refused by the game's command without a reason; `blockade` and `hunt_fleets` with the region numbers 0, 5 and 40 were taken by the command. What a mission does once taken was not read back: the framework reads no mission.
- **Transport**: `army.move <army> 87 --transports` (Calais, across the Channel; `army.route_to` found no route there) set the army busy with a route to the coast, and 20 days later it stood in Calais. **Not verified: boarding by `army.move` onto a sea zone with a fleet of the player's, landing from it, armies moving aboard**: the route query found no route from London, nor from Dumfries, to the sea zone where the player's fleet was, and seven transports ordered in Gwynedd were never built (the orders took no money); why was not worked out.
- **Sieges**: England at war with Scotland (France and its allies joined). Besieging Roxburghshire (no fort): the three refused "该地无要塞，没有城墙可轰击。" and "该地无要塞，无可供强攻的目标。"; a province without a siege was refused by the framework. Besieging Dumfries (fort level 2): bombard "你需要数量超过要塞等级的满员炮兵团，……你目前只有0支炮兵团，但轰击此要塞需要2支。", naval bombard "你在相邻的海域没有舰队……" with the cannons' line, assault "你不可以在城墙破裂之前强攻这座要塞。"; with the fleets next to it, naval bombard "需要50mil来进行海上炮击，而你只有40。"; with 200 more, `siege.naval_bombard` took 50 mil and made a breach (bombard and naval bombard then "这个要塞已经出现城墙缺口。", assault `can` at 5 mil), `siege.assault` took 5 mil (then "你正在强攻，不能打出城墙缺口。" and "现在我们已经开始强攻此城了。"), and Dumfries fell to the assault within 10 days. **Not verified: an artillery barrage carried out** (England had no artillery).
- **The battle event**: after a read of `--kinds battle`, two naval battles of England's fleets were heard, such as `{"province":1285,"naval":true,"attackers":["ENG"],"defenders":["ULS"],"winner":"attackers","attacker_losses":0,"defender_losses":2000}` (the losses then in thousandths, 2 ships; divided since), and a land battle in Roxburghshire, England's army of 14 regiments losing to Lorraine, Ulster and Scotland, `attacker_losses` 12,000,000 (12,000 men in thousandths), heard once, after `army_defeated` for the same army. Read with `--countries FRA`, none passed. **Not verified**: a battle the player only sees (none in sight was looked for), the full view's every battle, an extension's `on_battle`, and what the event costs the game (not measured into performance.md).
- **Not tried**: an extension calling any of these; recruiting a conquistador or an explorer the player can recruit; the fort box's own reasons (under siege, not the controller); mothballing fleets; merging units of different provinces.
