# 2026-11-09 The army panel's button area on a frame, and a mod's settings window (tested by me)

W5 of [extension-windows.md](../plans/extension-windows.md), and the regional combat mod's radius editor made an extension window (its own repository, branch `settings-window`). One claim, the test game started hidden three times, the save Austria-37_4-1 (the Holy Roman Empire, 1724) loaded each time, the army in Jabalpur selected by a click.

- **The frame**: the "区域" button stood beside the army panel on a blue frame with a gold edge, a little right of the panel's gold edge. The first try, a frame 8 units above and below nine rows, reached past the panel's button columns at both ends; with eight rows of 46 and the frame from y = 69 to 461 it stood level with them, its edge a few pixels outside the vanilla buttons' visible edges, which lie inside their sprites.
- **Hidden with its buttons**: with a fleet selected the mod hides its button, and the frame went with it; selecting the army again showed both.
- **Clicks on it**: a click on the frame's empty part left the army selected; a click on the map then unselected it.
- **The settings window**: a right click on the button opened it beside the frame, its top level with the frame's: the red ribbon "区域战斗", the left and right arrows around "半径 5", and the game's long Close. The map showed the range at once. A click on the right arrow made it "半径 6" and the range grew; Close closed the window and the map's colors went.
- **Shift and Ctrl**: a click with `input.click --modifiers shift` and then `ctrl` on the right arrow each added 1, not 5 and 10. The input capability's keys reach the game as its SDL events ([research/input.md](../research/input.md)), and the framework reads the keys held with `GetKeyState`, which sees only the real keyboard; the army panel's buttons read them so too. A window's click now takes the keys held as it comes, as the army panel's does, rather than at the next frame. Not verified with a real keyboard; the mod's tests cover its steps of 5 and 10.
- **Logs**: the game's `error.log` has no line of the framework's or the mod's; its `unit4_type` lines were there in earlier runs. The game did not crash.
