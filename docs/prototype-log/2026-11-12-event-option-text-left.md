# 2026-11-12 An event's first option laid out to the left (tested by me)

A player on Harmony 0.1.2 (1.37.5, 2560x1440 at an interface scale of 1, the Chinese language mod for 1.37 and a large Chinese overhaul mod) sent an event window, `EDG_LOT_War_of_Succession.10` of the overhaul, whose first option's text, 一点钱即可。, lay at the button's left edge, its first character under the button's end cap; the other six options were centered, the button itself whole. The option list scrolled. The event had come up in the day, its third time, with two of its nine options gone.

The cause: the drawing thread drew the event's window while the main thread was activating its options, found the first without its texture, and made one itself, laid out to the left; the game's own drawing does that only for a text element it meets without a texture, which in the game never happens ([research](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md), "An event's window, met half made").

- **The text was ruled out first**: 一 (U+4E00) is the overhaul's legacy escape `11 0e 4e`, decoded as it should be; its glyph and every other character's in the two language fonts are of ordinary size; the button text's measuring and drawing passes look at the same formatting codes, at a character's first byte only.
- **Not reproduced from the console**: a hidden test game from master (`9bae5de8`) with `dev-test-games`, the player's playset (but for two mods not on this computer), started as Hungary in 1444. `event EDG_LOT_War_of_Succession.10` fired at 1.5 and 3840x2160, and at 1, with eight options and the list scrolling; and three times in a row as the player had it, answered so that the third showed the player's options. Every option centered, in every screenshot. The console's commands run outside a day.
- **Traced, before**: the same game with `dev-call-trace` on `0x1550e90` and `0x1550d10`, timed, at speed 5 from 1444.11.11 to 1453.7, 40 events shown and answered with their first option. The drawing thread made an option's texture 8 times, each through the element's drawing (`0x1550d10` from `0x15509fb`, format 0), 6 to 15 ms after the main thread began to activate it; each time the activation finished after and wrote its centered texture over the left one, which was not let go. No screenshot showed a text to the left.
- **Fixed**: the map manager showing an event (`0x828c00`) is a crossing. The same game and trace, at speed 5 from 1444.11.11 to 1461.9.12, 60 events: no texture made by the drawing thread; the crossing held back 1 to 4 frames in 51 of the drawing log's ten-second lines, 66 in all; startup with 2419 dependency points, the offline checks passing; the game did not crash.
- **Not verified**: the player's machine, and the order of writes that left the left texture in place, which this computer never produced; other windows whose elements are activated in a day; the leaked texture, now not made, was not measured.
- **Found on the way**: a test game's user folder links `mod\` to the player's own, so a mod put there is the player's. A test mod written into it was taken out again at once. A test mod given with `--mod` showed none of its events in the console (`No event found`); why was not looked into.
- **Cleanup**: the test game released; its logs kept in its claim's `kept\`.
