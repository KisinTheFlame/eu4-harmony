# 2026-11-11 Signals, the framework's events renamed (tested by me)

Part M1 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): the framework's events renamed signals ([ADR framework-notifications-are-signals](../adr/2026-11-11-framework-notifications-are-signals.md)), on `claude/mod-capabilities-gaps-899e75` from `0b57a4b9` with the change not yet committed. The build had the test games' pipes. One test game of this checkout's, started hidden, with the player's playset (the Chinese one) and a mod of my own, `signal-probe`, made by `eu4-harmony new` with an `on_day` that logs the date (`game start --mod`).

- **Checks**: `cargo fmt --check`, the release build, `cargo test --release` for the workspace (the core's 643 passed, 5 ignored), and both clippy runs passed. `cargo xtask offline-check` was not run: the change touches no dependency point.
- **By hand**, a new game as France (`menu start FRA`):
  - `describe` lists `signals` and no `events`; `eu4-harmony game events` is refused with "no capability events".
  - `signals --kinds day` answered `{"last":2,"missed":false,"process":…,"signals":[{"date":"1444.11.11","number":2,"signal":"day"}]}`: the list is `signals` and each item names its kind in `signal`.
  - `time advance 5` first stopped at `question` with `"signals":[]`; the question waiting (`flavor_fra.206`, given by `question.list` as its `event`, the game's event id, which keeps the word) was answered with `question answer`, and `time advance 5` then answered with the five days 1444.11.12 to 1444.11.16 under `signals`.
  - `signals --since 5 --kinds day` gave the days numbered 6 and 7; `--kinds weekday` was refused `invalid_argument`, "no signal is called weekday"; `--since 999999 --wait-ms 3000 --kinds month` answered with none and `missed` true, as a `since` beyond the last one is read.
  - `signal-probe.log` had `on_day 1444.11.11` to `on_day 1444.11.17`, one a day: the handler is heard as before.
  - After `menu return`, `signals --kinds session,session_ended` gave the session (number 1) and `session_ended` with reason `return`.
- **`cargo xtask scenario sweep extensions`**, from 04:25 to 04:32, another checkout's test game running beside it; both passed and both games ended with exit code 1, no crash.
  - sweep: 285 capabilities in 5 places, 3,656 requests, every answer of its shape or a refusal the catalogue has; `signals` answered 9 times and refused `invalid_argument` twice in each place.
  - extensions: `qa-steady` heard 271 dates, its count unbroken through the autosave's load and 10 reloads, its save data got back 11 times; `qa-looping` stopped by the time limit 4 times, then for good.

Not verified: the scenario `perf` with its option now `--perf.signals`, and its log line `signal costs:`, were not run; the other signals (`war_declared`, `battle` and the rest) were heard only as the sweep reads them, not looked at one by one; remote clients outside this repository that read `events` were not looked for.
