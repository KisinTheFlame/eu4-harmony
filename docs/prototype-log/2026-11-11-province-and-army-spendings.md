# 2026-11-11 The province view's, the army panel's, the siege view's and the rebels' spendings (tested by me)

Part M16 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): `province.move_capital`, `move_trade_port`, `establish_siberian_frontier`, `add_tribal_land` and `raze`; `army.scorch_earth`, `seize_colony`, `burn_colony` and `attack_natives`; `siege.sorties`; `stability.harsh_treatment`, and each in its group's `options`, built on `171c9125`. A release build with the test games' pipes, one test game of this checkout's started hidden, with the player's playset. The game's own side is [research/power-spending.md](../research/power-spending.md#the-province-views-and-the-army-panels-spendings).

- **Checks**: `cargo fmt`, the release build, `cargo test --release` (the core's 657 passed, 5 ignored), both clippy runs and `cargo xtask offline-check` passed.
- **Castile**, a new game, 1444.11.11:
  - `province options` in four provinces gave `move_capital` refused "你无法将首都迁移到该省份。 需要至少201adm。" (201 to 236 adm by the province), `move_trade_port` refused "你的贸易本埠已经处于此节点。" at 200 dip, and no reasons for the Siberian frontier, tribal land and razing, whose buttons Castile does not have.
  - After `console run adm 999` and `dip 999`: `move_capital` allowed in every province but 219, refused "这里已经是我们的首都了。"; `move_trade_port` allowed only in 209.
  - `province move_capital 217` and `province move_trade_port 209` answered `null`: administrative points 1041 → 840 (201) and diplomatic 1041 → 841 (200); 217 then refused moving the capital as the capital, 219 allowed it, and 209 refused the trade port as already in its node. `move_capital 217` again was `rejected` with that line.
  - `stability options` gave `raise`, then `harsh_treatment` for faction 113, `noble_rebels`, refused "起义的进度必须至少达到30%。" at 200 mil; `stability harsh_treatment 113` was `rejected` with it, `9999` `not_found`.
  - `army options` for an army at peace: `scorch_earth` refused "你只能在战时实行焦土政策。" at 5 mil; `seize_colony` (25 mil) and `burn_colony` (5) refused with the game's two lines each; `attack_natives` refused "此地没有可以攻击的原住民。" (5 mil). A fleet's options are as before.
  - At war with Granada (declared 1444.12.12): `scorch_earth` allowed; `army scorch_earth` answered `null`, military points 1097 → 1092, the province's devastation 10, and the button then refused "此地已然被毁灭殆尽，故而进一步的焦土政策是没有意义的。". So the army's ID reached the game in `data` and `second`.
- **The Golden Horde**: every province refused `raze` with "只能焚掠你拥有并控制的非核心的省份。". After `console run own 295` (Moscow, orthodox, 6/6/5) `raze` was allowed; `province raze 295` answered `null`, Moscow was 4/4/3, and `raze` was refused "这个省份在1464年11月11日前无法被再次焚掠。". So the country's index in `raze_province` was read as the game's.
- **The Cherokee**: `add_tribal_land` in every province two steps around refused with "…已是…的部落领地。" at 120 adm; no province there was nobody's tribal land.
- **Perm**: the first build passed the Siberian frontier's check its text third and showed no reasons; the check takes it fourth ([research](../research/power-spending.md#the-province-views-and-the-army-panels-spendings)). With that mended, Perm and Muscovy were refused in 1078 and 1080 with "缺乏相关国家理念，无法建立西伯利亚拓荒区。" at 20 dip. Perm's army, marched into 1078 (uncolonized), had `attack_natives` allowed at 30 mil and the colony buttons refused with the game's three lines; `army attack_natives` answered `null`, military points 1135 → 1105, and the button then refused "战斗中无法攻击原住民。", "此地没有可以攻击的原住民。".
- **Granada**, at war with Castile from 1444.12.27: by 1445.1.26 Castile besieged 223. `siege options 223` gave the three buttons refused as the fort's owner's and `sorties` refused "如果敌军数量远多于驻军，驻军将拒绝离开要塞。" at 10 mil; `siege sorties 223` was `rejected` with it. The nobles' faction 115 was refused harsh treatment for its progress (with too few military points the points' line comes alone, as the game's check stops at its first). After `console run add_unrest noble_rebels 223 50` (progress 50%) and 200 mil, `stability harsh_treatment 115` answered `null`: military points 248 → 188 (60), progress 20%, and the button refused for progress again. So the faction reached the game as `rebel_faction={ id type }`.
- `cargo xtask scenario sweep` in the same claim passed: 305 capabilities in 5 places, 3925 requests, each answered with its shape or a refusal the catalogue has. The test game was released.

Not verified: a Siberian frontier started (it needs Russia's ideas or the Great Mongol State's reform, which no 1444 country has), tribal land added (every province near the Cherokee was a tribe's already), a colony seized or burnt (no enemy colony was at hand), sorties carried out (the garrison refused against Castile's army); whether the province view shows the Siberian frontier and tribal land buttons only where the checks hold, and the player's view of the new options for a province the player does not see (they read nothing the province view does not show).
