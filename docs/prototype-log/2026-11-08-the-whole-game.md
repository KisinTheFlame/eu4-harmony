# 2026-11-08 The whole game played through (tested by me)

The scenario set `whole` and its scenarios ([whole-game.md](../whole-game.md)), on the branch of this work, played in test games of my own, each started hidden from the game folder with the player's playset (the Chinese one, regional combat among its Harmony mods) and every DLC. First short runs of each scenario, two of them side by side in two test games, then the whole set with its parameters as the code has them.

## The short runs, and what they found

- **sweep**, the main menu and a game as France, paused: 283 capabilities, 1,440 requests. Every answer held but those of `events`, whose answers were not of the shape the catalogue declares: an event flattened into its number lost every field of its own from the shape, as any internally tagged enum flattened into a struct did. **Fixed** in the shape module and its derive (`shape::object`), with a test; the next sweeps found the events' answers of their shape.
- **sweep**, all five places: it crashed the game in the third, at speed. `menu` had answered `starting` while the game ran, at the frames whose daily update was still under way, so the sweep took itself for out of its place and started a new game from the session while time ran; the game crashed on that game's first day, in the unit manager's queue of units to delete (`unit_manager.cpp:174`, research/main-menu.md, "Leaving while time runs"). **Fixed**, both: `menu` tells such a frame as the game (`Course::place`, with a test; tried: `game` at speed 5, `game` paused), and a return, a new game or an in-game load asked while time runs pauses the game first and leaves the session once it has been still for three game frames (`STILL_FRAMES`). By hand, eight new games asked one after another at speed 5 crashed the build before at the second, in the main menu's frame, executing an economic command of the last session's; with the repair they, three returns and four in-game loads asked at speed 5 all went through.
- **sweep**, all places but the main menu, after the place's fix: one finding, the sweep's own: a value an answer gave (`ally`, a relation's kind) refused as a leader's kind before the place's `no_country`. A capability that refuses an argument before it looks at the place is right; the check now takes `invalid_argument` and `not_found` as answers there too.
- **sessions**, two rounds: passed, every load, in-game load, new game, autosave and its load, observe mode, a country put in view and out, the ironman save, the in-game load from it and every return in this process.
- **extensions**: first failed on the scenario's own reckoning (a date counts hours, not days) and on its words for a stopped extension (the time limit's own error says "stopped"); then passed: `qa-steady` heard every one of the 133 dates of its session, its count unbroken through the autosave's load and four reloads, and got its save data back each of the five times; `qa-failing`'s error was written in full three times a session and counted after, its wrong call written, and it was never stopped; `qa-looping` was stopped by the time limit three times and then for good; `qa-hungry` was refused memory each month and never stopped. The first day of a session is heard at the session's own date, so the day an autosave was written on is heard before it and again after its load; whether that is the game's or the framework's was not looked into.
- **late**, the saves of 1700 and 1820, 120 days each, observed: first failed on the scenario's own read of the session right after the console's `observe`, which begins a session (`session_changed`); the scenario reads it again since. Then passed: 1700 at 436 game days a minute, 1820 at 377, the autosave of each loaded, the game's private bytes 4.1 to 4.3 GB.

## The whole set

`cargo xtask scenario whole` with the parameters as the code has them, from 2026-11-08 20:26 to 2026-11-09 00:15, one claim, the games started hidden; at most one other game ran beside it. Six of the eight scenarios passed; the two that failed did so on their own reckoning, not on the game's, and passed once that was put right (below). The game never crashed or hung.

Put right since: `late` lets a save run no further than a month before the game's end; `checked` counts the differences of the speed-ups `may_differ` names (`army-searches`) and fails only on those of the others. Played again so, `cargo xtask scenario late checked` (2026-11-09 00:17 to 00:35), both passed: the save of 1820.7.1 ran 153 days to 1820.12.1, then its autosave and 30 days more; `checked` found the four speed-ups whose answers must be the game's agreeing in every answer again (`modifier-sums` 104,564,375 of them) and `army-searches` differing in 5,945 of 69,573, 8.5%. The share of `army-searches`' differences early in a game, twice the one its research measured late in a game, is written down here and not looked into; its log line of a difference prints the province and the value alike on both sides, so what differs is not in what it prints.

## Not verified

- Whether the stale entry of the unit manager's queue the first crash found was put there by the last session's day, as the economic command of the second was, or after the world's clear by something else: the repair keeps a session from being left before its last day's commands ran, which covers the first; the second crash's cause was found from its dump, the first's inferred.
- The sweep's acts with a player's arguments act as the player: which of them changed the game as they say was not checked, only that each answered of its shape or with a refusal the catalogue has.
- What only looking shows: the framework UI, the notices the test mods' errors raise, text drawn on the map.
