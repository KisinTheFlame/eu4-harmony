# 2026-11-03 The AI leaving a coalition, and the heaps' free pages (tested by me)

Where the private bytes the census does not count go, why the game's heap was corrupted at the fifth of six rounds in the measuring, and the two repairs that came of it (`core/src/repairs.rs`; research/main-menu.md, "The heap's free pages", and research/countries.md, "A coalition"). Experiment builds, not committed: one that measured the heaps and the private memory at the main menu when a file asked it to, and could have the heaps give back their free pages; one that kept every block the game freed while a repair's callback ran, filled with a pattern, and watched it for writes; one that counted the calls of the coalition's two functions and how they overlapped. Eight processes under the game lock, which backed up and restored the player's files; the framework files in the game folder copied aside first. `eu4.exe` started directly, the playset as installed, release builds, driven by `eu4-harmony game`, rounds as in the acceptance: a load of `法兰西1495_11_19` from the main menu, 30 days at speed 5, an in-game load of `英格兰1444_11_11`, 10 days, a return, a new game as France and a return.

- **Measured**, eight rounds: each round the game's heap, the process heap and the only large one, grew by 11.4 to 12.7 MB of blocks and 20.5 to 25 MB of committed pages; the private bytes outside the heaps went up and down by up to 25 MB with no trend (1,889 MB after the first round, 1,874 MB after the eighth), the regions that came and went being write-combined (`0x404`), 16 MB each. The heap's committed pages beyond its blocks were 229 MB at the first main menu, 503 MB after the eighth round.
- **The heaps giving back their free pages** at the main menu after each round, by the experiment build: 35, 64, 71 and 119 MB, in 10 to 20 ms. At the fifth round the game ended a moment after time began to run in the France just loaded, its heap corrupted (`0xc0000374`). The dump: a worker thread, in the AI (`0x13afb0`, `0x58aa00`), freeing a node of 0x20 bytes it had unlinked from a country's coalition in `0x3b0220`, at an address not aligned to 16 bytes.
- **What the repairs free, kept and watched**, ten rounds: 1,148,611 blocks, 148.5 MB, kept by the end; none was written to after it was freed. No crash.
- **The coalition's functions counted**, three rounds: in each session of the French save `0x3b0220` ran 705 to 837 times, 662 to 756 of them on worker threads, up to four at once, on the coalition against France each time two did (45, 24 and 27 times); `0x3e3940` 23 to 432 times a session, on the main thread only; none in the other sessions.
- **With both repairs**, twenty rounds, twice: every answer right, no crash. Handles 1,046 to 1,069. Private bytes 3,957 MB after the first round and 4,359 MB after the twentieth, about 21 MB a round; then 3,938 and 4,349 MB, 21.6 MB a round (23 and 24.7 MB with the sets of modifiers let go and the pages kept). The heaps gave back 3 to 125 MB at each first frame after a world reset, in at most 39 ms.
- **Long and ironman**, with both repairs: France two years from 1495.11.19 (its questions answered with their first option), an in-game load of England and half a year, a return; Castile's ironman save 30 days, the same save loaded again in the game, 10 days, a return; Austria's ironman save of 1724 30 days and a return. Every answer right, no crash; the heaps gave back 10 to 127 MB each time; private bytes 4,143 MB at the end, where the same run of the build before ended at 4,724 MB.
- **Seen on the way**: after each return the log says the new game asked for before it failed ("the main menu is open"), though it had started and been answered: the menu's follow of a start it already reported; and `time.advance` right after `menu.start` answers at once without a day. Neither looked into.
- **Not seen in the running game**: multiplayer; the player's own in-game load from the menu; how much longer the AI takes when its countries leave a coalition one at a time, which was not measured; the rounds took as long as before (33 to 46 s).
- **Cleanup**: each game was closed by ending its process; the dump was deleted. The three framework files in the game folder were put back and compared equal to the copies taken before. Releasing the game restored the player's files, the ironman saves and the autosaves the runs rewrote among them, and found every file as the backup had it. Scripts and copies of the runs were deleted.
