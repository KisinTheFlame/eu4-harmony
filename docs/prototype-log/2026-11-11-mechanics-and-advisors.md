# 2026-11-11 Government mechanics and advisors (tested by me)

Part M8 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): `government.mechanics`, `government.interact` and the `advisor` group, built on M1, M13 and M14 (`2dbd9fed`). A developer build with the test games' pipes and the command log, its `dev.toml` logging `change_advisor`, `dismiss_advisor`, `promote_advisor` and `government_interaction_command`. One test game of this checkout's, started hidden, with the player's playset (the Chinese one, which has the regional combat mod). The game's own side is [research/government.md](../research/government.md#government-mechanics) and [research/advisors.md](../research/advisors.md).

- **Checks**: `cargo fmt`, the release build, `cargo test --release` (the core's 651 passed, 5 ignored), both clippy runs and `cargo xtask offline-check` passed. The startup report said `1833 dependency points match`, the new ones among them.
- **The AI's commands** (an earlier start of the same build, a game as the Mamluks let run to 1445.4): 47 `government_interaction_command`s, all of the form `government_interaction_command={ … country="ADE" parent=iqta_mechanic index=land_acquisition }`; no `change_advisor`, `promote_advisor` or `dismiss_advisor` in those five months.
- **Mechanics**, a new game as the Mamluks (1444.11.11):
  - `government mechanics` listed `mamluk_mechanic` with its three powers (`mamluk_adm_power` 0.872, `mamluk_dip_power` 0.872, `mamluk_mil_power` 1.04, each 0 to 150, no effects) and its three interactions, each costing 100 `mechanic_power` of one of them, refused with the game's line "需要为「招募切尔克斯人参军」积攒100军事文化进程，而你只有1。" and, for two, a failing condition of their trigger.
  - `government interact mamluk_mechanic recruit_from_culture_lands` was refused `rejected` with those two lines; `… nothing` `not_found` ("no mechanic interaction"), `government interact iqta_mechanic land_acquisition` `not_found` ("no government mechanic").
- **Mechanics**, a new game as Adal: `iqta_mechanic` with no powers and three interactions, all usable. `government interact iqta_mechanic efficient_tax_farming` answered `null`, and the command log had `government_interaction_command={ … country="ADE" parent=iqta_mechanic index=efficient_tax_farming }`. Afterwards all three were refused with "一项伊克塔税收政策的行动每隔20年才能使用一次。你将在1464年11月11日后能够再次使用。" and `ready_on` 1464.11.11, the shared cooldown token at work; `interact … land_acquisition` was refused with the same line.
- **Effects**, a new game as Oman let run to 1444.11.25: `arabic_trade_influence_power` at 0.2 of 100 gave `effects` `placed_merchant_power` 0.03, which the game's formula (research) gives for the entry without conditions (15 × 0.2%); the three entries with conditions gave nothing, their conditions failing.
- **Advisors**, a new game as the Mamluks, treasury 70.381:
  - `advisor list` answered `[]`; `advisor options` twelve advisors, four of each power, each with its kind, culture, religion, skill, salary and hire cost (a skill 1 artist 16 ducats and 1 a month, a skill 2 philosopher 59.2 and 4 a month).
  - `advisor hire 3545` (the philosopher) answered `null`; the log had `change_advisor={ … country="MAM" index=0 advisor={ id=3545 type=51 } }`, the treasury became 11.181 (59.2 paid), and `advisor list` had the philosopher, its `fire` refused with "这个顾问最近刚被我们雇用，此日期前不能被解雇：1444年12月11日" and its `promote` refused with "你无法承担雇佣此顾问的费用。这将消耗你240.00¤。".
  - `advisor options` then had eleven; the administrative ones refused with the framework's "the treasury is 11.181 ducats, and hiring this advisor costs 16" and the seated advisor's firing line. `advisor hire 3545` again was `not_found`.
  - After `console run cash 1000`, `advisor promote 3545` answered `null`: skill 3, salary 9, the treasury 771.181 (240 paid); the log had `promote_advisor={ … members={ dummy="MAM" dummy={ id=3545 type=51 } } }`.
  - `advisor fire 3545` was refused `rejected` with the firing line until 1444.12.11; on 1444.12.26 it answered `null`, the log had `change_advisor={ … country="MAM" index=0 }`, and `advisor list` was `[]`.
- **Names**, a later build with the mechanic named by `ability_<key>`: `mamluk_mechanic` was "马穆鲁克文化", `iqta_mechanic` "伊克塔税收".
- The test game was released.

Not verified: the effects against the government view's own tooltip or the country's modifier sums (the view was not opened; the sums are M4's); a range modifier and a reverse scaled one with their conditions holding (Persia's and the Aztecs' mechanics come with reforms the 1444 countries tried do not have); a mechanic interaction with a power cost carried out (no power was high enough in the games tried; the Adal one costs none); an advisor of a culture the country does not accept refused promotion (`CANT_PROMOTE_ADVISOR_WRONG_CULTURE`); whether the court view's hire list shows exactly the pool the framework lists; `dismiss_advisor`, which is not offered.
