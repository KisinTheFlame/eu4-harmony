# 2026-10-30 Subject types in the country snapshot (tested by me)

Every read went through `eu4-harmony game`, run from the game folder, with this change's build, after the build, the tests and the offline checks against `eu4.exe` passed; `eu4.exe` was started directly, with the playset unchanged. Backups as AGENTS.md says. The diplomacy panels were opened by clicking a province and its diplomacy button, and their tooltips by pointing at a row.

- **Startup**: 570 dependency points match (the three new ones keep the subject type getter, the subject relation's class and its type's key); 104 hooks, 4 writes.
- **The save**: `menu.load Castilla-5` (Castile, 1470.3.13) answered after 7.3 s.
- **Castile**: `subject` BUR, ARA and NAP `personal_union`, GUY, TOU and TLC `vassal`, the personal unions and the vassals its diplomacy page showed on two rows in C2's run of the same save; ARA read `overlord` CAS `personal_union`, GUY `overlord` CAS `vassal`. Poland: MOL `vassal`, LIT `personal_union`; Lithuania `overlord` POL `personal_union`.
- **Every subject relation**: `country.get` on each of the 528 countries that own a province read 84 subject relations: 33 `tributary_state` (14 of them Ming's), 26 `vassal`, 13 `daimyo_vassal` (all the Ashikaga shogunate's), 8 `personal_union`, 3 `appanage` (France's Alençon, Bourbon and Orléans) and 1 `integrated_personal_union` (Austria's Hungary).
- **Against the game**: France's diplomacy panel says "拥有以下封邑：阿朗松, 波旁与奥尔良" on the row of its appanages, and Austria's "拥有以下一体化被联统国：匈牙利"; Bourbon read `overlord` FRA `appanage`.
- **Costs**: 538 reads, every one `ok`, 0.9 to 3.3 ms each in the engine (median 0.9); the core log has no error.
- **Not seen in the running game**: a subject type a mod adds (no mod in the playset adds one); a march, a colony and the other kinds no country had; a relation changing between two reads; a subject whose relation to its overlord cannot be found, which fails the snapshot (unit test only); the extension side, `GameApi::country`'s `subject_type` and the SDK's `Relation::subject_type` (unit tests: the core's writer and the SDK's reader against `abi/json/country.json`, and a core without the member).
- **Cleanup**: the game was closed by ending its process, which left `graceful_exit=no` in `settings.txt`; the save games folder and the framework's folder were replaced by the backup's and the 13 files at the top of the user folder put back, and all compared equal to the backup. No crash folder was made. The framework was uninstalled, which put back the earlier `version.dll`; the backup and the screenshots were deleted.
