# The empires' reforms and decrees

2026-11-11. M19 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): the `empire` and `celestial_empire` groups, tried in a hidden test game on the developer build with test games' pipes, in new games of 1444.11.11 as Austria, Ming, Korea and Austria again. What the game's code says is in [research/empires.md](../research/empires.md).

## What was tried, and what came out

- **The Empire's reforms, as Austria.** `empire.reforms` answered Austria as emperor, authority 20, and 17 reforms, those of the Emperor DLC's set, in the view's three parts (`mainline`, `left_branch`, `right_branch`); the 8 of the set without the DLC were left out. The first, `emperor_reichsreform` (呼吁帝国改革), could not be passed, with the game's reasons: 为了通过改革，诸侯中的支持数必须高于反对数, and 你需要至少50帝国权威，目前只有20; the next ones added that the reform before them is not passed. `revoke` could not: 你还没有通过此项改革.
- **Passing.** Authority raised by 60 with the game console's `run` of a file holding `add_imperial_influence = 60`: authority 80, and the first reform could be passed, its reasons gone (the princes' backing follows the authority). `empire.pass_reform emperor_reichsreform` answered null; the reform read passed, and the authority 0: passing takes all the authority, not the 50 it must be at least, which the cost now says (80 at 80). Passing it again was refused with the game's reasons, its first that it is passed already; `no_such_reform` and `reichsreform` (of the set without the DLC) were `not_found`.
- **Revoking.** At 60 authority `revoke` could, for 50; `empire.revoke_reform` answered null, the reform read not passed and the authority 10. Revoking again was refused: 你还没有通过此项改革.
- **The Celestial Empire's reforms, as Ming.** `celestial_empire.reforms` answered Ming as emperor, mandate 80, and 21 reforms, none in a part of a view (`branch` null), each costing 70 mandate and 1 stability; those whose conditions fail list them as the view's tooltip does (以下条件至少满足一个: …). `pass_reform seaban_decision` answered null: the reform passed, the mandate 10 and stability -1. The next was refused with the game's reasons, 实施此项改革需要至少80.00天命值 and 这一改革决议花费70.00天命，但是你只有10.00天命.
- **Decrees, as Ming.** `celestial_empire.decrees` listed 16 of the 19 decrees, the 3 whose `potential` fails left out, each for 20 meritocracy, none in force. `enact_decree expand_bureaucracy_decree` answered null; it read in force until 1454.11.11, ten years on, as the refusal of the next says: 增置职官已经颁布，将在1454年11月11日到期. The first build read the day it was enacted as the day it ends; fixed (the end is its duration in days after it). `no_decree` was `not_found`.
- **Not the emperor, as Korea.** Every decree was refused 只有皇帝才能颁布圣旨, every celestial reform 必须成为中国皇帝才能实施天朝改革, and `pass_reform seaban_decision` refused so; the Empire's reforms gave 只有皇帝才能在神圣罗马帝国中实施改革 first, and `revoke` the framework's own words, since the game's check does not ask who revokes.
- **The sweep**, `cargo xtask scenario sweep --sweep.only empire,celestial_empire`: passed, 7 capabilities in 5 places, 59 requests.
- The test game did not crash, in three starts.

## Not verified

- A reform of a branch passed, and a branch closed by the other's; a reform refused for war in the Empire, a league war, or the princes' backing alone.
- Passing a reform or enacting a decree from an extension; only remote control asked.
- What the Empire's view itself shows was not looked at on screen; the reasons were compared with the game's localisation, not with the view's tooltip.
- The imperial incidents are not offered: what the view lists of one and how a pick of an option is written were not read.
