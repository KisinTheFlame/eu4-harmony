# 2026-11-05 Player actions follow-ups: subjects, government, and what A2, A3 and A5 only saw refused (tested by me)

Developer builds of this branch with the command log (`subject`, `diplomaticactioncommand`, the parliament and mission commands), within one claim, started hidden; the player's playset, 3840x2160. Three processes: the player's save `阿尔达比勒1462_08_30` loaded (its four mods in the playset) and played as Muscovy, then Hungary, then Muscovy again through the console's `tag`; and two new games as England at 1444.11.11. The console's `cash`, `adm`, `dip`, `mil`, `winwars` and `run` (a file of effects in the user folder, deleted after: `add_trust`, `add_prestige`, `add_base_tax`, `add_spy_network_in`) set the cases up; the game's questions were answered with their first option.

- **Annexing and integrating** (A6): Muscovy's AI was annexing Beloozero; `diplomacy.cancel_annexation BLO` was done and the panel then offered `annex` with the game's reasons (no diplomat, "+190" opinion). Once a diplomat was back, `annex PSK` was done, and `options` gave `cancel_annexation` with "吞并进度0.0%（0/162），预计将在1469年7月完成。" and its monthly 2 dip; the outliner showed "吞并/合并 0.0% 正在吞并". Hungary's AI was integrating Croatia (104/294); `cancel_integration CRO` was done, `integrate` refused for want of a diplomat, then done after twelve days, its progress started again at 0/294 (the game's rule), the outliner's diplomat "0.0% 合并".
- **Subject interactions with a province** (A6): the interactions' methods read the command's `index` as a province (research/subjects.md), and `subject.interact` took a `province` for it. `grant_province CRO --province 1864` gave Croatia the province with its core (the game's report said so), one not Hungary's was refused with no reason; `seize_territory PRM --province 1077` made it Muscovy's, Perm's core kept; `grant_core_claim PRM --province 305` changed the claim on Kazan's province from Muscovy's to Perm's, one of Muscovy's own refused with no reason.
- **The panel's acts with a window's choice** (A6, one of each kind the diplomats allowed): `subsidize_war KAZ --amount 1 --duration 20` done (`warsubsidy={ … duration=20 amount=1.000 }`, then `stop_war_subsidies` offered); `transfer_trade_power`, `threaten_war`, `break_alliance`, `share_maps`, `designate_march`, `steer_trade` refused with "最近已经派遣过外交官…" and "你没有外交官可供派遣。"; `fabricate_claim LIT --province 269` refused with "没有可伪造宣称的接壤省份。". **Found**: `support_rebels LIT`, sent with no faction as the panel made it, answered `done` and supported nothing (the panel still offered it). It now takes the faction's kind: as England with a spy network in Scotland, `support_rebels SCO --rebels anti_tax_rebels` posted `rebel={ id=117 type=50 }` and the panel then said "我们在苏格兰境内支持的叛军当前的叛乱几率为0%。我们的支持将持续到1449年11月。"; a kind Scotland had not was refused by the framework. `share_maps CAS` with no region was `accepted`; what it shared was not looked at.
- **Buildings and states** (A3): `building.build 295 temple` took 100.3 ducats and the outliner showed "建筑 233d 教堂" (to 1463.6.19, as `building.options` said); `building.cancel 295` gave the 100.3 back. `province.make_territory 1079` made Galich a territory at 50 autonomy; Moscow, the capital's state, was refused with "不能取消首都所在的直属州。". The make-state and make-territory reasons carry the button's own line after them ("（点击设置为自治领地）"), as A3 decided.
- **The treasury and technology** (A2): bank loans taken one by one were refused at the 56th, "你无法从银行获得更多贷款。" and "- 你已经借贷了56笔贷款，无法获得超过56笔贷款！"; the second kept the check's `- `, which is now left out. Mercantilism raised 90 times reached 100 and was refused with "我们已经拥有了最高的重商主义倾向". Each technology researched to 32 (points refilled from the console) was then refused "the adm technology is at its last level", while its `technology.options` entry had no reason; it now gives the same.
- **War and peace** (A5): war declared on Kazan with no ally called; Circassia's call to arms read `accepts` false, total -5; after `add_trust` +30 it read true, 25, and `call_to_arms CIR` was `accepted`, Circassia among the attackers. After `winwars`, `peace.evaluate CHG []` read East Chagatai accepting ("被彻底击败" +100), and `peace.demand CHG [gold 20]` was `accepted`: East Chagatai and its three subjects left the war, Kazan and Crimea stayed.
- **Government** (A7, England): `parliament.options` offered ten issues; `debate fund_expansion_of_bureaucracy` began it, every other issue was then refused "a debate is going on", and the parliament view (the first of the three buttons at the lower right) showed "辩论议案：拨款扩大公职体系" and six seats, Kent (234) among them after `add_seat 234`; London, already seated, and Paris were refused with no reason. `remove_seat` was refused by the game's check for London and Kent, during the debate and in a new game without one; the check (`0x2a4110`) asks the parliament, a modifier `0x249`, the seat, `0x9623a0` (a DLC check and the parliament's `+0x61`) and `0xacccf0`, and which failed was not found. `grant_consessions_to_the_church`'s name read "向让步": its text names `$ESTATE_CHURCH$`, which the lookup left out; names now fill such a key in (not seen again, the issue not offered in the second game). After `add_prestige` and 700 base tax in London (the total counted at the month), `raise_rank` made England an empire (rank 3) and the rank was then refused "你的政府等级已达最高！"; the rank's earlier reasons started with "－", the check's `- ` then the text's own dash, which are now both left out. `eng_recruit_the_mercenaries` read only "陆军数量至少为上限的80.0%" once the three hired companies arrived (`mercenary.hire` takes days: the outliner showed "征募部队 6d"), then `can`; `mission.complete` completed it and a second was refused "the mission is completed". The estates view showed the clergy 50% 22%, nobles 50% 34%, burghers 50% 27%, land 25%, 19%, 25%: `estate.list` gave 22.613, 34.772, 27.614 and land 25.227, 19.545, 25.228, the view rounding down. No reform was offered free (every change cost 50 or more); the framework posts every change paying.
