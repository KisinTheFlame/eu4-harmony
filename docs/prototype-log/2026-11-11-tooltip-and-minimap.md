# 2026-11-11 The tooltip at the cursor, the minimap deferred (tested by me)

What the owner found in [2026-11-11-mesh-objects-shown.md](2026-11-11-mesh-objects-shown.md): a province's tooltip behind the map as it is dragged, and the minimap holding frames back. The drawing thread now keeps the tooltip at the cursor between updates, and the minimap's drawing is a deferred call ([research/drawing-beside-the-day.md](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md), "Frames late at speed 5"). Hidden test games from this branch's core with `dev-test-games`, the 1780.7.1 save in observe mode, at speed 5 (`time.advance`); the cursor moved by `input.move`, the camera by `camera.look_at`, where said; the player's playset and settings, 3840×2160 borderless, a display of 144 Hz.

- **The tooltip, checked against the game**, a check built in for these runs and not kept: the cursor moved 40 pixels at a time; at each of nine cursors the place the drawing thread worked out, before the game's own next placing for the same cursor, was the game's exactly, 52 compared. A first build read the place through the stub's own `rbp`, not the game's (`call_rust!` keeps the game's at `[rbp]`, as `stub.rs` says), and moved the tooltip from a wrong place; seen in the same check, before anything was looked at in a game shown.
- **The minimap deferred**, two game years with the camera and the cursor moved: 0 to 2 frames held back in ten seconds, by the monthly refresh and a port's model, none by the minimap; 29 to 64 late, 3 to 17 waiting for the lock, 15 to 60 neither, the longest gap 80 to 297 ms. No crash, no error in the log.
- **The days' pace**: one game ran four game years at 155 ms a day and, loaded again in it, a year in 40 s; a profile of it had the main thread asleep in the frames' pacing (`pace`) 38% of the time. The build before these changes, in a new game, ran the year in 17 to 18 s; without the minimap deferred, 17 s; this build in new games, 17 s, then 18 s after two hundred days with the camera and the cursor moved, then 17 and 21 s, the process's memory from 4,242 to 4,267 MB over two years. What slowed that one game was not found, and it was not seen again.
- **Not verified**: a game shown, the map dragged with the cursor over provinces; tooltips over windows and buttons, which go the same way but were not looked at; the tooltip near the screen's edges, where the place kept on the screen is moved from rather than worked out again from the cursor; the minimap in a game shown.
- **Cleanup**: every test game released, their logs kept in their claims' `kept\`; the checks taken out of the code; the build before the changes, in a worktree of its own, removed.
