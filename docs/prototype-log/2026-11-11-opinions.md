# 2026-11-11 What countries think of each other (tested by me)

Part M5 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): `country.opinion` and `country.opinions`, on a branch of `claude/mod-capabilities-gaps-899e75` from `1557dbbf` with the change not yet committed. The build had the test games' pipes. One test game of my own, started hidden with the player's playset (the Chinese one), loading `observer-1700.1.1` (the Ottomans played, 1700.1.1). The game's side is [research/diplomacy.md](../research/diplomacy.md#opinion).

- **Checks**: `cargo fmt`, the release build, both clippy runs and `cargo xtask offline-check` passed. `cargo test --release` passed but for xtask's `a_return_waits_for_the_main_menu_across_the_restart`, a named pipe's timing that this change does not touch: run alone six times it failed three times and passed three.
- **`cargo xtask scenario sweep`**, in this claim from 07:28 to 07:35, one other test game running beside it: passed, 288 capabilities in 5 places, 3,692 requests, every answer of its shape or a refusal the catalogue has. `country.opinion` answered twice and refused `invalid_argument` once in each place with a game (`not_in_game` three times in the main menu); `country.opinions` answered twice in each, observing too. The game did not crash; every `eu4.exe` of the claim ended with exit code 1.
- **Whose the record's trust and favors are**: Aq Qoyunlu's opinion of the Ottomans read trust 100 and favors 36.445; the Ottomans' `diplomacy.options AKK` offered every trade of favors (each weighing "使用人情：+50", "白羊对奥斯曼的好感度：176"), and sharing maps weighed "信任：+50". So country X's record of Y holds X's trust in Y and the favors X owes Y, the other way round from what the framework's comment on the fields said, which this change corrects.
- **The opinion against the game's own words**: `country.opinion AKK TUR` read 176, as the alliance's weighing above says it.
- **The parts**, as the tooltip writes them in Chinese:
  - The Ottomans' of Aq Qoyunlu: twelve, among them "同信仰国家的好感度" +10, "同盟" +50, "改善关系" +82 with -3.0 a year, "解放我们的省份" +15 with -1.0 a year, "边境摩擦" -4. They sum to 233 against the 195 shown: the shown number is the one the game last counted, clamped to 200.
  - Aq Qoyunlu's of the Ottomans: fourteen, among them "侵略扩张" -11 with +2.4 a year and "觊觎领土" -21, under which the tooltip writes on a line of its own "(他们想要 君士坦丁堡, 科贾埃利, 科尼亚, 安卡拉, 锡瓦斯, 开塞利和苏丹诺努)", kept in that part's text.
  - The first two builds refused these: the first read a part's value as the whole rest of the line, and "改善关系: +91 (-3.0 每年)" is no number; the second failed on the line with no value under a part. The parts are now read as the research says.
- **Aggressive expansion**: Aq Qoyunlu's, Sibir's and Songhai's of the Ottomans read 11.192 each, and each one's parts had "侵略扩张" -11; the Commonwealth's and Austria's read 0, with no such part among their parts (their opinions -200). So many countries holding the same 11.192 against the Ottomans is the game's, not a misread: the first build found the modifiers by the index asked for, the second by the record's own other tag, as the writer in saves does, and both read the same numbers, the two being the same index.
- **`country.opinions TUR`**: 143 countries, every one that owns a province but the Ottomans, in under 0.1 s for the request.
- **The views**: as the player, the Commonwealth's opinion of Austria read -181 with its thirteen parts and null trust and favors; as the game, trust 30 and favors 0. `country.opinions FRA` as the player gave null trust and favors for every country, as the game 50 and 0 for Sweden and Norway.
- **Refusals**: `country.opinion TUR TUR` was refused `invalid_argument`, "TUR's opinion of itself: of and toward are one country"; `QQQ` `not_found`, "no country QQQ", in both capabilities. `ZZZ` is a country of the game's (its opinion of the Ottomans 25), so the fake game's tests keep it as the tag of none.
- **A country that owns nothing**: Byzantium's opinion of the Ottomans read -39, with "历史宿敌" -25: the game counts it for a country gone too, and `country.opinions` leaves such countries out.

Not verified: the diplomacy view was not opened on screen. What the player's view shows of two other countries rests on the opinion map mode, which shows any country's opinion of the one selected with its tooltip, and was not looked at in this run; that the panels show trust and favors only between the player and another is likewise not compared on screen. The yearly change of a part was read only in Chinese, `(-3.0 每年)`; another language's brackets were not seen. Opinion modifiers other than aggressive expansion were read only through the parts.
