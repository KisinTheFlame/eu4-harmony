# 2026-11-05 Player actions A3: province administration and buildings (tested by me)

A3 of [player-actions.md](../player-actions.md), a developer build of this branch with the command log (the A3 commands' types only), within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset. Three processes, each a new game as Castile at 1444.11.11, driven through remote control; the second played to 1449.8.1, the game's questions answered with their first option. The console's `cash`, `adm`, `dip` and `own 223` gave the player money, points and Granada where an action needed them. What was found in the game's code is in [research/province-view.md](../research/province-view.md).

- **`province.options`** gave every action of the province view for Madrid (217), Pontevedra (4555) and Granada (223, Granada's, then the player's), each with the game's reasons in the player language as the buttons' tooltips write them, and the prices: developing 54 points, then 55; coring 70 to 150 adm; changing culture 37 to 140 dip; 30 edicts, those of other ages or nations refused with their conditions. A foreign province got the game's own refusals for every action ("这个省份不属于你或你属国…", "只能将属于我们的省份转换为核心省份。", the four reasons for a culture change).
- **Developing**: `develop_manpower 217` raised Madrid's base manpower from 5 to 6 and the next was refused, "你需要55 军事点数才能在马德里执行提高基础人力。"; with points, `develop_tax` and `develop_production` raised both to 6. The first try was refused by the command layer: the power spending's `second` does not come back from the game's reader; without it, the form is the one the AI's commands have.
- **Exploiting**: `exploit_tax 4555` took Pontevedra's base tax from 3 to 2 and gave 12.7 ducats; then every exploit there was refused until 1464.11.11 with the game's text. `exploit_production` in Madrid, which has no port, was refused with "这里没有水手。".
- **Autonomy**: `raise_autonomy 4555` was done, then both buttons were refused until 1474.11.11. `lower_autonomy` was refused below 10% and, in Granada while it was being cored, with "由于自治度下限为90.00%…"; once Granada was a core (1448), `lower_autonomy 223` was done and refused again until 1478.10.25.
- **States**: `make_territory 4555` made Galicia a territory and `make_state 4555` a state again, each refused the other way round with the game's two lines (the reason and the button's own hint, "（点击设置为直属州）"). `make_territory 217` made Southern Castile a territory, and `make_state 217` a state again.
- **Edicts**: `set_edict 217 edict_advancement_effort` set it, and changing or cancelling it was refused for a year, "1445.11.11之前促进进步不可以被改变或者移除。"; `edict_of_absolutism` was refused with its two conditions; an unknown key was `invalid_argument`. In 1448.10 `cancel_edict 4555` cancelled Galicia's edict, after which it was refused with "当地没有法令可供废除！".
- **Coring**: `core 223` spent 70 adm and started coring Granada, until 1446.5.11; `options` then offered `cancel_core`, which stopped it, and `core` again started it.
- **Culture**: `change_culture 4555` with too few points was refused with "你需要80dip以转换该省份的文化，但你目前仅有42。" (the culture button's own check, which the game's culture check leaves out); with points, it started converting Pontevedra to Castilian, until 1450.9.11, and `cancel_culture 4555` stopped it. `change_culture 217`, Castilian already, was refused by the framework.
- **Buildings**: `building.options 217` listed every building with its price and the game's reasons ("集市需要外交科技达到4级。", "这个工场与马德里出产的产品不适配。"); `build 217 fort_15th` started a fort, to be finished 1447.3.11, after which every build there was refused with "城堡已正在马德里建造"; `cancel 217` cancelled it; `destroy 219 fort_15th` destroyed the fort of province 219, the only building of Castile's at the start; a building that does not stand there, and another country's province, were refused by the framework.
- **The building finished event**: read by remote control with `--kinds building_finished`, four buildings of other countries were heard by 1448.10 (natives' fields, a temple), and with `--countries CAS` Madrid's fort, built again in 1446.10, at 1449.2.1. The first try ended the game through the framework's own fatal error: the observer used the construction's class, which no dependency point checked; a point now keeps the constructor's instruction that sets it.
- **Not tried**: an extension's `on_building_finished` (only remote control read the event); cancelling a building another country is building in the player's province; destroying under siege; making the capital's area a territory (the command's check refuses it, the tooltip does not); a refusal by a command's own check after the framework's checks passed; the player's view against the full view, which these reads do not differ in.
- **Cleanup**: the release put the player's files back (every file as the backup had it) and the game folder's framework files.
