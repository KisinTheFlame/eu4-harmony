# 2026-11-05 Player actions A5: war and peace (tested by me)

A5 of [player-actions.md](../plans/player-actions.md), a player build of this branch, within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset. Two processes, each a new game as France at 1444.11.11, driven through remote control only; the war set up with `diplomacy.declare_war` and the game console's `winwars`, `control`, `favors`.

- **Events**: after a read of `--kinds war_declared,peace_made,province_owner_changed`, the days to 1445.2.12 gave `war_declared` for Timurids on Qom ("阿贾姆叛乱") and Sirhind on Delhi, and `province_owner_changed` 59 from Saxony to Thuringia. France's declaration on England gave `war_declared` FRA on ENG "法兰西-英格兰战争"; read with `--countries FRA` it alone came, with `--countries ENG` the declaration, the peace and the province. The peace with England gave `peace_made` with the attackers France and its five appanages and the defenders England, Leinster, Ulster and Portugal, `ended` true, then `province_owner_changed` 174 ENG to FRA; the offered peace with Brittany `peace_made` and 4111 FRA to BRI. Extensions' handlers were not tried.
- **`peace.terms ENG`** listed 67 terms: 40 `demand_provinces` (Calais 87 at 11, Bordeaux 174 at 18, ...), `return_cores` to Armagnac, Provence and Scotland, `release_annexed` (Gascony 28, Normandy 33, ...), `annul_treaties` with Leinster, Ulster and Portugal, `end_rivalry`, `war_reparations` 10, `concede_defeat` 10, `steer_trade` 60, and `gold` with at most 860 ducats; `become_vassal` cost 357 and was refused "不能提出超过100%战争分数价值的条件", `pillage_capital_state` "伦敦未被法兰西或它的一个属国占领。" with the term's description as its second line, as the tooltip writes it. Names of a few terms keep the game's `@ENG` flag codes ("@ENG法兰西的附庸"). With `--offer` against Brittany: Brittany's demands of France, most refused "不能选择这个省份，因为布列塔尼无法将其核心化。". Against Castile, not at war: `rejected` "the player is not at war with CAS". Not compared with the peace view on screen.
- **`peace.evaluate`**: Calais alone cost 12 (11 in the list) and England would not accept: −1000 "在未占领与加莱陆路相连的要塞的情况下索取该省份：", −1000 "至少需要10点战争分数来提出要求：", −32 "要求超出战争分数：" among seven factors, total −2093; while the diplomat was away, `can` false with "最近已经派遣过外交官，所以直到1445年3月13日我们才能再次派遣。". Refusals: `concede_defeat` with another term "要求承认战败时，不能附带其他任何条件。"; `humiliate`, not offered, `invalid_argument`; a term twice `invalid_argument`; 2000 ducats "from 0 to 860.0 ducats". After `winwars`: Bordeaux, war reparations and annulling the treaty with Portugal cost 38, accepted, the one factor "被彻底击败：" +100.
- **Gold**: in the first process gold had no effect (cost 0, the answer unchanged): the game's setter found no chosen gold term. With the fix (the term's effect taken off and put on again around the amount) 100, 300 and 860 ducats cost 3, 9 and 25. Demanding 300 ducats and Calais was accepted; France's treasury went from 74.1 to 302.8, 228.7 more, not 300: why was not found.
- **`peace.demand`, `peace.offer`**: the demand to England `accepted` and the war ended; Bordeaux was France's. Offering Saintonge (4111) to Brittany, refused for the diplomat first, then `accepted` a month later.
- **`diplomacy.call_to_arms`**: Provence, France's ally, before any war: `rejected` "the diplomacy panel offers no call to arms to PRO"; in the war on England `rejected` "它们不会接受。" (the panel's check, the AI would decline), the same after the console's `favors PRO 50`; in the second process, at war with Brittany, the call was sent and Provence `declined`. Burgundy, no ally: no call offered. Scotland, allied the same day, joined Brittany's side as Brittany's ally and was offered no call. No ally that accepted was seen.
- **`occupation`**: Calais, England's, controlled by England: `can` false "只有控制者能将省份控制权转移给其他国家。"; after `control 87 FRA`, the allies Orléans, Auvergne, Foix, Bourbon and Armagnac (France's appanages; Provence was not in the war); to Provence `rejected` "the transfer occupation window does not list PRO to take province 87"; to Orléans `{"controller": "ORL"}`, and then, Orléans holding it, France could still hand it on (`FRA` among the allies listed) and gave it to Bourbon. Not compared with the window on screen.
- **Not tried**: an ally accepting a call to arms; a scripted peace treaty term, a colonial region and the terms France was not offered (`humiliate`, `revoke_reform`, religion and government); a peace offer to an ally of the war's leader (a separate peace); extensions' calls and handlers; the peace view on screen.
