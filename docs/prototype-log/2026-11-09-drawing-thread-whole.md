# 2026-11-09 The drawing thread through the whole scenario set (tested by me)

The `whole` scenario set ([whole-game.md](../plans/whole-game.md)) played on the drawing thread's branch as it went ([drawing-thread.md](../plans/drawing-thread.md)), the drawing thread a fixed part of every build and no fallback, six times from 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11, and `long` whole once; what each crash was is in [research/drawing-beside-the-day.md](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md), "What the whole scenario set found". Every game a hidden test game of this checkout, on the player's playset and settings, 3840×2160 borderless.

- **First, 18:09, one scenario at a time**: all eight crashed, on the drawing thread: six in the drawing's tail after Present (`0x81aaa3`), at a return to the main menu or a start (the idler switched under the frame), two at `0x1540f20` in long play (a mesh object let go while drawn). Fixed: the idler's switch lets the drawing thread end its frame first; the mesh object's destructor is a crossing.
- **Second, from 19:06, several at a time**: six passed; `long` and `checked` crashed at `0x1540f20` again, a mesh object let go with the lock held, after the frame was prepared, in the safe frame. Fixed: a crossing anywhere holds the prepared frame back until the next update.
- **Third, from 19:46**: `sweep` crashed on the drawing thread in the renderer (`0x16d4fcf`, its vertex declaration forgotten by a scene the main thread began); the run was stopped there. Fixed: the renderer's BeginScene is a crossing on the main thread. `sweep` alone afterwards (23:20) passed.
- **Fourth, from 23:25**: `sweep`, `late`, `checked` and `growth` ended without a crash; `long` slowed to more than ten minutes a game year and was stopped as hung, with the run. The cause was not the drawing thread: Windows throttled the hidden games ([A hidden game at full speed](2026-11-10-hidden-start-at-full-speed.md)).
- **Fifth, 2026-11-10 from 01:09**, four at a time, `long` in seven stretches ([The long game in stretches](2026-11-10-whole-in-stretches.md)), power throttling off: 13 of 14 passed and no game crashed, in 2 hours 19 minutes. `long-1600` failed a check of its own: the autosave it wrote after 20 years, in observe mode, loaded as Sweden's, not observing; its save `observer-1600.1.1` begins observing, unlike `observer-1700.1.1`, which the stretch put in observe mode itself and whose autosave loaded observing; not looked into further. From 02:46 the games' main threads were unpinned from outside ([Hidden games' main threads unpinned](2026-11-10-hidden-main-thread-unpinned.md)), which shortened the run.
- **Sixth, 2026-11-11 from 05:28**, three at a time, on master's `5a0a0a8a` with the drawing thread's work up to the late frames counted by part: 13 of 14 passed and no game crashed, in 2 hours 20 minutes, beside two other sessions' test games. `long-1600` failed as in the fifth. The cause is the game's: it puts Sweden in view as it loads `observer-1600.1.1` (as in [An observe-mode game with no country in view](2026-11-07-observe-mode-no-country.md)), and a save made with a country in view names that country its player. The stretches now put the game in observe mode with no country in view as they begin and after each autosave they load. `long-1600` again afterwards, beside `long`: passed, its autosaves of 1620, 1640, 1660 and 1680 each loaded observing.
- **`long`, 2026-11-11 from 07:51**, the whole game in one process, beside `long-1600` and two other sessions' test games: passed, no crash, in 3 hours 17 minutes; the autosaves of 1494 to 1794, every 50 years, loaded in 9 to 14 s; the slowest year 1485 at 237.5 days a minute; private bytes 4886 MB at 1820.11.11.
- **Not verified**: a game shown and played by a person, which the scenarios do not do (two faults it found, the camera's cursor and the game quitting, are in [The drawing thread shown](2026-11-10-drawing-thread-shown.md)); other machines and graphics cards; the sets after the sixth.
