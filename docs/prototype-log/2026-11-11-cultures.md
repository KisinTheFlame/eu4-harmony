# 2026-11-11 Cultures: the primary culture, promoting and demoting (tested by me)

Part M17 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): the `culture` group, read and pressed in a test game. The research is [power-spending.md](../research/power-spending.md), "Cultures".

## Setup

- Branch `worktree-agent-a12472721e9116adf`, on `69e255bd` of `claude/mod-capabilities-gaps-899e75`, built with `dev-test-games`; a test game of its own, started hidden, the player's playset as the claim copied it.
- A new game as Castile, 1444.11.11, then the console's `tag NAV` in the same session; requests through `eu4-harmony game --test-game`, as the player.

## What came out

- **The read**, Castile at the start: primary `castillian`, promoted `andalucian` and `leonese`, slots 2 of 2; listed `castillian` (116 development), `andalucian` (79), `leonese` (52), `galician` (28), `basque` (6), each with the game's own name (卡斯蒂利亚, 安达卢西亚, …). `andalucian` and `leonese` had set_primary and demote (10 diplomatic points, allowed); `galician` and `basque` set_primary and promote (100 points).
- **The reasons** are the game's tooltips: set_primary "安达卢西亚文化在国内的地区至少要占有全国50%的发展度才有资格成为主流文化，但它只占28.10%。" and "你需要100外交点数以改变主流文化，但你只有42点！"; promote `galician` "由于我们已认同了2种文化，所以不能再认同更多文化。…最多可认同2种文化。"; promote `basque` "…需要至少20发展度…但它只有6发展度。". The first build also listed set_primary's price line ("改变主流文化为安达卢西亚将花费100外交点数。") among the reasons; the check writes it whether it allows or not, and the build after leaves it out, which the same read in a game started again showed.
- **Demote**: `culture.demote andalucian` answered null; a day later `andalucian` was no longer accepted, offered promote instead, the slots read 1 of 2, and the diplomatic points went from 42 to 32.
- **Promote**: after the console's `dip 500`, `culture.promote galician` answered null; `accepted` read `leonese`, `galician`, slots 2 of 2, and the points went from 532 to 432.
- **Refusals**: promote `basque` and promote `andalucian` (slots full) `rejected` with the game's lines above; demote `castillian` and set_primary `castillian` "castillian is the player's primary culture"; promote `leonese` "the player accepts leonese already"; demote `basque` "the player does not accept basque"; promote `nosuch` `not_found`.
- **Set primary**: Castile's six Leonese provinces (207, 208, 216, 1747, 4553, 4788) given to Navarra with the console's `own` and `add_core`, `tag NAV`, `dip 500`. Navarra's read showed `leonese` at 52 development but refused at "只占0.00%" a month later, cores or not; once both areas were made states (`state 207`, `state 1747`) the next month allowed both set_primary and promote, with no reasons. `culture.set_primary leonese` answered null: the primary read `leonese`, the points went from 572 to 472, and `basque`, the old primary, became accepted, taking one slot of 2.
- **The sweep** (`scenario sweep --sweep.only culture`, as France, in the claim): passed, 4 capabilities in 5 places, 44 requests, each answered with its shape or a refusal the catalogue has (`not_in_game` at the main menu, `no_country` observing, otherwise `ok`, `rejected` or `not_found`). The game did not crash in any of the run's three starts.

## Not verified

- `CHANGE_CULTURE_CANNOT_GOV_REFORMS` and `SET_PRIMARY_CULTURE_WRONG_TYPE`, and that a culture's share counts only provinces in states, which the run suggests but the code was not read for.
- An extension's calls; the fake game answers the group as any other, by `mod:answer`.
