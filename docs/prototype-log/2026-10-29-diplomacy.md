# 2026-10-29 Diplomacy (remote control C8, tested by me)

`eu4-harmony game diplomacy ...` built from this branch at its third commit (the capability layer, the engine and the ABI, before the peace offer's own "not at war" refusal), and for the extension side a throwaway probe made with `eu4-harmony new` in a scratch folder, linked and added to `dlc_load.json` for the run: on its first `on_day` it listed the war goals against the owner of Serbia (141), proposed an alliance to the owner of Marash (332), offered that country peace and declared war on the first, then asked `diplomacy_answer` each frame until it was no longer pending. `eu4.exe` was started directly with `-start_tag=TUR` (a new game as the Ottomans, normal mode, 1444.11.11), the provinces in its environment; Chinese playset with the unchanged regional-combat extension, system fonts. Backups as AGENTS.md says, `dlc_load.json` too. Time was run on with the space bar and the speed keys (`tools/game-input.ps1`), C1 not being merged.

- **Startup**: 475 dependency points match; 103 hooks (the new one observes the game carrying out a diplomatic action's command), 4 writes; both extensions loaded.
- **War goals**: against Byzantium, Karaman, Serbia, Athens, Wallachia, Dulkadir and Candar only `{"casus_belli": "none", "war_goal": "nowargoal", "name": "无战争目标"}`; against Albania `cb_core`/`take_core` for Lezhe (4175, "收回核心省份莱什") and Kruje (4750, "收回核心省份克鲁耶"), then `none`. About 1 ms each in the engine. Not compared with the declare war window on screen.
- **Alliances**: Byzantium refused at once, `rejected` with the game's two reasons, "除非我国对拜占庭有正面看法（当前为-45），否则无法与其结盟。" and "拜占庭不喜欢我国，无法提议结盟。他们对我们必须有正面的看法，当前为-95。", the description the check writes after them left out; Karaman `{"answer": "declined"}` after 89 ms; a second offer the same day, to Dulkadir, `rejected`: "你必须等待一天才可以提出另一个结盟提议。". On the probe's day the probe's offer to Dulkadir came back `Accepted` 3 frames after it was sent, and `country.get TUR` then had `ally DUL`.
- **Declaring war** on Albania for Kruje: `rejected` "在1444年12月11日之前不能宣战。" on the first day (the game's first month), and the same from the probe for Serbia; on 1444.12.13 `{"answer": "declared", "war": "奥斯曼收复克鲁耶之战"}` after 43 ms, and `country.get TUR` then had that war, the Ottomans attacking, Albania, Venice and Naxos defending. Declaring again: `rejected` with three reasons, the diplomat's wait until 1445.1.14, "他们正与我们交战。" and the month after the last war began.
- **Peace**: offered to Byzantium, not at war, `rejected` "the game does not allow it, and gives no reason" (the build after the run says the player is not at war with them instead); to Albania during the diplomat's wait, `rejected` with it; to Venice, a separate peace, `declined` (91 ms); a second offer the same day, to Naxos, `rejected` "你必须等待一天才可以提出另一个和平提议。" and "附庸国或被联合统治的国家无法签订和平条约。"; to Albania on 1445.1.17 `declined`. After the game console's `winwars` and one more day, Venice `accepted` (136 ms), and the war's defenders were Albania alone.
- **The probe** also read `NotFound` "no diplomatic action 9999 is kept" for a ticket never given, and the peace offer to a country it was not at war with refused as above; its `on_day` took 63 ms, the two province lists most of it.
- **Costs**: 1 to 1.5 ms in the engine for a check or a refusal; the answers came 43 to 136 ms after the request ran, the game paused or running at speed 5. The core log has no error, and no extension was stopped.
- **Not seen in the running game**: an extension's declaration that began a war (the probe's was refused for the first month); a `pending` answer, which needs a human player; a declaration the game carried out without a war beginning; Dulkadir, the Ottomans' ally, called into the war on Albania (it did not join, and whether it was called was not established); the declare war window compared with `diplomacy.war_goals`; a war goal about a country (`target`); the peace offer's "not at war" refusal, added after the run.
- **Cleanup**: the run rotated the autosaves and changed `settings.txt` and `continue_game.json`; the save folder, those two and `dlc_load.json` were put back from the backups, and both save folders and the four settings files compared equal to them. No crash folder was made. The game was closed by ending its process. The probe's link file and log were deleted; the framework was uninstalled, which put back the earlier `version.dll` (its hash as before the run); the backups, the probe and the screenshot were deleted.
- **Rebased onto C1 and C6, not run in the game**: the branch was then rebased onto the merges of C1 and C6, which put diplomacy's five fields in `GameApi` behind time control's four and `recruit`, where the run's build had them right after `country`, and gave the session's objects to both (the same file); that build passed the build, the tests and the offline checks against `eu4.exe`, and was never started in the game. The SDK's tests check the fields' places against a table built as the core builds its own.
