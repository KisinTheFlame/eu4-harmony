# 2026-11-11 Mesh objects let go, and every deferred call, at the frame's preparation (tested by me)

What still held the drawing thread back at speed 5 after the tooltips moved ([2026-11-11-tooltips-shown.md](2026-11-11-tooltips-shown.md)), and what keeping the mesh objects the main thread frees, with the other deferred calls, to the next frame's preparation gives ([research/drawing-beside-the-day.md](../research/drawing-beside-the-day.md), "Frames late at speed 5"). Hidden test games from this branch's core with `dev-test-games`, the 1780.7.1 save in observe mode, the camera on Constantinople, at speed 5 (`time.advance`), the camera moved 8 map pixels every 100 ms by `camera.look_at` where said; the player's playset and settings, 3840×2160 borderless, a display of 144 Hz.

- **The stacks**, a count built in for one run and not kept, two game years with the camera moved: the mesh objects let go that held frames back came from the commands' step before a day, with the lock held; the minimap's crossings came from the deferred daily refresh, 644 of 668 at the day's end, where they held nothing back.
- **Before** (the tooltips moved, the camera moved): 114 to 131 frames a second, 20 to 41 late in ten seconds, 4 to 24 held back (mostly by mesh objects let go), 4 to 7 waiting for the lock, the longest gap 158 to 211 ms.
- **After**, two game years with the camera moved: 115 to 123 frames a second, 42 to 51 late, 1 held back, 13 to 15 waiting for the lock, 26 to 37 neither, the longest gap 146 to 191 ms; 4,454 to 5,565 calls deferred in ten seconds. Four game years more without the camera moved, in 66.5 s: 0 to 28 late, 0 to 2 held back, the longest gap 85 to 238 ms where days ran. No crash, no error in the log; a screenshot near Constantinople showed the units, the fleets and the cities.
- **So**: no day is held back whole any more, but the frames late did not fall with the camera moved: the drawing thread now draws through the days and waits for the lock at their ends instead, and the longest gaps, about one in ten seconds, are something else.
- **Not verified**: a game shown, the map dragged; a game played rather than observed; how long the days take against before (not measured side by side); a mesh's resource gone before the frame's preparation, which this would not survive.
- **Cleanup**: every test game released, their logs kept in their claims' `kept\`; the stack count taken out of the code.
