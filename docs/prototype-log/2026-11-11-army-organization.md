# 2026-11-11 Organizing armies and fleets: detaching a kind, attaching, transports, special units, automatic transportation (tested by me)

Part M15 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md), on a branch of `claude/mod-capabilities-gaps-899e75` at `e5864abb` (M1, M6, M7, M9, M13, M14), with the change not yet committed. The build had the test games' pipes and the command log. One test game of this worktree's, started hidden twice (the second time with the automatic transportation's check asked in both states), with the player's playset; the late game test save `observer-1700.1.1` copied into its save folder and loaded as the Ottomans, the game paused. Every request went through `eu4-harmony game` as the player.

- **Checks**: `cargo fmt`, the release build, the core's tests and `cargo xtask offline-check` passed before the run.

## What was tried

- **`army.options`** of a two-regiment army in province 4779 (one infantry, three artillery after a first detach) and of a fleet in 4699: `detach_kind` once for each kind the unit had (infantry and artillery; heavy_ship, galley and transport), `attach` with `targets` (the other army in 4779; the two other fleets in 4699), the army's `attach_to_transport` refused "港口中无友方运输船" (no friendly transports in the port) and `detach_special_units` refused "军队之中没有特殊单位可供分离。" (DETMER_NONE), and the fleet's `stop_auto_transport`, the AI having turned it on.
- **`army.detach_kind`**: artillery from the army answered the new army, three artillery regiments, the old one keeping its one infantry; cavalry, which it had none of, was refused "没有这种类型的单位可供分离。"; its last infantry was refused "无法拆分这种类型的单位，因为这将导致这个单位成为空编制。" (it would leave the unit empty). Transports from the fleet answered a new fleet, the old one keeping 12 galleys and a heavy ship.
- **`army.attach` and `army.detach`**: the new army attached to the 35-regiment army; `army.get` then read it `attached` and the other `has_followers`, its `army.options` gave `detach` in place of `attach`, a second attach was refused ("the unit is attached already"), and `army.detach` freed it (`blocked` empty). The large army attached to the small one too, and was detached. A fleet in another province as the target was refused `not_found`; `army.detach` of a unit not attached was refused. A fleet attached to another fleet, its options then giving `detach`, and was detached.
- **`army.attach_to_transport`**: a 72-ship fleet with 33 transports ordered from its sea zone into Constantinople (151), where the 18-regiment army stood, arrived after 50 days. The army's options then gave `attach_to_transport`, can; after it, `detach_from_transport`, and attaching again was refused. `army.detach_from_transport` turned it back. Attached again, with the fleet then ordered out to sea zone 1320: six days later the army was aboard the fleet (`aboard` the fleet, the fleet `carrying` 18), as the button's description says.
- **`army.stop_auto_transport` and `army.auto_transport`** on the fleet with five transports: off, the options giving `auto_transport`; a second stop refused ("the fleet does not transport armies on its own"); on again, the options giving `stop_auto_transport`. A fleet of 30 light ships, which the AI had put on automatic transportation, read both states refused "你需要运输船来运输军队。" (AUTO_TRANSPORT_NO_COG); after the restart its `stop_auto_transport` was refused so, as the button refuses it, and the fleet with transports was turned off and on again.
- **`army.detach_special_units`**: none of the Ottomans' sixteen armies had special units (DETMER_NONE). Played through the console's `tag`, neither the Commonwealth, Ethiopia, Muscovy, Sweden, France nor Korea had an army with any; Spain's three were mercenary companies, refused "这支军队完全由特殊单位组成。" (DETMER_ALL) and "佣兵团不能被拆分，也不能和其它单位合并。". An army aboard a fleet read "部队装载进运输船时无法拆分。" (DETACH_SUBS_OF_TYPE_ARMY_ON_SEA).
- **The sweep** (`cargo xtask scenario sweep`), played in the same claim afterwards, passed: 295 capabilities in 5 places, 3,811 requests, each answered with its shape or a refusal the catalogue has. The release reported that none of the run's three games crashed.
- **After the run**: the workspace's tests and both clippy runs passed; the Lua host's `files_keep_the_extension_s_data_in_its_own_folder` failed once and passed when run again, as it has before.

## Not verified

- **Detaching special units successfully**: no army of special units and other regiments was found in the save, so `detach_merc` was posted only in the unit tests, not in the game.
- Attaching to another country's unit, which the game allows in some cases (`0xd4f0c0`), was not tried; nor attaching in the attach window's way to a unit the Shift click would not pick.
- The game's own written form of `toggle_auto_transfer` and `detach_subunit` was not compared on the command log: the commands the framework wrote were read back by the command layer and carried out, the fleet's and the army's state then changing as the panel's would.
- What the AI does with a unit the player attached or a fleet whose automatic transportation the player turned off was not watched beyond the days above.
