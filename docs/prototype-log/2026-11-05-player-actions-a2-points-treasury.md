# 2026-11-05 Player actions A2: monarch points and the treasury (tested by me)

A2 of [player-actions.md](../plans/player-actions.md), a developer build with the command log, within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset, the game in Chinese. Two new games as France at 1444.11.11, driven through remote control; the panels compared on screenshots.

- **Technology**: `technology.options` gave levels 3, 3, 3 at 598 points each, each refused with the view's lines ("没有足够的军事点数研究这一级科技！你需要 598 点而现在只有 84 点可用。", "我们预计将在1452年2月拥有足够点数来完成它。"). The first build gave only the second line; the view's tooltip has both, and the second build gives both. After the console's `powerpoints 1000`, `technology.research mil` took 598 military points and the view showed military 4; the next level cost 1231 ("领先于时代 +90%" in the view), its tooltip reading "你需要 1231 点而现在只有 486 点可用" as the refusal did.
- **National focus**: France starts on diplomatic; `focus.options` said so and offered cancelling, as the court view's tooltip ("点击将您的国家焦点从外交点数移除"). `focus.choose dip` was refused ("the national focus is on dip already"); `focus.choose adm` set it (the view's administrative gain went from 6 to 9); then `focus.cancel` and `focus.choose mil` were refused with the game's line, "每20年只能改变一次你的国家焦点，1464年11月11日后才能再次改变国家焦点。", the same as the court view's tooltip.
- **Stability**: at 84 administrative points refused with "你没有足够的adm提高稳定度！你需要100而可用点数只有84。"; with the points, three raises took stability from 0 to 3, the fourth was refused, "你的稳定度已达到最高。".
- **Economy**: `take_loan` offered 128 ducats, as the economy view's button; taken twice, the treasury grew by 256. `reduce_inflation` at 0 inflation was refused with the view's tooltip ("你目前通货膨胀不够高，你只能在它高于0时才能手动降低。", base cost 75, 74 with innovativeness, as `economy.options` said); after the loans it reduced inflation once and was refused again. `raise_mercantilism` was refused at 98 diplomatic points ("你需要100dip来提高重商主义，而你仅有98。"), then raised mercantilism from 10% to 11% for 99 points, the trade view's tooltip saying 99.
- **Loans**: `loan.list` gave each bank loan, 128 ducats at 4%, due 1449.11.11, repaying it 153.572, as the loan window shows them ("128 及 4.00%的利息。（总计：153.57）"). The first build read every loan's ID as the same (the ID's type, not its number); the second gives 1, 2, 3…. `loan.repay 1` paid 153.572 and the loan was gone; again, `not_found`. With the treasury at −235 (console `cash -4500`) every repay was refused with "你无力支付153.57¤以偿还贷款！", the loan window's tooltip on its disabled button.
- **Commands**: the command log showed the framework's commands run as the game writes them, `take_bank_loan={ … country="FRA" interest_modifier=0.000 duration=-1 fixed_interest=no interest=0.000 estate_loan=no }`, the keys left out at the game's defaults. The buttons were not pressed by hand to compare their commands.
- **Not tried**: too many bank loans (34 were still allowed), the rule against ruining a nation, the scripted functions' conditions (empty in the unmodded game), `may_not_reduce_inflation`, mercantilism at its most, a technology at its last level, loans from countries or estates, an extension calling these (only remote control was used).
