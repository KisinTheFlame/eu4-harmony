# 2026-11-05 Player actions A7: government (tested by me)

A7 of [player-actions.md](../player-actions.md), a developer build of this branch (its command log on), within two claims, started hidden. First, the AI's own commands logged for five game years from the save `Castilla-4` (the Netherlands, 1592.4.5) at speed 5, which gave the written forms of the A7 commands ([research/government.md](../research/government.md)). Then the same save loaded again for each build, the player the Netherlands, driven through remote control.

- **Reads**: `idea.list` gave the four groups taken (all ideas bought) first and 22 others, each refused with "the player has no free idea group slot"; `policy.list` four policies, `vassal_integration_act` enabled and refused with "－你已经启用了这一政策。"; `government.options` rank 3, reform progress 97.075, the reforms adopted, and raising the rank refused with "－你的政府等级已达最高！"; `government.reforms` 11 tiers, nine reached, changing a reform there costing 50, the next tier costing 420; `estate.list` the clergy, nobles and burghers with loyalty, influence and land share and their privileges, `estate_burghers_admirals` refused with "海军传统至少为30"; `estate.interactions` `seize_land` refused with "可在1594.03.09再次使用"; `parliament.options` no parliament; `mission.list` 64 missions in five columns, the uncompleted ones with their conditions ("拥有至少50艘轻型战舰"); `decision.list` seven, two takeable.
- **Actions**: enabling `the_royal_commission_act` enabled it (listed enabled after); disabling `vassal_integration_act` disabled it, and disabling the policy just enabled was refused by the game's command with no reason; changing tier 0's reform to `autocracy_reform` replaced `austrian_dual_monarchy` and took 50 reform progress; granting and revoking `estate_nobles_levies` both went through (the nobles' loyalty 10 lower after); `sale_of_titles` went through (every estate's land share higher, the clergy's and burghers' loyalty 10 higher) and was then refused with its cooldown; `development_of_amsterdam` was taken and left the list; abandoning `spy_ideas`, taking `aristocracy_ideas` and buying `serfdom` for 320 military points went through (571 to 251 points).
- **Refusals**: an enabled policy, an unknown policy (`not_found`), a group with no slot free, a group with every idea bought or not taken, the rank at its highest, a reform with too little progress or adopted already, a privilege not granted, a decision with its failing conditions (all seven lines of `ned_unite_two_crowns`), an unknown decision and mission, a completed mission, a mission with its conditions, a second idea with too few points ("buying it takes 320 mil points, and the player has 251"), debating and a seat without a parliament.
- **Found by trying**: the first build ended the game at `government.reforms` with "the framework used 0x79ead0, which no dependency point checks": four functions called had no point of their own; added. A change command naming the reform's place in the stack (1) replaced the second tier's reform instead: the member is the tier's index. The missions view's columns hold mission series, not missions (the first build faulted in writing the conditions of a series as a mission's). An idea's key is at `+0x158`. The database's group `none` was listed; it is not now.
- **Not tried**: parliament with a player who has one; a mission the player can complete; raising the rank where the game allows it; changing a reform for free; any panel compared on screen, the estates' influence among them; an extension calling the capabilities.
- **Cleanup**: both releases put the player's files back (every file as the backup had it) and the game folder's framework files.
