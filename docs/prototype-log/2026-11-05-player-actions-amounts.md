# 2026-11-05 Player actions follow-ups: the amounts (tested by me)

The two amounts the parts left unexplained ("Player actions A5", "the parts together" above), on a player build of `claude/player-actions-fu-amounts-3c701e`, within a claim, started hidden; two new games as France at 1444.11.11, driven through remote control only, the second with `remote_full_view=yes` in the framework settings to read the other countries' treasuries. What the game's code does is in [research/peace.md](../research/peace.md) ("Gold when the treaty is carried out") and [research/power-spending.md](../research/power-spending.md) ("The economy").

- **The loan**: paused, `economy.options` gave `take_loan` 128 ducats; `economy.take_loan` raised the treasury from 66.487 to 194.487 and `loan.list` listed a loan of 128. A month on (1444.12.1) with nothing done it was still 128. Then `province.develop_tax 183` raised it to 129 at once (`estate.grant_privilege estate_church estate_church_land_rights` after it left it at 129); a loan taken then raised the treasury by 129, from 198.323 to 327.323, and `loan.list` listed 128 and 129. So `gives` is what the player gets when they take the loan; in the parts' run together the options were read before `province.develop_tax` and the privilege, and the loan taken after. Nothing in the framework changed; the descriptions say the amount follows the income.
- **Peace gold**: at war with England (`declare_war ENG cb_core --province 167`, no allies called; France and its five appanages Armagnac, Auvergne, Bourbon, Foix and Orléans attacking), after `winwars` and the diplomat's month, the treasuries were France 74.262, Armagnac 35.334, Auvergne 38.964, Bourbon 37.958, Foix 38.02, Orléans 25.035, England 120.097. Demanding `[{"term":"gold","amount":300}]` was accepted: England −179.903 (300.000 paid), France 333.039 (+258.777), Orléans 66.258 (+41.223), the other four unchanged. The game shares the gold among the countries of the receiving side by their participation in the war, the treaty's actor taking what rounding leaves; the peace view's gold term says the whole 300, as the framework's term, `peace.terms`' most and `peace.evaluate`'s cost do, so they were left as they are, and the descriptions of `peace.terms`, `peace.demand` and the term's `amount` say that the player receives a share.
- **Not verified**: who counts as the receiving side when the treaty's lists and the war's side differ (a separate peace, a war leader's ally); the game's switch that gives the actor all the gold (`0x975110`), which was on in both runs; gold given in a peace offer (`peace.offer`), which by the code is paid in whole by the player.
