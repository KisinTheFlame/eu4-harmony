# 2026-11-04 The game unseen (tested by me)

Whether the game runs headless ([research/headless.md](../research/headless.md)). Two runs of `eu4.exe`, started directly, a player build of master (5d63c0a) installed with `dev-install.ps1` after the framework files in the game folder were copied aside; the player's playset (the Chinese language mod and the regional combat mod, whose extension loaded), a borderless 3840x2160 window, vsync on. The game was claimed with the game-lock skill, which backed up the player's files. A script in the scratchpad hid, showed and minimized the game's window with `ShowWindow` from outside the game, and measured through the command line: `menu` answered 15 times, the process's CPU over 8 s, `nvidia-smi pmon`, a screenshot at 25%, and `time advance` at speed 5 with questions answered with their first option.

- **The window shows itself again**: hidden 0.6 s after launch, it was shown again at 3.1 s, by the application's Init (`ShowWindow` at `0x14c02ca`, found afterwards); hidden again, it stayed hidden through the startup load (36.1 s to the main menu), `menu.start FRA` (5.2 s) and the game.
- **Hidden**: the game drew, presented and answered as when shown: requests answered in 28 to 37 ms (median, the command line's own start included), screenshots of the full frame in 103 to 158 ms, the graphics card 11 to 38% busy. At speed 5, 180 days at a time in the same game, hidden and shown in turn: 16.0 and 16.5 days a second hidden, 17.4 and 14.9 shown, at 286 to 304% and 269 to 290% of one core. `input key space` resumed and paused the game hidden.
- **Minimized**: requests answered in 86 to 191 ms, 2% of one core while paused, the graphics card idle in `pmon`, a screenshot in 581 ms, 4.1 days a second at speed 5 (one question on the way).
- **The camera ran away**: from the second measurement on, the map was at its northern edge in every screenshot, until the click of `input click` held the cursor. Inferred: the game read the cursor against the active window, another program's, and scrolled at the edge.
- **The first run's numbers are left out**: its hide came before Init showed the window, so its "hidden" was shown, and its time advances answered no questions; it showed the same for minimized.
- **Not tried**: hiding the window from inside the game (the framework has no such switch); a full screen game; sound; drawing skipped; a second game at once; a machine without a graphics card.
- **Cleanup**: the game was closed by ending its process. The three framework files in the game folder were put back and compared equal to the copies taken before. Releasing the game restored the player's files (the autosaves, `settings.txt` and the framework's logs among them) and found every file as the backup had it. The script, its screenshots, the copies of the framework files and of the logs were deleted.
