# 2026-10-31 Army orders that change nothing (tested by me)

The run of 2026-10-30 saw `army_ordered` for the Ottomans' army after a right click on the Sea of Marmara, its route unchanged. Two runs of `eu4.exe`, started directly, each a new game as Castile at 1444.11.11 (`menu.start CAS`), paused but for days passed with `time.advance`. Backups as AGENTS.md says, with the framework files installed in the game folder and the mod's DLL copied aside; the regional-combat extension was rebuilt from its repository's master against this SDK, in a copy of the repository, and its DLL replaced the mod's for the runs. An army was selected by clicking its model after `camera.look_at_province`, and ordered with a right click (`tools/game-input.ps1`). What the game does is in [research/army-orders.md](../research/army-orders.md).

- **First run, master with logging added for the run** (each movement command posted, each move and stop of the game's for an own army with its route before and after, each order heard at a safe point):
  - The army in Toledo, four days on its way to La Mancha (`2754`) and movement-locked: a right click on Albacete was posted, heard at the next safe point, and carried out 9 ms after the post, two installs making the route `[2754, 4547]`; one on Toledo gave `[2754, 219]`, and the same again `[2754, 219]`, set anew. Each was heard two or three times. Right clicks on the Gulf of Valencia and on Mallorca posted nothing.
  - The army in Asturias, with no route: a right click on the Cantabrian Sea next to it, with no fleet there, was posted and heard as `army_ordered`, and no install followed; the route stayed empty. So too with the army movement-locked on its way to `4554` with `206` next: heard, and the route `[4554, 206]` as it was. That is what the run of 2026-10-30 saw.
- **Second run, this change** (576 dependency points match; 105 hooks, 4 writes; the extension loaded), the army in Asturias:
  - With regional combat's task turned on by its button ("task on: center 207 radius 5"), the right click on the Cantabrian Sea raised no event, the route stayed empty and the task on. A right click on León raised one `army_ordered`, made the route `[208]` and turned the task off ("task off: manual order").
  - A right click on Asturias itself, with that route: one event, the route cleared; again, with none: no event.
  - `army.move` to `206`: one event. Five days later, movement-locked on the way to `4554` and the task on again: the Cantabrian Sea, no event and the task still on; León, two events, the route `[4554, 208]` and the task off; León again, two events and the same provinces.
- **Not seen in the running game**: orders given otherwise than by a right click (the stop button, a Shift-click, a mission); a move onto the sea with a fleet there; the game moving an own army by itself, as in a retreat; the final build, which differs from the one tried only in a type named for clippy.
- **Cleanup**: each game was closed by ending its process; no crash folder was made. `player-state.ps1 -Restore` listed what the runs changed (the framework's logs, `pdx_login_cache.txt`, `settings.txt`), put each back and found every file as the backup had it. The framework files and the mod's DLL were put back and compared equal to their copies; the regional-combat repository has no change. The copy of the repository, its build, the backups and the screenshots were deleted.
