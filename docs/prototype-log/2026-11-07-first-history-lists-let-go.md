# 2026-11-07 What a country's first history lists alone let go (tested by me)

The leftovers each new game left (research/main-menu.md, "A country's two histories"), found with `cargo xtask scenario leak`: six rounds listing them, three watching `0x296fba`, `0x298266`, `0x297c5e`, `0x71b1b1` and `0xa5c4b1` from the first round, and an experiment build, not committed, that counted at each world reset what `0x7e7f20` let go of a list, by the place that called it. Then the repair (`core/src/repairs/history_lists.rs`), four rounds of `leak` with the quarantine and ten of `growth`. Every run within the runner's claim.

- **Before**: each new game left, in every round, 2,101 blocks of records under a date (`0x296fba`, 131 KB) and the records under them and beside them in the history reader (`0x29a1bc` 134 KB, `0x299caa` 96 KB, `0x297c5e` 84 KB, `0x298266`, `0x298312` and `0x297d12` 76 KB each, and others), about 0.9 MB, nothing pointing at the blocks. The watch: of the 4,175 blocks under a date nothing pointed at, 1,825 were last pointed at by a list's node that `0x7e7f20` let go, 547 unrecorded, the rest by blocks that pointed at them for a moment (sets of modifiers, `0x7d34a0`, `0xa36d80`).
- **Who lets them go**: the experiment counted `0x7e7f20` called from `0x2e37de` (a country's clear, its first history) letting go 12,065 or 10,474 objects after a load from a save, as many as the second history (`0x2e381a`) had just deleted, and 10,293 after a new game, against 880.
- **With the repair, the quarantine**: four rounds, no crash; each new game's world reset logged "9413 records and blocks of records the countries' first histories listed alone were deleted, 0 kept", the loads' 0; the quarantine kept 58,356 blocks of the first histories' (3.9 MB), and nothing was written to after a repair let it go. `0x296fba` and the history reader's sites were in no later generation's leftovers. The monarchs, heirs and queens the histories left were deleted as before, 2,838, 2,286 and 154 a round.
- **Ten rounds of `growth`**: passed, no crash, each round 26 to 28 s. Over rounds 2 to 10: the game's heap held 1.18 MB a round more, against 2.13 to 2.42; the private bytes grew 10.4 MB a round (13.3 to 14.1 on 2026-11-06), 10.7 MB the heaps' unused pages, -1.6 MB outside them, which go up and down by 40 MB from round to round.
- **Not verified**: twenty rounds; a new game as a country other than France, and the in-game load of a save made in a new game; the panels showing a country's history (its rulers in the court and the ledger) after a new game and a return. `0x71b1b1` and `0xa5c4b1`, which loads leave, were watched and not followed.
- **Cleanup**: the experiment was taken out before anything was committed. Each release put back the player's files and the game folder's framework files and found them as the backup had them; the reports and the logs the releases kept were deleted.
