# 2026-11-05 Player actions from a Lua extension, and the new events' handlers (tested by me)

The capabilities of [player-actions.md](../plans/player-actions.md) called from a Lua extension, and the handlers of its new events, which no run had tried. A player build of the follow-up branch, within a claim, started hidden; the player's playset with a scratch mod made with `eu4-harmony new`, linked and added to `dlc_load.json` for the run. The mod logged every answer as one line of JSON, and every event its handlers heard; it ran Lua it found in its data folder (`files`) at its next frames, as a task, which drove the run. A scratch program held each logged answer to the catalogue's result shape (`result_shape`, the shapes the editor's types are written from). Two processes, each a new game as France at 1444.11.11; time advanced through remote control, questions answered through it too.

- **Every group** extensions have (38) answered a read from Lua, and every group with actions took one or refused it with the game's reasons: `province.develop_tax` (refused for points), `recruit.order`, `diplomacy.improve_relations`, `declare_war` and `warn`, `question.answer`, `focus.choose`, `economy.take_loan`, `reduce_inflation`, `loan.repay`, `estate.grant_privilege`, `subject.interact` (`placate_rulers` on Orléans), `leader.recruit`, `assign`, `mercenary.hire`, `fort.mothball`, `reopen`, `building.build`, `province.make_territory`, `peace.demand`, `camera.look_at`, `zoom`, `missionary.raise_automatic`, `lower_automatic`; refused with the game's words: `technology.research`, `stability.raise`, `idea.take_group`, `policy.enable`, `government.adopt_reform`, `mission.complete`, `colonist.send`, `trade_company.add`, `center_of_trade.upgrade`, `merchant.collect`, `missionary.send`, `siege.assault` (no fort at Bordeaux), `occupation.transfer` (no ally in the war), `parliament.debate` (no parliament).
- **Shapes**: all 156 answers held to their capability's shape, the empty table Lua writes as `{}` read as an empty list where the shape is one.
- **The editor's types** were wrong for seven answers, which no answer showed: a class is written once, by its name, and six names, `Loan`, `Technology`, `IdeaGroup`, `Interaction`, `Construction` and `Fort`, were each two types, so `loan.list`, `technology.options`, `idea.list`, `estate.interactions`, `subject.options`, `building.options` and `fort.list` had another answer's fields (`loan.list`'s loans `lender, ducats, interest, due`, a country's loans). Each now has a name of its own, and a test holds one class to one shape. The handlers' types gave `on_province_owner_changed`'s `from` and `to` as `string`; they are `string|nil` now.
- **The full view**: Lua read Muscovy's treasury (52.7) and monarch power and the Muscovite army in Moscow under fog of war; remote control's `country.get MOS` gave both as null and `army.at 295` `not_ready` (visibility not current yet) at the same moment.
- **Errors**: a call the game could not do returned `nil, err` with `{code, message}` and nothing else: `not_found` for `army.get(987654321)`, `country.get("QQQ")`, a decision and an offer that do not exist, `army.selected` without the army panel open; `rejected` with the game's reasons for the refusals above, `peace.terms` not at war, `siege.options` with no siege; `invalid_argument` for `time.set_speed(9)`; `not_ready` for `army.route_to` and `army.move` while paused, as documented for extensions. A call made wrongly raised an error naming the argument (`province.get("Paris")`, `army.move()` with no arguments, `building.build(183, 7)`), which `pcall` caught. The wording read "a integer"; it is "an integer" now.
- **Found and fixed: `load` without an environment**: the sandbox's `load` passed its fourth argument on even when not given, and an environment given as nil is the chunk's, so every chunk an extension loaded without one had a nil `_ENV` and failed at its first global ("attempt to index a nil value (upvalue '_ENV')"). Fixed, and in the second process the mod's `load(source, "=cmd")` saw the globals. An error in the task did not stop the extension: its next commands ran.
- **Found and fixed**: `government.adopt_reform centralize_reform` gave "在欧洲拥有少于15个省份。" twice, the line being in the reform's potential and its trigger; in the second process once.
- **Handlers**: `on_month` at 1444.12.1 and 1445.1.1, `on_year` at 1445.1.1 after it; `on_battle` for battles anywhere (rebels against Ansbach, Livonia, Trier, Liège, Münster, Riga, ...), each a table with province, naval, attackers, defenders, winner and both losses; `on_war_declared` for France's declaration on England ("法兰西收复拉布尔之战", FRA, ENG) and for wars of others (Mamluks on Anizah, Hosokawa on Kono); `on_province_owner_changed` 49 and 4747 Teutonic Order to Brandenburg, and after the peace 174 ENG to FRA, `from` and `to` strings; `on_peace_made` with the attackers France and its five appanages, the defenders England, Ulster, Kildare and Portugal, `ended` true; `on_building_finished` 16 `fort_15th` NOR.
- **The war**: `diplomacy.declare_war` was refused before 1444.12.11 ("在1444年12月11日之前不能宣战。"), then declared with the first war goal (`cb_core`, Labourd); both armies were sent to Bordeaux, besieged and took it; after the console's `winwars`, `peace.evaluate` accepted (+100 "被彻底击败：") and `peace.demand` Bordeaux `accepted`.
- **Not tried**: a battle France fought (the English armies were in London and Portugal, out of reach), so `on_army_defeated` was not heard; a building of France's finishing (the castle built in Paris finishes in 1447); an extension's first error raising its notice, not looked at; `on_battle`'s table has no class in the editor's types (`table`).
- **Cleanup**: the release put the player's files back (every file as the backup had it) and the game folder's framework files; the mod's link file was deleted.
