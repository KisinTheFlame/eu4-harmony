# 2026-11-07 The windows killed at a return taken out (tested by me)

Why the game ended about once in ten rounds of `growth` ([The relations and the trade nodes let go](2026-11-07-relations-trade-nodes-let-go.md)): runs of twenty rounds of `cargo xtask scenario growth` on this branch and on the commits before it, each within the runner's claim, the crash folders the runner kept read with `cargo xtask crash-dump`.

- **The crash**: in a return's switch, as the map manager's popups were deleted (`0x81e953`), a GUI object's destructor (`0x14317c0`, `0x14318c0`, `0x14a1ce0`, `0x14a24e0`) called a method of each window in its lists, one of them freed: a pure call (`0x1a35124`), the game's crash handler then writing its dump (`0x1655290`; its dumps have no exception context, and the breakpoint, `0x80000003`, is the handler's). In one run, an in-game load answered "faulted: 0xc0000005 at RVA 0x152e105, reading 0xffffffffffffffff" and another at `0x144f5b8`, as it killed a window of the interface (`0x10547d0`, `0x1547520`).
- **Where it came from**: the branch ended at the 4th, 7th, 8th, 8th, 12th, 15th, 18th and 20th round of runs of twenty; `05022e80` (master with the session's panels' windows killed) passed once and ended at the 4th round of the next; `c48c14a0` (before them) passed twice, forty rounds. Guessed before that and found wrong by the runs: the trade nodes and the relations deleted too early (they now wait for a return, which changed nothing), the leaders' records and the persons of what a country's first history lists alone.
- **Taken out**: the session's panels' windows are no longer killed at a return; the return is still counted, for the trade nodes and the relations. Twice twenty rounds of `growth` with every other repair: both passed, no crash; over rounds 2 to 20 the game's heap held 3.9 MB a round more, against 0.78 with the windows killed, the private bytes 10.1 to 10.3 MB, the heaps' unused pages 6.5 to 6.7 MB.
- **Not verified**: which windows the GUI object's lists keep; whether killing only the windows no global and no such list holds would keep the 3 MB a round without the crash.
- **Cleanup**: the experiments (a vectored exception handler, which itself faulted once reading past a thread's stack; a probe of the pure call; leaving the leaders' records and the persons) were taken out before anything was committed. Each release put back the player's files and the game folder's framework files and found them as the backup had them; the reports, dumps and logs the releases kept were deleted.
