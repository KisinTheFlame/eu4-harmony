# 2026-11-10 The world in a second process: addresses and a handover's cost (tested by me)

For [research/world-in-a-second-process.md](../research/world-in-a-second-process.md) (W2, W3 of [drawing-thread.md](../plans/drawing-thread.md)). Two hidden test games (`w23-a`, `w23-b`) held at once beside another session's, cores built from the drawing thread's branch with `dev-test-games`, the second start of `w23-a` with `dev-memory` too; the player's playset and settings. Read from outside with `NtQueryInformationProcess`, `ReadProcessMemory` and `VirtualQueryEx`; the Windows experiments in a throwaway C# snippet against `cmd.exe` children.

- **Module bases**, both games at the main menu: `eu4.exe` at `0x7ff655300000` in both, and every other module looked at (`ntdll`, `kernel32`, `KERNELBASE`, `ucrtbase`, `tbb.dll`, `steam_api64.dll`, `d3d9.dll`, `d3dx9_43.dll`, `nvd3dumx.dll`, `EOSSDK-Win64-Shipping.dll`, `pops_api.dll`, `nakama-cpp.dll`, `version.dll` both the proxy and the system's) at the same base in both; the framework's core, from each claim's own folder, not.
- **Private memory**, the same two: the process heap at `0x25979e80000` and `0x1cb87640000`, the PEB at `0xadca40d000` and `0x4b072c3000`; 3,677 and 3,670 MB committed in 611 and 615 allocations, the largest two of 255 MB, then 140, 66.8 and 55.6 MB, at different addresses in each. The PEB's count of heaps read 1 in both, which the memory account's six contradict; the PEB's offsets used (`+0xe8`, `+0xf0`) were not checked.
- **The memory account**, `w23-a` with the 1780.7.1 save: at the main menu the game's heap 1.80 GB of 2.04 GB committed; after `menu.load` (9 s) 2.08 GB of 2.36 GB, 1.99 GB outside the heaps, 4.35 GB private in all, six heaps. Startup to the main menu: 31 and 36 s for the two games side by side, 18 s for `w23-a`'s second start.
- **A range kept free**: in a child made suspended, a placeholder of 64 GB reserved at `0x100000000000` before it was resumed; after it ran, the range was still reserved (state `MEM_RESERVE`, 64 GB) and its process heap was at `0x203ad550000`, then `0x21063810000` in a second run.
- **A section at a chosen address**: in another suspended child, a placeholder of 64 MB at `0x100000000000` replaced by a view of a paging-file section; the child read through it the eight bytes the parent wrote through its own view, and their change.
- **Write watching**: 2 GB allocated with `MEM_WRITE_WATCH`, every page touched, the watch reset; 17,920 random pages written (13 to 16 ms), 17,602 different ones; `GetWriteWatch` with reset 6.0 and 7.1 ms; the pages copied into a 2 GB section on one thread 41.6 and 44.7 ms the first time, 14.0 ms again once the section's pages were committed.
- **Not verified**: a second process holding a game's world, or anything of B in the game; that the base of an image is the same in every process in every case; the heap's large blocks walked; the copy on several threads.
- **Cleanup**: both test games released, their logs kept in their claims' `kept\`; the scripts in the session's scratchpad; the children ended by the snippet.
