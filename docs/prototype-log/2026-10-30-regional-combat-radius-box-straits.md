# 2026-10-30 Regional combat's radius box, straits, defeat and ironman saves (tested by me)

What the F1 run left unseen of the regional-combat extension. The build was master at `Draw the map font's outline around system glyphs on the map`, after the build, the tests and the offline checks against `eu4.exe` passed. The regional-combat extension was rebuilt for the first time since the SDK changes, from its repository's master (`Move texts to EU4 Harmony localisation files, with English`) against this SDK, in a copy of the repository; its 16 tests pass, and the DLL it made replaced the one in the mod for the run. `eu4.exe` was started directly, Chinese playset, system fonts. Backups as AGENTS.md says, with the framework files installed in the game folder and the mod's DLL copied aside. The game was driven by `eu4-harmony game` and `tools/game-input.ps1`.

- **Startup**: 579 dependency points match; 106 hooks, 4 writes; the extension loaded.
- **The radius box**: a new game as Byzantium, its army (8 regiments) selected in Constantinople. A right click on the extension's button opened the box at "半径 5" with the map preview; two clicks on its plus button showed "半径 7", and its apply button closed the box, put the map mode back and logged "army 111669149750 radius 7". Opened again it read "半径 7"; the minus button made it "半径 6", the cancel button closed it, and opened once more it read "半径 7".
- **Straits**: at 1444.12.11, war on the Ottomans (`cb_core`, Edirne; the game refuses every declaration before that date). `province.neighbors 151` lists 316 across a strait; the adjacency file has both the Bosporus (151–316) and the Dardanelles (4779–2296) through the Sea of Marmara (1320).
  - Open: `army.can_enter 316` turned from false to true with the war, and both straits read open. The radius box's preview at 7 took in Thrace, the Morea and much of Anatolia across the straits.
  - Blocked: on 1444.12.14, with nothing done, the Bosporus read closed to the Byzantine army for one day. To hold a block, the Byzantine fleet was sent into the Sea of Marmara (where the game said it blockaded Gallipoli, Kocaeli and two more ports), and `tag TUR` in the game console made the player the Ottomans. For their army, the Bosporus read closed and the Dardanelles open. The game's own route from Thessaly to Kocaeli agreed with that: `[148,4705,4779,2296,317,316]`, across the Dardanelles. Why the Dardanelles stayed open for the Ottomans was not looked into; their fleet lay in Gallipoli's port, at that strait's end. Their 19-ship fleet was then sent into the Sea of Marmara, and on 1444.12.25 `tag BYZ` made the player Byzantium again. Both straits read closed to its army, and the preview at 7 took in Thrace and the Morea but none of Anatolia.
- **Defeat**: after `army.stop` cleared the route the game had given the army while the player was the Ottomans, a left click turned its task on, "center 151 radius 7". On 1444.12.27 it ordered "move to Some(149): accepted". From 1444.12.29 the army fought in Constantinople, and from the next day the task logged "deferred: army cannot take orders now" with `in_battle: true` each day. On 1445.1.2 the army was gone (`army.get` answered `not_found`), remote control's events had `army_defeated` for it, and the extension logged "task off: lost a battle". Later, in the ironman save below, a task turned on for a Castilian army of three regiments also ended in "task off: lost a battle", with that army destroyed too.
- **Sessions on a change of country**: each `tag` in the game console started a new session ("session 2 started (from a loaded save: false)"), and the extension started it with no task ("restored 0 task(s)"). The radius applied before showed as 5 again, and the 7 had to be applied once more.
- **Ironman saves**: a copy of the ironman save `Castilla-5` as `rc-ironman-test` (Castile, 1470.3.13, on a crusade against the Ottomans), loaded with `menu.load`. The game rewrites an ironman save it loads: the file's digest changed as the session started.
  - Writing: the half-year autosave of 1470.7.1 and the save the game makes when it leaves for the main menu (`menu.return`) each wrote extension save data under the file's new digest ("saves: wrote extension data for …rc-ironman-test.eu4").
  - Restoring: a task turned on for the army in Constantinople (14 regiments, with followers, so the task gives it no order) with radius 8 was saved on leaving. Loading the save gave "loaded … with data for 1 extension(s)" and "session 2: restored 1 task(s)". Its radius box opened at "半径 8", and the preview was centered on 151.
  - **Lost after a crash (a defect)**: the save the game makes while loading writes no extension save data. The framework makes the loaded data current only at the session's first safe frame (`ext::frame`), after that save. The game was then ended before any other save, as a crash would end it; started again and loaded, it gave "data for 0 extension(s)" and "restored 0 task(s)", and the task was gone. The standalone regional combat before the port had handled this save.
- **Seen on the way**: as the Ottomans, a right click on the Sea of Marmara with their movement-locked army selected raised `army_ordered` for that army, though its route stayed `[149,151]`. Not looked into.
- **Not seen in the running game**: the extension ordering a move across a strait; a strait closing while a task's army was on its way across; a defeat the army survived (both armies were destroyed); a normal save's own rewrite on loading.
- **Cleanup**: each game was closed by ending its process; no crash folder was made. `player-state.ps1 -Restore` listed what the run changed (the autosaves, the test save and its `_Backup`, four extension save data files, the framework's logs, `continue_game.json`, `pdx_login_cache.txt`, `shortcuts.txt`), put each back and found every file as the backup had it. The framework files installed before the run and the mod's own DLL were put back and compared equal to their copies; the regional-combat repository has no change. The copy of the repository, its build, the backups, logs and screenshots of the run were deleted.
