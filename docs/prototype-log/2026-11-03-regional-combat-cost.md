# 2026-11-03 Regional combat's cost, and what the capabilities read (tested by me)

L6's fourth point: regional combat in Lua, France from 1444.11.11, war on England in December, the Paris army's task on (center 183, radius 5), then 120 days at speed. Six runs, the same steps each time; from the second on, the mod's `main.lua` timed every capability call with `os.clock` and logged each frame over 5 ms (not committed).

- **`army.range` and named provinces, core unchanged**: 17,975 stretches, 4,917 ms in all, 111 over 5 ms, the longest 55 ms, no better than the run before them. Lua's own work was about nothing: a day's decision spent 30 to 55 ms in the capabilities, `province.list` 13 to 29 ms for two provinces (the core read every province, then picked), `army.range` 11 to 21 ms, `army.can_attack` about 1 ms a call (it looked through every land province for the army).
- **Two shortcuts, taken back out**: `army.range` asking a province's open steps in one call, and `army.can_attack` looking first where `army.at` last saw the army. 2,966 ms, 37 over 5 ms, the longest 18 ms. They went around the cause rather than at it, and `army.range` carried the mod's own algorithm into the catalogue ([ADR mods-hold-the-logic](../adr/mods-hold-the-logic.md)).
- **The cause**: every read of game memory was a `ReadProcessMemory` call, 325 ns for 8 bytes against under 1 ns for a plain read; and a capability call from Lua cost 5.8 µs before it reached the game, measured with the test host, most of it finding the capability by name and writing the request as JSON text and reading it back.
- **Atomic capabilities, reads in the process, the game's ID lookup**: the mod back on `province.neighbors` and `army.can_step` for its range, the provinces it reads named; reads under SEH ([ADR game-memory-is-read-in-process-under-seh](../adr/game-memory-is-read-in-process-under-seh.md)); armies found by the game's own lookup. The task never decided: the decision core refused a snapshot whose range steps named provinces not in it (the mod read only the range's members), a mod bug the fake world could not show, as it has no military access. 18,910 stretches, 3,144 ms, 41 over 5 ms; a day's 452 `can_step` calls 3 to 18 ms.
- **The snapshot fixed, and the call from Lua made cheaper** (1.4 µs with the test host): 19,403 stretches, 2,305 ms in all, 20 over 5 ms, the longest 14 ms. A day's decision took 2 to 3 ms in most frames, its 452 `can_step` calls 1 to 2 ms. The task waited, attacked Bordeaux (174), moved to 4387 and back to 174, each move accepted, and waited while the army's orders were locked.
- **Frames over 5 ms with nothing costly in them**: most of the 20, 7 to 13 ms in one call that reads a few fields (`session.get`, `army.selected`) or in no call at all; frames like them came before the task was on and in every run. Not explained: `os.clock` and the stretch's own timing count the wall clock, so the main thread may have waited or been off the processor.
- **The spread, a sixth run**, the same build with stretches kept as a histogram and the session's share logged at its end (`menu.return` after the 120 days; the session from 1444.11.11, the task on from 1444.12.12): 20,547 stretches, 1,908 ms in all; mean 0.093 ms, median 0.063 ms, p95 0.132 ms, p99 0.424 ms, the longest 12.7 ms; 12 over 5 ms (5 to 12 ms). 0.59% of the session's 321.0 s, 0.95% of the main thread's 200.5 s of CPU, 0.46% of the game's 417.0 s of CPU. The task attacked, moved between 174 and 4387, and returned to 183, each move accepted.
- **Not met**: no stretch over 5 ms.
- **Cleanup**: each game was closed by ending its process. Releasing the game restored the player's files, the autosaves and the logs among them, and found every file as the backup had it.
