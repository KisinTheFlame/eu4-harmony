# 2026-11-05 Player actions A6: the diplomacy panel, subjects and rivals (tested by me)

A developer build of this branch with the command log (`dev-command-log`, logging `diplomaticactioncommand`, `subject`, `add_rival` and `remove_rival`), installed within a claim and started hidden; new games as France at 1444.11.11, driven through `eu4-harmony game …`; the player's Chinese playset, 3840x2160. Three processes: the first ended at the first subject request (below), the second and third ran the rest.

- **`diplomacy.options CAS`** listed 56 actions in the panel's order, each named by its capability, with its button text as the game's (宣战, 提议结盟, 建立包围网, …) and the game's reasons for each that could not be sent (宣战: "在1444年12月11日之前不能宣战。"; 要求附庸: two reasons at once; the spy actions: the network's size and the technology). The diplomacy panel opened on Castile from its province showed the same: 提议结盟 green with its tick, 建立包围网 greyed, 提供遣外雇佣军团 enabled. **Acceptance**: `propose_alliance` had `accepts` true, total 60, and five factors (陆军实力 -1, 海军实力 -5, 好感度 +6, 态度是友好 +50, 外交声誉 +10); the tick's tooltip said 他们很可能会接受, 赞成 66, 反对 -6, 当前有60, and the same five lines with the same values (it lists those for first, those against after; the framework keeps the game's order). Not compared on screen: any other action's acceptance.
- **`diplomacy.options ORL`** (an appanage) listed among its actions the scripted `promote_appanage_heir` (指定王储) and `annex` with the game's three reasons (ten years, +190 opinion, a seizure of land first).
- **Sent and answered**: `propose_alliance CAS` accepted, and Castile was an ally; `arrange_royal_marriage POR` and later `CAS` declined; `insult ENG` done; `send_gift SAV --amount 10` done, the treasury 66.44 → 56.44; `improve_relations ARA` done, after which `options ARA` listed `stop_improving_relations`, and that was done too; `embargo ENG` done; `ask_military_access NAV` accepted; `offer_loan ORL --amount 10` declined; `sell_province SAV --province 200 --price 100` declined; `offer_condottieri CAS --army 545460846646 --price 5` declined. The command log showed what each window would have written: `money=10.000`; `amount=10 duration=12 interest=1.000`; `province=200 amount=100`; `cost=5.000 unit={ id=127 type=54 }`.
- **Refused with the game's reasons**: a second diplomat to the same country ("最近已经派遣过外交官，所以直到1444年12月12日我们才能再次派遣。"), none left ("你没有外交官可供派遣。"), `send_gift --amount 100000` ("我们缺乏所需的金钱", after the diplomat's), `warn CAS` (too rich and strong), `declare_war CAS` on an ally (three reasons), `claim_throne CAS` (three), `transfer_trade_power ORL` (a subject), `sell_province SAV --province 183` ("巴黎距离萨伏依太远，无法将其核心化。"), `fabricate_claim SAV --province 200` (the network's size, and "蒙彼利埃已有核心。": the province given reached the game's check), `break_alliance SAV --ally CAS` (prestige).
- **Subjects**: the first `subject.options ORL` ended the game: the framework stopped it, "the framework used 0x1fd5238, which no dependency point checks" (the interactions' objects were not yet points of their own; they are now, each with its class). Then `subject.options ORL` listed 16 interactions with their names (安抚当地统治者, 免服兵役税, 转移贸易, 授予省份, …) and reasons (夺取宫廷资源: "属国的独立倾向必须低于25%。"). `subject.interact ORL scutage` was carried out, and `options` then showed it `active`; `placate_rulers` was carried out, and a second refused ("必须有正威望。", the full-width bullet then left in the reason, now taken off); `seize_court_resources` refused with its reason; an unknown key `invalid_argument`; Castile "not a subject of the player's".
- **Rivals**: `add_rival ENG`, `NAV` and `BUR` were carried out, and `country.get` listed them as rivals; a second `add_rival ENG` was refused with no reason; `remove_rival ENG` refused with "最近刚将英格兰设为宿敌，1449年11月11日前不能将其移除。", `remove_rival CAS` (no rival) with "需要花费100dip才能移除宿敌。". Before the game's own check was asked first, `add_rival NAV` had been carried out once and Navarre was no rival 35 days later; not looked into.
- **Not tried in the game**: most of the panel's other actions (each goes the same way as those sent: the panel's own action, its checks, its command); the acts whose window has the player choose a province, a country, an army or an amount beyond those above (`fabricate_claim` and `claim_states` carried out, `subsidize_war`, `transfer_trade_power`, `threaten_war`, `break_alliance`, the favors, `enforce_peace`, `intervene_in_war`, `demand_unlawful_territory`, `charter_company`, `appoint_cardinal`); those sent as the panel made them though their window chooses something the framework does not set (`support_rebels`, `share_maps`, `steal_maps`, `sell_ships`, `invite_to_federation`'s name); a scripted action carried out; annexing and integrating (no subject could be); the subject interactions that need a third country, an index or a name; Lua extensions calling any of it.
- **Cleanup**: the release put back every file the run changed and the game folder's framework files; `dev.toml` and the screenshots were deleted.
