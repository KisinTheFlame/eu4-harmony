# The map camera

Baseline 1.37.5. All addresses are RVAs. Found by reading eu4.exe on 2026-10-29; what was checked in the running game is marked "tested".

## Where the map is in the camera's world

- **Map positions.** The camera works in a world where x runs across the map from its left edge and z up the map from its bottom edge, both in pixels of the province map (`map/provinces.bmp`; 5632 by 2048, `map/default.map`), and y is the height above the map. The same pair is how `map/positions.txt` writes positions: Stockholm (1) at 3085, 1723 is north of Paris (183) at 2842, 1494. Tested: see [the camera's run](../../research/interface-evidence.md).
- **The province at a position.** The map (the global `0x233fed8`, `campaign::globals::MAP`) has the map's width at `+0x28` and its height at `+0x2c` (i32 each), and at `+0x10` the province ID of every pixel, an i16, row after row from z = 0 up: the ID at x, z is at index z × width + x. 0 or less is no province. Code that finds the province under a point reads it so, for example `0x109315a`–`0x10931c6` (`mov r15, [0x233fed8]` … `41 8b 47 28 3b c8 7d 2c 41 3b 5f 2c 7d 26 0f af d8 03 d9 8b cb 49 8b 47 10 48 0f bf 14 58`: x against the width, z against the height, z × width + x, the i16 there) before handing the ID to `0x9aa80`, which gives the province. The mouse's province (`0x8333e4`–`0x83342a`) reads the same layout from the map the map renderer keeps.
- **A province's position.** A province's map data (`[province + 0x28]`, `campaign::geometry`) has at `+0x7c` two i16, x and z: where the game looks at the province from (below). `+0xa0` is another such pair, which the game looks at when a game starts (`0x81b691`, the capital's).

## The camera

- **Where it is.** The map manager (`campaign::world::MAP_MANAGER`) keeps the camera at `+0x1200`. Its method at vtable offset `0x1a0` (`0x5c44e0`, `48 8b 81 00 12 00 00 c3`) reads it; the game console command `debug_lockcamera` (`0xe85a90`) reaches the camera so, through the world's map manager. The map manager makes it at `0x80a179` (`new` of 0x7d0 bytes, constructor `0x109b830`, which names `eu3camera.cpp` in its asserts) and stores it at `0x80a17f`.
- **Its class** is the vtable `0x1d57348`, which the constructor sets at `0x109b871` (`48 8d 05 d0 ba cb 00 48 89 01`).
- **Its points**, three f32 each, x, y (the height) and z:
  - `+0x67c` where it is and `+0x688` where it looks, as drawn now. The debug readout's "Camera coords" prints `+0x67c`, `+0x680` and `+0x684` (`0x819c4f`–`0x819cb9`).
  - `+0x694` where it is going and `+0x6a0` where it will look. The game moves the camera from the first pair towards the second a little every frame.
  - `0x109cd80` puts the camera at its goal at once: it copies `+0x694`..`+0x6a8` to `+0x67c`..`+0x690`, keeps both on the map (`0x109d0e0`, which hands `+0x67c`/`+0x688` and then `+0x694`/`+0x6a0` to `0x109cf60`, bytes at `0x109d0ff`), and rebuilds the view of the camera object inside it at `+0x30` (position `+0x1b0`, look-at `+0x1bc`, up `+0x1c8`), its outline on the ground (`0x109ded0`) and its copy across the wrap (`0x109e040`). The game itself calls it when the camera is made (`0x109bcc1`) and after the camera is panned (`0x109f655`, which moves both pairs by the same amount first).
- **Its heights.** `+0x6ec` and `+0x6f0` (i32 each) are the lowest and the highest the camera may be: the constructor truncates the f32 globals `0x233e714` and `0x233e770` into them (`0x109bbba`), which the defines loader fills from `NFrontend.CAMERA_MIN_HEIGHT` and `CAMERA_MAX_HEIGHT` (`0x4d8f6c`, `0x4d8ff9`; 50 and 3000 in `common/defines.lua`).
- **Keeping it on the map**, `0x109cf60` (rcx the camera, rdx a position, r8 its look-at, r9 the map): the look-at's z between 0 and the map's height; its x between 0 and the width unless the map wraps; the position follows the look-at; the position's height between the lowest the ground allows there and `+0x6f0`. The lowest there is `0x109cd00`: the largest of the heightmap's value under the position × 0.2, 19, and `+0x6ec`.
- **Locked**, `+0x758` (u8): while it is set, `0x109d140` (below) leaves the camera as it is, and so every move of the game's that goes through it. `debug_lockcamera` toggles it, and the game sets and clears it itself, for example clearing it once a new game looks at the player's capital (`0x81b6dc`); `0x818719`, `0x824acc`, `0x10d29e9` and `0x1111bae` set it.
- **Wraps**, `+0x778` (u8): the map wraps around from its right edge to its left. The map manager makes its camera with it set (`r9b` = 1 at `0x80a16d`).

## Moving it

- **Looking at a point from a height**, `0x109d140` (rcx the camera, rdx three f32: x, a height, z), when not locked: the goal look-at becomes x, 0, z, and the goal position x, the height, z − 0.3 × `camera_tilt` (the f32 at `+0x27c` of `CEU4Settings` at `0x242bde8`, default 100.0); then both are kept on the map (`0x109d0e0`). So the camera looks up the map, tilted, and the point it looks at is on the plane y = 0, the map's base plane.
- **Looking at a province**, `0x80fe20`, the map manager's method (rcx the map manager, rdx the province): nothing for a province ID of 0 or less (`+0x20`); otherwise the position at `+0x7c` of the province's map data, with x moved by the map's width (`[0x233fed8] + 0x28`) where the map wraps and the copy across the edge is nearer the camera's x; the height kept from the goal position (`+0x698`); then `0x109d140`. It has 54 call sites, which were not looked at one by one; `0x80fd30` is the same for a province ID, through the world's province array.
- **Looking at a map position**, `0x80fd70` (rcx the map manager, edx x, r8d z): the same as `0x80fe20` for a position given as two i32. No direct call to it was found.
- **The game's own moves glide**: none of the moves above calls `0x109cd80`, so the camera reaches its goal over some frames through `0x109f6d0` (below).
- **Zooming to a province with a panel open**, `0x139c6a0` (rcx the camera, rdx a position's two i16, r8 a panel or null, xmm3 a zoom between 0 and 1): its height is `0x109d830`, the lowest the ground allows at the goal + zoom² × (`+0x6f0` − that); the panel's width moves the province off the middle of the screen into the part the panel leaves (`0x109d540`, which finds where the camera has to be for a point to show at a point of the screen). The game uses it with `NGui.PROVINCE_FOCUS_ZOOM_HEIGHT` (0.25, the global `0x233e844`). The framework does not use `0x139c6a0`.

## Mouse-wheel zoom and gliding (read 2026-11-25)

- **Settings fields** on `CEU4Settings` (`[0x242bde8]`, constructed by `0xad2380` on top of `CSettings` `0x1559fc0`):
  - `+0xc8`: `scroll_speed` (f32, default 50.0)
  - `+0xcc`: `camera_rotation_speed` (f32, default 50.0)
  - `+0xd0`: `zoom_speed` (f32, default 50.0, in-game slider clamped to >= 1.0 in `0x10e17f0`)
  - `+0x278`: `camera_speed` (f32, default 50.0; higher values slow down `0x109f6d0`)
  - `+0x27c`: `camera_tilt` (f32, default 100.0)
  - `+0x280`: `centered_zoom_in` (u8, default 1)
- **From Windows to the camera**:
  - SDL 2.0.4's window procedure (`0x173ac20`, `WM_MOUSEWHEEL` at `0x173b763`) accumulates the signed 16-bit delta into the global i16 `0x23349e8` (`0x173b76a`) without clearing on reversal, calling `SDL_SendMouseWheel` (`0x1755230`) once per ±120 with `y = ±1`.
  - The event pump (`0x1569c70`, `SDL_MOUSEWHEEL` `0x403` at `0x156a44f`) normalizes `wheel.y` to `±1` and appends a mouse event (`type = 0`, `subtype = 4`, `value = ±1`) to the frame's list.
  - On desktop (`[0x233ff4f] == 0`), `0x830fd0` calls `0x109bee0` → `0x109e750`. As `0x109e750` walks the frame's event list, `0x109e7bb` executes `mov [r14], eax` (`41 89 06`) instead of adding, overwriting any earlier wheel event in the same frame while still consuming and deleting every wheel event; if the cursor is over a GUI element (`[0x23494f0] + 0x130`) whose `+0x8a` flag is 0, or if camera zoom is locked (`+0x759`), it zeroes the wheel delta.
- **Vanilla desktop zoom (`0x109ec30`)**: called from `0x109bee0` at `0x109bf79` (`rcx` camera, `xmm1` wheel notches as f32, `r8` screen cursor `[i32; 2]`).
  - When `[camera + 0x764]` (a countdown timer decremented by `dt` in `0x109bee0`) is 0, it raycasts the cursor through `0x10a3e50` and stores `(hit.x, min_height, hit.z - camera_tilt * 0.3)` at `+0x768..+0x770`, then resets `+0x764` to 0.3 s on every tick.
  - Its step size is linear in world units and scaled by current smoothing lag: `clamp(|pos.y - goal.y| / 50, 1, 5) * zoom_speed`. Zooming out (`notches < 0`) moves vertically along `(0, +step, 0)` (clamped to `1..=1000`); zooming in (`notches > 0`) with `centered_zoom_in` moves along the 3D line toward `+0x768`, otherwise vertically. It writes `goal_look_at.y = 19.0` and calls `0x109d0e0`.
- **Touch zoom (`0x109dad0` → `0x109d8b0` → `0x109d540`)**: used only when `[0x233ff4f] != 0` (`0x109c9e0`). Maps height quadratically (`0x109db50`), casts a ray from `camera + 0x30` via `0x1581ff0` using the viewport at `[[0x23494b8] + 0x488]` (`0x109d939`, `0x109d95e`), intersects with a horizontal plane (normal `(0, 1, 0)` at `0x1fd70f0`), and calls `0x109d540` (`rcx` camera, `rdx` world point `[f32; 3]`, `r8` cursor `[f32; 2]`, `xmm3` target height, stack `u8` immediate) to keep that map point under the cursor.
- **Gliding (`0x109f6d0`)**: called at the end of `0x109bee0` (`rcx` camera, `xmm1` dt). While `|goal_pos - pos|² > 0.02` or `|goal_look - look|² > 0.02`, it advances both by `alpha = clamp((100 - camera_speed) * speed_mult * 0.13 * dt, 0, 1)` where `speed_mult` is at `+0x774` (1.0 in-game; `0x10d2e80` lowers it to `0x233e74c` on main-menu flyover pages `0, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7` and ramps it back toward `1.0` at `+0.1/s` on lobby pages `1, 3`), clamps via `0x109d0e0`, updates the view (`+0x30`) via `0x1581930`, applies north/south edge clamping (`0x109dc30`), rebuilds the ground outline (`0x109ded0`) and wrap copy (`0x109e040`), and sets the moving flag `+0x6f4 = 1` (else `0`).
- **Framework replacement (`core/src/tweaks/camera_zoom.rs`)**: patches `0x109e7bb` to `add [r14], eax` (`41 01 06`), resets `0x23349e8` on wheel direction reversal in `render::window_input`, replaces `0x109ec30` with a multiplicative height step (configurable via `zoom_step`) anchored to the map point under the cursor for both zoom-in and zoom-out via `0x1581ff0` and `0x109d540` (when `centered_zoom_in` is on, pre-clamping the goal's north/south frustum bounds on a stack copy of `camera + 0x30` via `0x109d330` and `0x1581930` to match `0x109dc30` without pole overshoot, and restoring `+0x774` to `1.0` if the lobby ramp is still in progress), and wraps `0x109f6d0` for frame-rate-independent exponential smoothing (`1 - exp(-k * dt)`) controlled by `zoom_smoothing` while a wheel zoom is active (invalidating the cached cursor anchor if `0x109dc30` edge-clamping or `0x109e040` horizontal wrapping shifts the goal laterally).
