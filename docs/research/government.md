# Government: ideas, policies, reforms, rank, estates, parliament, decisions, missions

Baseline 1.37.5. All addresses are RVAs. Read from `eu4.exe` on 2026-11-05 with `cargo xtask disasm` and scripts over the PE file (the command vtables, the token table), and from the running game's memory read from outside (a 1592 save, the Netherlands); the commands' written forms were then seen in a developer build's command log as the AI posted them. What was tried in the game through the capabilities is in [Player actions A7](../prototype-log/2026-11-05-player-actions-a7-government.md). What is marked _inferred_ was concluded from the shape of the code. Where the conditions are kept and how the game writes them is [triggers.md](triggers.md); the country's fields are [countries.md](countries.md).

## The commands

Found by scanning `.rdata` for vtables with the command layout ([commands.md](commands.md)) and naming each by the token its `+0x50` answers; each command's `+0x10` writes it, `+0x40` is its check and `+0x48` its execute. The forms below are those the AI's own commands were logged with (the stamps the session adds left out):

| What the player does | Command (vtable) | Written form |
|---|---|---|
| Take an idea group | `add_idea_group` (`0x1c556b8`) | `add_idea_group={ country="JMB" idea_group="defensive_ideas" }` |
| Abandon an idea group | `abandon_idea_group` (`0x1c55610`) | the same keys |
| Buy an idea | `spend_power_command` (`0x1c554c0`) | `spend_power_command={ country="PAL" power={ data=89 category=0 skill=2 } }`: kind 0 (`buy_idea`), `data` the idea's index in the idea database's ideas, left out when 0, `skill` the group's category |
| Enable, disable a policy | `enable_unlockable` (`0x1c66ac8`), `disable_unlockable` (`0x1c66b70`) | `enable_unlockable={ policy="global_trade_networks_act" country="JOL" }` |
| Enact a reform in the next tier | `select_government_reform` (`0x1c38a08`) | `select_government_reform={ members={ dummy="MGD" dummy="the_social_contract_reform" } }` |
| Change a reached tier's reform | `change_to_government_reform` (`0x1c38ab0`) | `members={ dummy=<tag> dummy=<reform> dummy=<int> dummy=<yes/no> }`, from its writer `0xc6e40`; not seen in the log |
| Raise the rank | `government_rank_command` (`0x1c53918`) | `government_rank_command={ country=<tag> value=<rank> prestige=yes }`, from its writer `0x2bace0` and the button's post (`0xf383ed`: the rank plus 1, prestige set) |
| Grant, revoke a privilege | `grant_privilege` (`0x1c38d50`), `revoke_privilege` (`0x1c38df8`) | `grant_privilege={ members={ dummy="VIJ" dummy=3 dummy=45 } }`: the estate's index in the estates' database, the privilege's in the privileges' |
| An interaction over crown land | `crown_land_interaction` (`0x1c38ea0`) | `crown_land_interaction={ members={ dummy="MAJ" dummy=2 } }`: the interaction's index |
| Start an estate's agenda | `create_new_estate_agenda` (`0x1c38f48`) | `members={ dummy=<tag> dummy=<estate> dummy=<agenda> }` (writer `0xc72d0`); not used: no button posts it (below, "Estate agendas") |
| Start a debate | `start_debate_command` (`0x1c56628`) | `start_debate_command={ country="GBR" which="restore_integration" }` |
| Give a province a seat, take it away | `add_to_parliament_command` (`0x1c55ca0`), `remove_from_parliament_command` (`0x1c55e98`) | `add_to_parliament_command={ country="C07" province=942 }` |
| Pay a seat's bribe | `bribe` | `bribe={ country="C07" province=913 }` (below, "Parliament") |
| Take a decision | `execute_decision_command` (`0x1c66c18`) | `execute_decision_command={ decision="enforce_religion_muslim" scope={ country="BNE" scope_is_valid=yes seed=343576198 random=343576198 } }` |
| Complete a mission | `complete_mission` (`0x1c3a100`) | `complete_mission={ members={ dummy="ASH" dummy="west_africa_embracing_old_traditions" } }` |

- **`members`** is how the game writes a command whose data is a tuple (`0xc15c0` and its kind): a wrapper object (vtable `0x1c384d0` and others, one per tuple type) over the command's `+0x50`, whose `+0x10` writes each element under the token `dummy` (`0xe6`), in the tuple's order: a tag as a string (`0x1707d90`), a `std::string`, an i32 (`0xcbff0`, `%d`), a bool (`0x42c5e0`). The elements lie in memory last first (`0xc8440`: the tag at `+8`, then `+4`, then `+0`).
- **A government mechanic's interaction**: `government_interaction_command={ country="PER" parent=feudal_theocracy_mechanic index=invite_minorities }` (below, "Government mechanics").
- **Other commands** of the government's, not used: `reset_debate_command`, `set_native_policy`, `select_trading_policy`, `enact_decree`, `ignore_decision_command`.

## Ideas

- **The database** (`[0x242bce0]`, made by its getter `0x850750`): groups at `+0x20`–`+0x28`, ideas at `+0x38`–`+0x40`, `std::vector`s of pointers.
- **An idea group** (vtable `0x1cbaad8`, constructor `0x84e4f0`, read by `0x850fb0`): `+0x10` its key; `+0x30` its ideas, a `std::vector` of pointers in order (`0x332723`); `+0x48` `trigger`, the conditions for taking it (`0x851489`); `+0xc0` `can_buy_idea` (`0x851580`); `+0x568` `free`, set for national ideas; `+0x570` its category, 0 `ADM`, 1 `DIP`, 2 `MIL` (`0x851cdf`); `+0x5b0` `important`; `+0x5b4` its index. Its vtable's `+0x10` (`0x84f1c0`, `(group, tag) -> bool`) evaluates `trigger`; `0x84f750` evaluates `can_buy_idea` the same way. An idea (class `0x1cbaa30`) has its key at `+0x158` (`0x84e65c`; its `+0x10` is an empty string) and its group at `+0x198`.
- **The country's**: `+0x1438` the groups taken (countries.md); `+0x10a0` a byte for each group by its index, set when taken (`0x2a7eb6`). The next idea of a group is its ideas at the count bought (`0x3326e0` says a country has an idea when it is among the first count of its group's).
- **Taking a group** (`add_idea_group`'s check `0x2a7e70`): the group is not free, its `trigger` holds, it is not taken, and a slot is free (`0x32e2d0`). The interface's tooltip (`0x139d080`, `IDEA_GROUP_CANNOT_TAKE`) writes `can_buy_idea`'s lines.
- **Buying an idea** (the spend command's check `0x94f250(spending, tag)`, kind 0): enough monarch points of the spending's skill (`+0x260` + skill × 4) for the cost (`0x9526d0`), the idea not had, the group not free, `can_buy_idea` (`0x84f750`). The framework builds a spending in its own memory as `0x94f3b8` does on its stack (vtable `0x1c55480`, `+8` `0x165`, kind, skill, data, second −1, `+0x28` from `[0x233fea8]`) and asks its cost and check.

## Policies

- **The check** `0x958e10(country + 0x140, policy, reasons)` (triggers.md) writes into a `std::string` the conditions of `allow`, all of them with their marks, and its own lines (`ALREADY_HAS_POLICY`, `MAX_POLICIES_ALREADY`); the framework keeps the lines that do not hold (`conditions::refusal`).
- **The category** at policy `+0x178` is what the policies view's walk compares (`0x959882`); the active policies are three arrays at country `+0x150`, `+0x180`, `+0x1b0` (countries.md), whose items keep the policy at `+0x10`.

## Reforms

- **The government type's tiers** (`0x798030`, which finds a reform's tier by its key): a game array at type `+0x60` (data `+0x68`, count `+0x74`) of tiers, each with its key at `+0x10` and its reforms, a game array at `+0x30` (data `+0x38`, count `+0x44`). The 1592 Netherlands' monarchy has 11 tiers, from `feudalism_vs_autocracy` to `separation_of_power`.
- **Enacting in the next tier** (`select_government_reform`'s check `0x40a9d0(country, key)`): the reform found by key (`0x70f9b0`, which finds by key in the reforms' registry and the other databases alike), not a mechanic (`+0x58`), its `potential` (`+0x1b0`) and `trigger` (`+0x228`) hold, it belongs to the tier after those reached, and the reform progress (country `+0x9a68`, thousandths) is at least the tier's cost, `0x79ead0(government, out, tier)`.
- **Changing a reached tier's reform** (`change_to_government_reform`'s check `0x40ade0(country, key, free)`): the same conditions, the reform's tier among those reached, and, unless `free`, the reform progress at least the define at `0x233e4cc`. The government view posts it from two places (`0x105cec0` and after), with `free` no and yes. Its execute (`0x2c0800`) hands the i32 member to `0x7afa60(stack, place, reform, tag, …)`, which puts the reform in the tier with that index, the mechanic before the tiers not counted: tried in the game, the member 1 replaced the second tier's reform (`hereditary_vs_nobility`), the stack's third.
- **The reform progress** is written as `government_reform_progress` (`0x3820`, `0x2e10e2`).

## Rank

- **The check** `0x3167b0(country, reasons) -> bool` writes the game's reasons into a `std::string`: `CANNOT_UPGRADE_RANK_JUNIOR_PARTNER`, `_SUBJECT`, `_HRE_ELECTOR`, `_HRE`, `_NEED_DEVELOPMENT`, `_NEED_PRESTIGE`, `_MAX_RANK`, `_FIXED_RANK`. It answers false with no reason where the next rank is not one the country may have (`0x317620`).
- **The command's check** (`0x2bafd0`) only asks that the rank be between 1 and the highest (`[0x233e35c]`); its execute (`0x2bac00`) sets the rank (`0x317110`) and, with `prestige`, gives the prestige the defines `0x233e4fc`, `0x233e4f8`, `0x233e4f4` name for ranks 2, 3 and above. Raising the rank costs nothing else.

## Estates

- **An estate** (the database's entries, vtable `0x1cad508`, read by `0x7233f0`): `+0x10` its key; `+0x520` its privileges, a game array of pointers into the privileges' database (data `+0x528`, count `+0x534`), read from its `privileges` (`0x72378c`); `+0x538` its agendas, the same from `agendas`.
- **A privilege** (the database's 0x888-byte entries): `+0x18` its key, `+0x38` its icon's name, `+0x498` `can_select`, `+0x588` `is_valid` (triggers.md). The estates view lists the privileges whose `is_valid` holds, and those granted.
- **Granting** (`grant_privilege`'s check `0x2c16e0`): the estate by its index (`0x716c30`), the privilege by its index, then `0x71edf0(entry, &tag, privilege, reasons)`, which writes `ESTATE_PRIVILEGE_ALREADY_GRANTED`, `CANNOT_AFFORD_ESTATE_PRIVILEGE`, crown land and `can_select`. **Revoking** (`0x2c1c40`): `0x71f430(entry, tag, privilege, flag, reasons)`, the tag by value, the flag 0 as the command's check passes it.
- **Influence** is `0x71a520(entry, out, modifiers)`, thousandths, with the country's modifiers `0x93d750(country + 0x28c0) + 8`, as the influence tooltip `0x718190` asks it (clamped to 0–100 there).
- **Estate agendas** (read on 2026-11-11 for M20 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md); the run is [estate agendas and parliament bribes](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-agendas-and-bribes.md)):
  - **How one starts.** No button of the estates view asks an estate for an agenda, completes one or dismisses one. In 1.37.5 an agenda starts from the game's event `estate_privileges_and_agendas_events.3`, whose options each run the effect `start_estate_agenda = <estate>` (`0x6cccf0`): it picks one of the estate's agendas (`0x6ccfa0`, by their `selection_weight` among those whose `can_select` holds; the effect's `random`) and runs `create_new_estate_agenda`'s execute (`0x2c2010`) itself, without posting the command. The game completes an agenda once its `task_requirements` hold and fails it when it expires or its `fail_if` holds (the texts `COMPLETED_ESTATE_AGENDA_DESC`, `TIMED_OUT_ESTATE_AGENDA_DESC`, `FAILED_ESTATE_AGENDA_DESC`); where it checks was not followed.
  - **The command** (`create_new_estate_agenda`, token `0x3983`): its members are the tag (`+0x90`), the estate's key (`+0x70`, found by `0x716c90` in the estates' database) and the agenda's (`+0x50`, found by key in the agendas' map, below). Its check (`0x2c22d0`) refuses a country with an agenda in progress (`+0x21c8`) and evaluates the agenda's `can_select` (`+0xb0`, its conditions at `+0xb8`, count `+0xc4`), each with its vtable's `+0x30`.
  - **The agendas' database** (`common/estate_agendas`): the object `0x725fa0()` answers, read by `0x7260c0`; a map by key, FNV-1a, with its buckets at `+0x30`, the mask `+0x48` and the end `+0x20`, each node's agenda at `+0x30`. An agenda (0x560 bytes, vtable `0x1cad6a0`, made by `0x725030`, read by its vtable's `+0x20`, `0x725440`): its token `+0x10`, its key `+0x18`; five blocks of conditions of 0x78 bytes, each read at the block's `+0x38`: `task_requirements` `+0x38`, `can_select` `+0xb0`, `fail_if` `+0x128`, `provinces_to_highlight` `+0x1a0`, `invalid_trigger` `+0x218`; `selection_weight` `+0x290`; its effects, 0x38 bytes each, `immediate_effect` `+0x2c8`, `failing_effect` `+0x300`, `task_completed_effect` `+0x338`, `pre_effect` `+0x370`, `on_invalid` `+0x3a8`; `modifier` `+0x3e0`; `max_days_active` `+0x558`.
  - **The agenda in progress** (country `+0x21c8`, null for none, written as `active_agenda` by `0x4205e0`): `+0x0` the agenda (`agenda`, its key), `+0x8` the day it expires (`expiry_date`), `+0x10` the estate (`estate`, its key at `+0x10`), `+0x18` its own scope (`scope`), which keeps what its `pre_effect` picked, such as `agenda_province`. The framework evaluates `task_requirements` in a copy of that scope (`0xcf6a0`), as the imperial incidents' options are.
- **The interactions over crown land** (`common/estate_crown_land`, `sale_of_titles`, `summon_the_diet`, `seize_land`): an object answered by `0x728430()` whose game array at `+0x58` holds 0x128-byte interactions (`0xc50ac`), each with its key, an index at `+0x10`, its `trigger` at `+0x38`; the check `0x4128b0(country, interaction, reasons)` writes the cooldown (`CROWN_INTERACTIONS_COOLDOWN`) and `trigger`'s lines.

## Parliament

- **The issues' database** is an object at the fixed place `0x2329390` (its getter `0x9688e0`): a game array of 0xe8-byte issues (data `+8`, count `+0x14`; 108 in the unmodded game), each with its key at `+0x10` and a pointer to its `allow`, an `and` block (vtable `0x1cc08d8`), at `+0x48`; a map by key at `+0x18` (`0x9694d0`, FNV-1a).
- **The parliament** (country `+0x24c8`): the issues on offer, a game array of i32 indices into the database at `+0x20` (11 for Great Britain in 1592), the debate at `+0x50`, the issue enacted at `+0x58`.
- **Debating** (`start_debate_command`'s check `0x2a4c00`): the country has a parliament and no debate going on; the issue is read by key (`0x2a4a45`).
- **The seats** (read on 2026-11-11 for M20): the parliament's seats are a game array of pointers at its `+0x38` (data `+0x40`, count `+0x4c`); a province keeps its own seat at `+0x518`. A seat: `+0x10` its province, `+0x18` the bribe it asks for its backing, null for none, `+0x20` whether it backs the issue debated (set by `0x966ea0`, which logs `SetSupportingCurrentIssue`). The debate counts the seats backing it at `+0x1c`.
- **The bribes' database** (`common/parliament_bribes`): an object at the fixed place `0x23293f0` (its getter `0x969570`, read by `0x969630`), a game array of 0xd0-byte bribes (data `+8`, count `+0x14`; made by `0x96a1f0`): its key at `+0x10`, again at `+0x30`, two weights at `+0x50` and `+0x88`, and at `+0xc8` a 0xb0-byte block holding its `trigger` (conditions, at `+0`) and its `effect` (at `+0x78`). A bribe's cost is what its effect does (`adm_power_cost`, scaled by an estate's loyalty), so it has no amount the framework reads.
- **Paying a bribe** (`bribe`, token `0x31d0`, vtable `0x1c55df0`; writer `0x271330`): the check (`0x2a4880`) asks only that the province (`+0x58`) has a seat that asks a bribe and does not back the issue. The execute (`0x2a4250`) sets the seat backing (`0x966ea0`), runs the bribe's effect in the country's scope (its vtable's `+0x78`), counts the seats backing into the debate's `+0x1c` and, once they times 150 over the seats (at least 1) reach 100, two thirds, passes the debate (`0x963020`). The framework also refuses a bribe while no debate is going on, and gives the bribe's `trigger` lines that do not hold, which the command's check does not ask: whether the view greys the button for them was not seen.

## Decisions and missions

- **Taking a decision**: the view's button (`0x1063660`) calls `0x488050(database, decision, scope)` (triggers.md). The scope written carries `seed` and `random`.
- **The country's missions** (`country_missions`, `0x3809`, inline at country `+0x99d8`, vtable `0x1c573c8`, writer `0x445540`): a game array of columns at `+0x10` (data `+0x18`, 0x18-byte items, count `+0x24`), each a game array of mission series (class `0x1cc2188`), written as `mission_slot`; a series keeps its missions (class `0x1cc21c8`) in a game array at `+0x120` (`0x8d78a9`). The 1592 Netherlands had five columns of two series, 64 missions. Whether a mission is completed is `0x1796c0(country, mission)`, and whether it can be completed `0x8d4fc0(mission, tag)` (triggers.md); the missions before one are checked by `0x8d4e50`, over a list whose layout was not worked out.

## Government mechanics

Read on 2026-11-11, from the code and from a new game as the Mamluks in memory, for the government group's `mechanics` and `interact` ([mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md), M8).

- **The mechanics a government has**: a `std::vector` of state objects at government `+0x20` (`0x2bfdad` `48 8b 88 20 14 00 00 4c 8b 41 28 48 8b 51 20`), one for each mechanic its reforms' `government_abilities` give; a state keeps its definition at `+0x10`, its country at `+0x18`, and its powers' values in a hash map from `+0x20` (slots of 0x100 bytes at `+0x28`, mask `+0x34`, the longest probe `+0x38`; read by `0x7bc0c0`). The Mamluks' 1444 government had one, `mamluk_mechanic`.
- **A mechanic's definition** (`common/government_mechanics`; its reader is `0x7b7430`): its token at `+8` (what the command writes as `parent`), its key at `+0x10`, its powers at `+0xc0` and its interactions at `+0xd8`, each the game's array of pointers (`0x7bbab0`, `0x2bfddb`).
- **A power** (vtable `0x1cb3130`, read by `0x7b3eb0`): token `+8`, key `+0x10` (`0x7b79b9`), `gui` `+0x30`, `monarch_power` `+0x50`, `default`, `min` and `max` at `+0x54`, `+0x58`, `+0x5c` in thousandths, `base_monthly_growth` `+0x60`, `scaled_modifier` (with `range_modifier`, marked) at `+0x70` and `reverse_scaled_modifier` at `+0x88`, the game's arrays of 0x280-byte entries; `on_max_reached` `+0xa0`, `on_min_reached` `+0xd8`, `reset_on_new_ruler` `+0x110`, `is_good` `+0x111`.
- **A power's value**: `0x7bc0c0(state, token)` answers the state's entry of the power, null for none; its value in thousandths is at `+0x20` (`0x7b6f9c` `8b 58 20`).
- **A scaled modifier's entry**: its conditions are a content trigger at its start (data `+8`, count `+0x14`), evaluated as every other ([triggers.md](triggers.md)); its modifiers a set at `+0x78` whose (modifier, thousandths) pairs are at `+0xd0`, count `+0xdc`, the modifier 0x286 meaning none; `start_value` `+0x270`, `end_value` `+0x274` (thousandths), `extend_beyond_value` `+0x278`, a range modifier's mark `+0x279`.
- **What a power adds to the modifiers** (`0x7b9b50(out, power, state, &value)`, called when the country's modifiers are made again): for each entry whose conditions hold, a share in thousandths. A range modifier: all within `[start, end]`, none outside. A scaled one: `(value − start) × 1000 / (end − start)` (all or none at `start = end` by whether the value is `end`), none below 0, above 1000 all when it extends and none otherwise. A reverse scaled one: 1000 less that share, the share taken as 1000 above 1000 and, below 0, as 0 when it extends and 1000 otherwise. Each modifier's value times the share, divided by 1000 toward zero, is added (`0x8ea1e0`). In 1444 the Omani `arabic_trade_influence_power` at 1.4 of 100 gave 1.4% of each of its entries' modifiers.
- **A modifier's key**: the modifier types are a `std::vector` of 0x60-byte records at `[0x2358080]` by the modifier's index (`0x8f09ea` `48 8d 0c 5b 48 c1 e1 05 48 03 0d …`), each starting with the key, a `std::string`, taken from the token's name when the record is made (`0x8f0810`): `0` is `can_not_build_colonies`, the generated `monthly_<power>` are among them.
- **An interaction** (vtable `0x1cb3188`, made by `0x7b5900`, read by `0x7b5b60`): token `+8` (what the command writes as `index`), its mechanic's key `+0x10`, its key `+0x30` (`0x7b599f`), `icon` `+0x50`, `gui` `+0x70`, `cost_type` (a power's token, 0 for none) `+0x94`, `cost` `+0x98` thousandths, `trigger` `+0xa0`, `potential` `+0x108`, `effect` `+0x170`, `cooldown_years` `+0x1a8`, `cooldown_token` `+0x1ac` (0: its own token), `cooldown_desc` `+0x1b0`, `ai_chance` `+0x1d0`. The Mamluks' three each cost 100 of one of their three powers and cool down a year.
- **Whether it may be used** `0x7b6ab0(interaction, state, reasons or null) -> bool`: no, without a reason, when its `potential` fails; then its cost (`0x7b6f40`: the power's value below the cost writes `NEED_VALUE`); its cooldown, a `std::vector` of (token, date) pairs at country `+0xeb8`, the date after today writing `COOLDOWN_UNTIL`; its `trigger`, whose failing lines it writes. The command's check (`0x2bfd80`) finds the state and the interaction by their tokens and calls it with no reasons; the execute (`0x2bfa70`) runs `0x7bc160(state, interaction token)`.
- **Names**: `0x7b5df0(interaction, out, country) -> out` and `0x7b4b00(power, out, country) -> out`, the government view's; a mechanic's own name is the text of `ability_` and its key (`0x7a4a29` builds the key from the token's name), "Mamlukian Culture" for `ability_mamluk_mechanic`.
- **The command**, as the AI posts it (seen in the command log, 2026-11-11, 47 of them in five months): `government_interaction_command={ country="ADE" parent=iqta_mechanic index=land_acquisition }`.
- **Tried** ([the run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-mechanics-and-advisors.md)): as the Mamluks, the three interactions were refused with the game's `NEED_VALUE` line (and the trigger's failing line) while their powers were near 0; as Adal, `efficient_tax_farming` was carried out, and all three iqta interactions, sharing the token `iqta_mechanic`, were then refused until 1464.11.11 with `COOLDOWN_UNTIL`; as Oman, the trade influence power at 0.2 gave `placed_merchant_power` 0.03, the 0.2% of 15 the formula above gives.

- An idea group's slots' text in the interface; the framework says that in its own words.
- The debate object's layout; the framework takes its `+0x10` for the issue only when it points at one of the database's.
- The key's place in a crown land interaction (`+0x18` by the reader `0x72835a`, whose object may not be the interaction).
- Where the game completes and fails an agenda, and in which scope it asks `task_requirements` there; the framework asks them in the agenda's own scope.
- Whether the parliament view refuses a bribe whose `trigger` does not hold, and its texts for a seat that asks none or backs the issue already; the framework says those in its own words.
- Which of a mechanic's powers and interactions the government view shows, beyond the interactions' `potential`; the framework lists every power.
