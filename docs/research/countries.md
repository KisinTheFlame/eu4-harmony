# Countries (for `game::countries`)

Baseline 1.37.5. All addresses are RVAs. Found by reading eu4.exe on 2026-10-29; what was checked in the running game is marked "tested".

## The country array

- **The country manager** is at the global `0x233d8d0`. Its countries are an array object at `+0x110`: the data, one pointer per country indexed by the tag's index (bits 32–47 of the tag), at `+0x118`; the length, an i32, at `+0x124`. In arrays of this type `+0x10` is the capacity (below), so `+0x120` presumably is; no code reads it through the manager, and the framework does not use it.
  - Indexing, at almost every place that turns a tag into its country, for example `0xb239c` (`4c 8b 05 … 48 8b 41 20 48 c1 e8 20 48 0f bf d0 49 8b 80 18 01 00 00 48 8b 1c d0`): load the manager, shift the tag right by 32, sign-extend the 16-bit index, read `+0x118`, read the pointer at index × 8.
  - The length: when the manager is made (`0xb510c6`, after `new` and the constructor `0x2812a0`), `lea rdx, [rax+0x110]` hands the array to `0xb510f0`, which reads its length at `+0x14` and its data at `+8` (`0xb5113f` `41 8b 46 14 4d 8b 76 08 48 98 49 8d 2c c6`) and walks every pointer, reading each country's tag at `+0x20` (`0xb5117c` `48 8b 5b 20`). The array it fills, in its first argument at `+0x10`, has the same layout: data `+8`, capacity `+0x10` (doubled when the length would pass it, `0xb51176`–`0xb51188`), length `+0x14`.
- **A country's tag** is at `+0x20` of the country (`country::TAG`); its index is the country's place in the array. The framework finds a country by its letters by walking the array to its length and comparing the letters of each valid tag, checking that each tag's index is its place (`game::world::countries`).

What the framework relies on is in `objects::campaign::countries` and the dependency points "country array" (`0xb510c6`) and "country array walk" (`0xb5113f`).

## A country's snapshot (`countries::snapshot`)

How it was found: the game writes a country into a save in `0x2dc6b0` (its argument in `r15`), one field after another, each followed by the token of its key in the binary save format (`mov edx, <token>`). The tokens' names are in a table in `.data`: an u32 token, then its key as text, for example `0x2876` and `treasury` at `0x207b068`. Read together, the writer gives the field of each key. Each field below was then checked against code of the game's own that reads it.

- **Numbers**: the game keeps its numbers as i32 thousandths (the ledger's getter for the number of cities, `0x6e01b0`, multiplies a count by 1000 to make one). In the writer:
  - `prestige` (token `0x29a1`) at `+0xcf8`, `stability` (`0x2886`) at `+0xbd8`, `treasury` (`0x2876`) at `+0xbb0`: `0x2df116`–`0x2df177`, each `lea rdx, [r15+field]`, the formatter `0x956f0` (`mov eax, [rdx]`, four bytes), then the token.
  - `manpower` (`0x283f`) at `+0xdb8` and `max_manpower` (`0x2973`) at `+0xdbc`: `0x2e00a9`–`0x2e00e7`. Manpower is in thousands of men, so its thousandths are men.
  - The ledger's table of getters (`0x6a7af0`, a name and a `std::function` each) reads the same fields: `treasury` `0x6e02c0` (`mov eax, [r8+0xbb0]`), `stability` `0x6e02f0` (`+0xbd8`), `prestige` `0x6e05a0` (`+0xcf8`), `manpower` `0x6e0540` (`+0xdb8`), `max_manpower` `0x6e0490` (`+0xdbc`).
- **Monarch points**: three i32 at `+0x260`, written as `powers` (`0x2ab2`) by the int-array writer `0x191900` (three values, `lea rdi, [r8+0xc]`) at `0x2e0b23`. The order is administrative, diplomatic, military: the tooltips of the triggers on each kind read `+0x260` (`0xcb41b5`, the function that names `HAVE_MORE_THAN_ADM_POWER`), `+0x264` (`0xcb4c23`, `HAVE_MORE_THAN_DIP_POWER`) and `+0x268` (`0xcb5683`, `HAVE_MORE_THAN_MIL_POWER`). The `adm_power` trigger itself (`0xcb3d70`, slot 6 of its vtable `0x1d04c60`) compares `+0x260` with its value. They are whole numbers, not thousandths: `0xab44a` multiplies one by 1000 to make a fixed-point number.
- **Overlord**: a tag at `+0x18f0`, written as `overlord` (`0x2cb6`) only when it is valid (`0x2ddee6`, `0x985c0` tests the valid byte).
- **Allies**: a list of the game's kind (`objects::list`) at `+0x19b8` whose items are the allies' tags themselves, written as `allies` (`0x2e9b`) by `0x4210e0`, which walks the list from its head (`+0`) through each node's next (`+0x10`), reading the tag in the node (`+0`), and skips the list when its count (`+0x10`) is 0. The AI walks it the same way at `0x3a37a7`; dozens of other functions read it, so it is the game's live state, not a copy made for saves.
- **Subjects**: a vector of tags (begin `+0x1a18`, end `+0x1a20`), written as `subjects` (`0x2edb`) by `0x420fc0`, which walks a vector of 8-byte tags. Clearing a country's diplomacy (`0x2e2ca7`) sets its end to its begin.
- **Subject types** (found 2026-10-30): which kind of subject a subject is (a vassal, a personal union, a tributary, an appanage) is not in the country: it is the subject relation's, a `dependency` in saves. The kinds are defined by `common/subject_types` (26 in the unmodded game), and a mod can add more, so the framework names one by its key there.
  - **The getter** `0x3d79f0`(country) answers the subject type the country is to its overlord, or null; a trigger's evaluation (`0xcfc4d0`, whose tooltip names `IS_SUBJECT_TYPE` at `0xcfcac2`, presumably `is_subject_of_type`'s) compares its result with the trigger's type, and the game walks an overlord's subjects (`+0x1a18`) calling it on each (`0x4e5ea4`–`0x4e5eea`). With no valid overlord (the tag's valid byte, `+0x18f7`) it answers null. Otherwise it takes the country's record of its overlord, in the table of relations at `[+0x1510]` (0x48 bytes by the other's index, `lea r8, [rax+rax*8]`); the record's first pointer leads to a country's diplomacy object, and its `+0x10` is the other's tag. In that diplomacy object, an array of the subject relations it takes part in: data at `+0x128`, 16-byte items, each the other country's index (i32) and the relation at `+8`, the count (i32) at `+0x134`. The item whose index is the overlord's holds the relation, and the relation's `+0x40` is the subject type.
  - **Where that diplomacy object is**: a country's diplomacy is at `+0x14f8` of the country (`countrydiplomacy.cpp`): adding a subject relation (`0xa5ff20`) hands `country + 0x14f8` of each side, with the other's tag, to `0x28bd00`, which looks for the other's index in the same array (`0x28be8c`, `+0x128`, `+0x134`) before adding to it (`+0x120`, an array of the kind the country manager holds) and marks the record of both (`[+0x18]` of the diplomacy object is the table at `+0x1510`). The framework follows the getter's own path and does not rely on this.
  - **A subject relation** is of class `0x1c81758` and 0x90 bytes; the game makes one when it reads a `dependency` (token `0x35c2`) at `0x4e5ced`, setting its class and its subject type (`+0x40`, `0x4e5d4b`). Its base, which every diplomatic relation shares, writes `first` (the overlord, `+0x10`), `second` (the subject, `+0x18`) and its dates (`0xa5c020`); its own writer (`0xa62b40`, its vtable's `+0x10`) writes `subject_type` (token `0x352a`) from the subject type's key, a `std::string` at `+0x10` of the subject type (`0xa62b6c`).
  - **Tested** ([2026-10-30](../prototype-log/2026-10-30-subject-types-country-snapshot.md)): every subject relation in a 1470 save, 84 of six kinds, read through `country.get`; three of the kinds checked against the diplomacy page's tooltips.
- **Rivals**: a chain of nodes from `+0x2248`, each one a persistent object of its own (written as `rival`, `0x2cf7`, through its vtable): the rival's tag at `+0x10`, the next node at `+0x28`. The `is_rival` trigger (`0xc84590`, slot 6 of its vtable `0x1cf7d10`) walks it (`0xc848d6`) comparing the tags' indices with its target's.
- Lists written beside these, which the framework does not read: `extended_allies` `+0x19d0`, `guarantees` `+0x1a00`, `support_independence` `+0x19a0`, `coalition_friends` `+0x19e8`, `warnings` `+0x1b70`, `current_at_war_with` `+0x1b88`, `current_war_allies` `+0x1bc0`, `trade_embargoed_by` `+0x1bd8` (lists); `call_to_arms_friends` `+0x1ad8`, `condottieri_client` `+0x1a30` (vectors); `enemy` (`0x2aea`) entries from `+0x2218`.
- **Tested** ([2026-10-29, remote control C2](../prototype-log/2026-10-29-country-snapshot.md)): Castile's treasury, manpower and its maximum, stability, prestige and monarch points as the top bar shows them, twice two months apart; its allies, subjects (personal unions and vassals both) and rival as its diplomacy page shows them; the Ottomans' stability, prestige, manpower, ally and rivals as their diplomacy panel shows them; an overlord on two of Castile's subjects.
- **The `alliance_with` trigger** (`0xca0d60`) goes through another table: the country's relations to every other country at `[+0x1510]`, 0x48 bytes each, indexed by the other's index (`lea rdx, [rax+rax*8]`), whose first pointer leads back to the country and whose `+0x10` is the other's tag. The framework reads it only on the subject type getter's path (above).

## Wars

- **The wars going on** are pointers in an array on the world (`[0x233fe78]`): data at `+0x1e30`, count (i32) at `+0x1e3c`. The save writer (`0x74ff30`) writes each as `active_war` (token `0x28f7`, `0x751883`), and the wars that ended, a list from `+0x1e40`, as `previous_war`. `0x291997` walks both, `0x75f540` finds a war by its name.
- **A war** is of class `0x1d1f768`, set by its constructors `0xd856b0` and `0xd859b0`. Its writer (`0xd88520`, slot 2 of the vtable) writes its name, a `std::string` at `+0x28` (the constructor sets its size `+0x38` to 0 and its capacity `+0x40` to 15), its participants (`+0xc0`, 0x90 bytes each), then `attackers` (`0x28f5`) and `defenders` (`0x28f6`), vectors of tags at `+0x48` and `+0x78`, with `0x420fc0`. `0xd894f0` compares a tag with the first attacker and the first defender before anything else: the first of each side is its leader. `+0x148` to `+0x158` hold three more tags (presumably the original attacker and defender), which the framework does not read. Tested: a war's name and both sides as the war panel shows them, the defenders' leader starred there; the panel lists the attackers after their leader in another order than the vector's.

## A coalition

- **The list**: a country's `+0x1928` lists the countries in a coalition against it, 0x20-byte nodes of a member's tag, the previous node, the next and a byte marking the member to leave; `+0x1930` is its last node and `+0x1938` its count (the constructor `0x2c9960` sets them, the destructor `0x2cbf20` frees the nodes). A member keeps the coalition's target at its `+0x1900`. The interface shows the list as the coalition's members (`0xe02260`, `COALITION_CURRENT_MEMBERS`).
- **Joining**: `0x3e3940(country)`, from the country's update (`0x2ee280`), sets the target at `+0x1900` and adds the country to the target's list. On the main thread only (2026-11-03, counted in the game).
- **Leaving**: `0x3b0220(country, …, other)`, the AI weighing what `other` (a tag in its third argument) means to it (from `0x58aa00`, the AI's evaluation `0x13afb0`, the country's AI tick `0xb0fa0`), takes members off the coalition against `other` and off the coalitions they are in, unlinking a node and freeing it (`0x3b0bac`, `0x3b0c77`), or, when the coalition's `+0x193c` is set, only marking it. It runs on the game's worker threads: in a session loaded from `法兰西1495_11_19`, 662 of its 705 calls; up to four at once, all on the coalition against France, 45 times in one session ([The AI leaving a coalition, and the heaps' free pages](../prototype-log/2026-11-03-ai-leaving-coalition-free-pages.md)). Nothing locks the list, and two threads unlinking at once corrupt it: the game's heap was found corrupted freeing a node whose address was not even aligned, read from a list the AI was taking members off.
- **The repair**: leaving and joining take turns on one lock (`repairs`), at their observers' entry and exit.

What the framework relies on is in `objects::campaign::{country, country_diplomacy, subject_relation, subject_type, war}`, `world::WARS`, `classes::WAR` and `classes::SUBJECT_RELATION`, and the dependency points named "country …", "… points", "subject type", "subject relation", "subject type key", "wars", "war" and "war leaders".

## Country fields for the player-actions reads

Read from `eu4.exe` on 2026-11-05 with `cargo xtask disasm` and scripts over the PE file (the token table, call sites, jump tables); what A1 reads of it was tried in the game on 2026-11-05 ([Player actions A1](../prototype-log/2026-11-05-player-actions-a1-reading.md)), and those fields are dependency points in `game::countries` (technology, ideas, governing capacity, inflation, mercantilism, loans, rebels, the budget tables and their names). As above, each field was found under its key in the country's save writer (`0x2dc6b0`, the country in `r15`) or in the writer of the object it is kept in, and the place named with it is an instruction that uses it, a candidate dependency point. What is marked _inferred_ was concluded from the shape of the code. Numbers are i32 thousandths unless said otherwise; dates are the game's i32 dates in hours ([dates.md](dates.md)).

**How the game writes a field that is an object of its own** (the technology, the ledger, an estate, a loan): through `0x17079f0(writer, token, object)`, which calls the object's vtable `+0x08` (`0xd2f410`), which calls its `+0x10`, the class's writer. So a class's layout comes from its vtable's `+0x10`.

### Technology

- **The technology object**: a pointer at country `+0x2440`, written as `technology` (`0x2862`; `0x2ddc0b` `4d 8b 87 40 24 00 00`). Class `0x1ce9d68`, 0x148 bytes, made by `0xb6b000(object, country)` in the country's constructor (`0x2cb718`); its writer `0xb6b4e0`.
- **The levels**: an i32 vector (begin `+0x128`, end `+0x130`) of whole levels, indexed by technology category. The writer walks the categories and writes each level under the category's own token (category `+0x54`, `0xb6b6c5`). The categories are a vector of pointers at `[0x242be50]+0x18` (end `+0x20`), each with its index at `+0x50` and its kind of monarch power at `+0x78` (0 administrative, 1 diplomatic, 2 military).
- **Which index is which**: when the categories are read (`0xb74120`), each one's kind (`0xb74349` `8b 4b 78`) puts its index into one of three globals: administrative `0x2340000` (`0xb74371`), diplomatic `0x233fffc` (`0xb7435e`), military `0x233fff8` (`0xb74351`). Code that wants one kind reads the global and indexes the vector with it, checked against the vector's length, for example the diplomatic level at `0x3c6143` (`48 8b 87 40 24 00 00 4c 8b 80 28 01 00 00 48 8b 88 30 01 00 00 49 2b c8 48 c1 f9 02 8b 05 97 9e f7 01 3b`). So a reader takes `[[country+0x2440]+0x128]` at index `[0x2340000]` and so on, and does not assume the order.
- **The cost of the next level**: `0xb6dc60(technology, out, category, level, text)` (five arguments; `out` an i32 of thousandths, which it returns). With `level` −1 it takes the current level from the vector (`0xb6dca5` `45 8b 48 50 48 8b 81 28 01 00 00 42 8b 1c 88`); at the last level it answers 0. `text`, when not null, receives the explanation (`TECH_EXPL_BASE_COST`, `TECH_EXPL_YEARS_AHEAD`). The technology view calls it with `[country+0x2440]`, −1, the category and a text (`0x10125e1`), and so does the monarch-point action that buys a level (`0x952e3d`, below). The result is monarch points times 1000. That `level` is the level the next one is reached from is _inferred_ from the AI's call (`0xab428`, which passes a level plus 1).
- **Two more values the technology object keeps**: `+0x18`, how many idea groups the country may have (below), and `+0x1c`, the governing capacity technology gives (`0x3f2e37`, the first thing the maximum below reads).

### Ideas

- **The idea groups taken**: a vector at country `+0x1438` (end `+0x1440`) of 16-byte items, an idea group pointer and an i32 count of ideas taken in it (`0xc9cb0` indexes it by 16, `0xcf470` reads `+8`). The writer writes `active_idea_groups` (`0x2e60`) as a block of `key=count` (`0x2dfd91`–`0x2dfdd6`), the key the group's token at group `+0x56c` (`0x43f280` `8b 81 6c 05 00 00`). Idea group names are not in the token table in the file; that the game adds a token for each group as it reads the groups is _inferred_.
- **The national ideas come first**: a group with the byte `+0x568` set is free, the country's national ideas. Whether a country may take another group is `0x32e2d0(country)` (`48 8b 81 38 14 00 00 48 8b 91 40 14 00 00`): it counts the groups taken, leaves out the first when it is free (`0x32e2ec` `80 b8 68 05 00 00 00`), and answers whether that is below **the slots the country has**, `[[country+0x2440]+0x18]` (`0x32e2f7` `48 8b 81 40 24 00 00 3b 50 18`). The `add_idea_group` command's check (`0x2a7e70`) calls it.
- **The cost of the next idea** is the power spending `buy_idea` (kind 0, below) with the idea as its first value (`+0x18`), an index into the idea database's vector of ideas (`+0x38`; each idea's group at `+0x198`, `0x94f2fb`–`0x94f306`). Which idea of a group is next was not followed.

### Governing capacity

- **Used**: country `+0xe2c`, written as `used_governing_capacity` (`0x3ab0`, `0x2dd183`), and read as is by the game (`0x9a205` `48 63 b1 2c 0e 00 00`).
- **The maximum**: `0x3f2e10(country, out, modifiers, text)` (four arguments), `modifiers` the country's modifiers (`0x93d750(country + 0x28c0)` plus 8, as every caller passes them) and `text` null or a text for the explanation. It starts from the technology's `+0x1c` and adds modifiers.
- **Used as a share of the maximum**: `0x9a1f0(country, out)`, used × 1000 / maximum, −1 when the maximum is 0; the governing capacity percentage trigger's tooltip (`0xbe47b0`) does the same.

### Money: the ledger, loans, inflation, mercantilism

- **Estimated monthly income** at `+0xe38` (`estimated_monthly_income`, `0x2a3a`); **inflation** at `+0xbd4` (`inflation`, `0x2888`; `0x2df1e1` `41 39 87 d4 0b 00 00`); **mercantilism** at `+0x1098` (`mercantilism`, `0x2bfd`; `0x2dfb51` `49 8d 97 98 10 00 00`). Both are thousandths of a percentage point (2.5 % is 2500), _inferred_ from how saves show them.
- **The ledger** is inline at country `+0x760` (`ledger`, `0x2875`; class `0x1ce42c0`, made by `0xaf0c50`, its writer `0xaf2f50`). Its tables, i32 thousandths of ducats, at these offsets in the ledger:
  - `+0x354` `income`, 19 entries, and `+0x3a0` `expense`, 38 entries: the month's figures. The economy view's budget list (presumably; the code at `0x11a9f57`, which also names the categories) reads income entry *i* at country `+0xab4 + 4i` (`0x11a9f76` `48 63 94 86 b4 0a 00 00`) and expense entry *i* at `+0xb00 + 4i` (`0x11ade66` `48 63 94 86 00 0b 00 00`). Their sums are `0xaf1390(ledger, out)` (`0xaf1399` `44 8b 81 54 03 00 00`) and `0xaf1490(ledger, out)`.
  - `+0x1d8` `lastyearincome` (19) and `+0x224` `lastyearexpense` (38), which the same list shows in its yearly mode (country `+0x938`); `+0x10` `lastmonthincometable` (19), `+0x5c` `lastmonthexpensetable` (38), and their totals `+0x440` `lastmonthincome` and `+0x444` `lastmonthexpense`; `+0xf8` `thismonthincome` (19), `+0x140` `thismonthexpense` (38); `+0x2bc` `totalexpensetable` (38).
  - **The order of the categories** is that of the names the game gives them, `0xaf28a0(out, index)` for income and `0xaf29f0(out, index)` for expenses (jump tables at `0xaf29a0` and `0xaf2bc8`). Income: 0 `INCOMETAX`, 1 `INCOMEPROD`, 2 `INCOMETRADE`, 3 `INCOMEGOLD`, 4 `INCOMETARRIFFS`, 5 `INCOMEVASSAL`, 6 `INCOMEHARBORFEES`, 7 `INCOMESUBS`, 8 `INCOME_WAR_REPARATION`, 9 `INCOMELOAN`, 10 `INCOMEGIFT`, 11 `INCOMEEVENT`, 12 `SPOILS_OF_WAR_ECOVIEW`, 13 `INCOME_TREASURE_FLEET`, 14 `SIPHON_INCOME`, 15 `CONDOTTIERI`, 16 `KNOWLEDGE_SHARING`, 17 `INC_BLOCKADES`, 18 `INC_LOOTING`. Expenses: 0 `EXPENSECOURTMAINTENANCE`, 1 `EXPENSEINTEREST`, 2 `EXPENSE_STATE_MAINTENANCE`, 3 `EXPENSEHARBORFEES`, 4 `EXPENSESUBS`, 5 `EXPENSE_WAR_REPARATIONS`, 6 `EXPENSEAM`, 7 `EXPENSEFM`, 8 `EXPENSEFORTM`, 9 `COLONIAL_MAINTENANCE`, 10 `EXPENSEMISSIONARY`, 11 `EXPENSEARMY`, 12 `EXPENSEFLEET`, 13 `EXPENSEFORT`, 14 `EXPENSEMAN`, 15 `EXPENSESUPPORTREBELS`, 16 `EXPENSELOAN`, 17 `EXPENSEGIFT`, 18 `EXPENSECOURT`, 19 `EXPENSEEVENTS`, 20 `EXPENSEPEACE`, 21 `EXPENSEVASSAL`, 22 `EXPENSETARIFFS`, 23 `SUPPORT_LOYALISTS`, 24 `SEND_OFFICERS`, 25 `EXPENSE_SIPHON_INCOME`, 26 `CONDOTTIERI`, 27 `EXPENSE_CORRUPTION`, 28 `EXPENSE_TECHNOLOGY`, 29 `EXPENSE_GREAT_POWER`, 30 `KNOWLEDGE_SHARING`, 31 `TRADE_COMPANY_INVESTMENTS`, 32 none (`NONE`), 33 `EXP_BLOCKADES`, 34 `EXP_LOOTING`, 35 `EXPENSEGREATPROJECTS`, 36 `CENTRE_OF_TRADE_UPGRADE`, 37 `COLONY_CHANGES`. These are localisation keys; what each stands for (6 and 7 presumably the army's and the navy's maintenance) is _inferred_ from them.
- **Loans**: a list of the game's kind (`objects::list`) from country `+0xde8` whose items are loans, written as `loan` (`0x28ad`; `0x2df601` `49 8b 87 e8 0d 00 00`); the item is at a node's `+0` and the next node at its `+0x10` (`0xc00c0`). A loan (vtable `0x1cbc338`, its writer `0x86b0e0`): its ID at `+0x10`; the lender's tag letters at `+0x20` (`---` for none); `interest` at `+0x28`, thousandths of a percentage point (`0x86b1af` `8b 5e 28`); `amount` at `+0x2c` in **whole** ducats (`0x86b202` `8b 5e 2c`, written with `%d`); `expiry_date` at `+0x30` (`0x86b24b` `48 8d 4e 30`); the bytes `fixed_interest` `+0x35` and `estate_loan` `+0x36`.
- Beside them: `+0xde4` `cancelled_loans` (i32), `+0x1105` `loan_size` (u8), `+0xe3c` `estimated_loan`.

### What the economy's actions cost

- **Taking a loan** (`take_bank_loan`, execute `0x5d54e0`): the amount is `loan_size × estimated_loan`, at least 1, in whole ducats (`0x5d558e` `41 0f b6 8e 05 11 00 00 41 0f af 8e 3c 0e 00 00`); the interest is `0x33e390(country, out)` (two arguments: the define at `0x233e148` plus a modifier) plus the command's own `+0x58`; the duration is the command's `+0x64` or, when −1, the define at `0x233f254` (in months, _inferred_).
- **Monarch-point actions** are one class, the power spending (`powerspending.cpp`; vtable `0x1c55480`, its writer `0x94eed0`), carried by `spend_power_command` (`0x2ab3`, vtable `0x1c554c0`; the country's tag at `+0x50`, the spending inline at `+0x58`) and run by `0x94f140`. The spending: its kind at `+0x10` (written as `category`), the kind of monarch power at `+0x14` (`skill`, 0 to 2), a first value at `+0x18` and a second at `+0x1c` (−1 for none), `negative_power` at `+0x30`.
- **The kinds**, by their names (`0x94e1d0(kind)`, jump table `0x94e358`): 0 `buy_idea`, 1 `advance_tech`, **2 `boost_stab`**, 3 `buy_general`, 4 `buy_admiral`, 5 `buy_conq`, 6 `buy_explorer`, 7 `develop_prov`, 8 `force_march`, 9 `assault`, 10 `seize_colony`, 11 `burn_colony`, 12 `attack_natives`, 13 `scorch_earth`, 14 `demand_non_wargoal_prov`, **15 `reduce_inflation`**, 16 `move_capital`, 17 `make_province_core`, 18 `replace_rival`, 19 `change_gov`, 20 `change_culture`, 21 `harsh_treatment`, 22 `reduce_we`, 23 `boost_faction`, 24 `raise_war_taxes`, 25 `increse_tariffs`, **26 `promote_merc`**, 27 `decrease_tariffs`, 28 `move_trade_port`, 29 `create_trade_post`, 30 `siege_sorties`, 31 `buy_religious_reform`, 32 `set_primary_culture`, 33 `add_accepted_culture`, 34 `remove_accepted_culture`, 35 `strengthen_government`, 37 `artillery_barrage`, 38 `establish_siberian_frontier`, 40 `naval_barrage`, 41 `add_tribal_land`.
- **The cost**: `0x9526d0(spending, tag, text, _)` (four arguments; the tag by value; `text` null or a text for the explanation; callers pass 0 as the fourth) answers the cost in whole monarch points (its finish, `0x9524d0`, divides the thousandths by 1000). It switches on the kind (`0x952711` `48 63 49 10 83 f9 29`, jump table `0x9551f8`): `boost_stab` starts from the define at `0x233f620` and the country's modifier `0x1b` (`0x952e7d`), `reduce_inflation` from `0x233f63c` and modifier `0xa9` (`0x953289`), `promote_merc` from `0x233dc4c` and modifier `0x1a7` (`0x953361`), `buy_idea` from `0x233e514` (`0x952732`), and `advance_tech` calls the technology cost above for the spending's category (`0x952df1`). The run checks with `0x94f250` and pays with `0x94fad0`. So the framework can ask any of these costs by building a spending of that kind in its own memory, as the game's own callers do (`0xb26ca`–`0xb270a`); on the paths read the cost reads only `+0x10` to `+0x1c`, and whether anything reads the vtable was not checked.

### National focus

- **Which**: country `+0x2868`, i32: 0 administrative, 1 diplomatic, 2 military, 3 none. The writer writes `national_focus` (`0x2ff4`) as `ADM`, `DIP` or `MIL` for 0 to 2 and nothing otherwise (`0x2dc95a` `41 8b 8f 68 28 00 00`); the setter `0x3db150(country, focus, stamp)` stores it (`0x3db1e0` `89 b7 68 28 00 00`). `set_national_focus_command` (`0x2ff5`, the focus at its `+0x58`) refuses a value above 3.
- **When it was last changed**: country `+0x2490`, a date (`last_focus_move`, `0x2d51`), which the setter sets to the world's date (`0x3db184` `89 81 90 24 00 00`).
- **When it may change again**: `0x3db490(country, text)` (two arguments) takes `+0x2490` (`0x3db4b3` `8b 99 90 24 00 00`) plus years × 8760 hours, the years being the define at `0x233dd2c` less (rank − 1) × the define at `0x233dd28`, plus modifier `0x1d6`, and compares that with the world's date; after it, the change is allowed (_inferred_ from the comparison and the `NAT_FOCUS_COOLDOWN` text it builds otherwise).

### Policies, government, reforms, rank

- **Active policies**: an object at country `+0x140` (`0x2de5c4` `49 8d 8f 40 01 00 00`) holding three arrays from its `+0x10`, 0x30 bytes apart, each with its data (pointers) at `+0` and its count (i32) at `+0xc` (`0x4209fb` `4c 8d 71 10 41 bf 03 00 00 00 49 63 6e 0c`). That the three are the administrative, diplomatic and military policies, in that order, is _inferred_. Each item is an active policy (writer `0x958700`, `active_policy`): the policy at `+0x10` (`0x95870f` `48 8b 59 10`), whose key is a `std::string` at policy `+0x158` (`0x958721`), and the date it was taken at `+0x18`.
- **Government**: a pointer at country `+0x1420` (`0x2dff58` `49 8d 8f 20 14 00 00`), written as `government` (`0x2866`, its writer `0x79dda0`): its type at `+0x10`, the type's key a `std::string` at type `+0x40` (`0x79ddc6`); the reform stack at `+0x38`, an object of its own (`reform_stack`, `0x385b`, its writer `0x7aba40`).
- **The reforms taken**: in the reform stack, a `std::deque` of reform pointers, two to a block: the block map at `+0x20`, its size at `+0x28`, the first element's place at `+0x30`, the count at `+0x38` (`0x7aba80` `4c 8b 61 30 48 8b 41 38`). Element *i*, at place *p* = first + *i*, is in block `(p >> 1) & (map size − 1)` at slot `p & 1`; the writer walks it so and writes `reforms` (`0x3825`) from each reform's key, a `std::string` at reform `+0x10`. That the order is the order taken is _inferred_.
- **Government rank**: country `+0x2860`, a whole i32 (1 to 3 in the unmodded game, _inferred_), written as `government_rank` (`0x319a`; `0x2dc864` `45 8b 87 60 28 00 00`) and read by the focus check (`0x3db4bf` `8b 81 60 28 00 00`, which subtracts 1).

### Estates and parliament

- **The country's estates**: a vector of 0xa8-byte estate objects, inline, at country `+0x21b0` (end `+0x21b8`), each written as `estate` (`0x32d6`; `0x2ddc64` `49 8d 8f b0 21 00 00`; the stride at `0x418880` `48 69 c2 a8 00 00 00`). In each (writer `0x71b840`):
  - `+0x10` the estate itself, whose key is a `std::string` at estate `+0x10` (`0x71b85b` `48 8b 59 10`), written as `type`;
  - `+0x18` loyalty (`0x71b891` `8b 5e 18`), left out of the save at 50.000;
  - `+0x20` `territory`, the land share, thousandths of a percentage point (`0x71b8fb` `8b 5e 20`); `+0x1c` `min_territory`;
  - `+0x58` (end `+0x60`) the influence modifiers and `+0x70` the loyalty modifiers, `+0x28` and `+0x40` the active influences and loyalties, `+0xa0` `num_of_estate_agendas_completed`;
  - `+0x90` the privileges granted, a `std::map` whose head node's pointer is at `+0x90` (`0x71b9db` `4c 8d 86 90 00 00 00`). Its writer (`0x721ec0`, `granted_privileges`, `0x397b`) walks it from the head's leftmost node; each node has its nil byte at `+0x19`, the privilege at `+0x20` (key a `std::string` at privilege `+0x18`) and the date granted at `+0x28` (`0x721f20` `48 8b 53 20 8b 73 28`).
- **Influence is not kept** in the estate object: it is worked out from modifiers. The estates view's tooltip `0x718190(estate, text)` explains it (`ESTATE_INFLUENCE_TOOLTIP`); the function that answers the number was not found.
- **The active agenda** is at country `+0x21c8` (`active_agenda`, `0x3982`), not followed.
- **Parliament**: a pointer at country `+0x24c8`, null without one (`0x2de7a8` `4d 8b 87 c8 24 00 00`), written by `0x9608f0`: `+0x18` `last_debate`; `+0x20` the issues on offer (count at `+0x34`); `+0x50` the active issue (`active_parliament_issue`) and `+0x58` the enacted one, objects of their own not followed. Seats belong to provinces (`set_seat_in_parliament` is a province's), and were not followed.

### Rebels

- **The rebel factions against a country** are at country `+0xe68` (end `+0xe70`; campaign.rs's `REBEL_FACTIONS`): a faction's constructor (`rebelfaction.cpp`) adds itself to the vector of the country whose tag it holds at `+0x30` (`0xa94922` `48 8b 43 30`, then `0x3dd160(country, faction)`, which adds it once: `0x3dd170` `48 81 c1 68 0e 00 00`). The world's writer writes each country's factions as `rebel_faction` (`0x2cc7`, `0x75105e`).
- **A rebel faction** (vtable `0x1cdfc80`, its writer `0xa94970`): its ID at `+0x10`; its rebel type at `+0x20`, whose key is a `std::string` at type `+0x18` (`0xa949da` `49 8b 5e 20`); its name, a `std::string` at `+0x60` (`rebel_name`); `progress` at `+0x148`, thousandths (`0xa94a36` `41 8b 9e 48 01 00 00`; whether full progress is 100.000 was not checked); the country it is against, a tag at `+0x30`; `independence`, a tag at `+0x38`; its religion at `+0x50`, culture at `+0x48` and government at `+0x40`; its province at `+0x28` (the province's ID at its `+0x20`); its provinces, a list at `+0x118`; and `active`, the byte `+0x18b` (written as `no` when clear).

### Legitimacy and the values every country has

Read from `eu4.exe` on 2026-11-11, for `country.get` ([plans/mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md), M3), as above: each field under its key in the country's save writer, then the code that reads it.

- **The fields**, i32 thousandths unless said otherwise:
  - `army_tradition` (`0x2951`) at `+0xe40` and `navy_tradition` (`0x2952`) at `+0xe44`, written when not 0 (`0x2df4ee` `49 8d 97 40 0e 00 00`, `0x2df516`); `corruption` (`0x33f1`) at `+0xed0` (`0x2df930` `49 8d 97 d0 0e 00 00`).
  - `splendor` (`0x356d`) at `+0x48`, `absolutism` (`0x357c`) at `+0x4c` (`0x2dfb94` `49 8d 57 4c`), `revolutionary_zeal` (`0x3a52`) at `+0x60`.
  - `army_professionalism` (`0x36d6`) at `+0x50`: an i64 fixed-point number with 15 bits after the point (1 << 15 is 100%), written with `%lld` by `0x1707b20` only when it is not 0 (`0x2dfbf8` `49 8d 4f 50`, compared by `0xc00a0` with the 64-bit zero at `0x1c3380c`). The `army_professionalism` trigger's tooltip shows such a value times the double 2^-15 at `0x1dc1580` (`0xd02fd3`). The next field at `+0x58` is of the same kind. **Tested**: the Ottomans' in a 1760 save read 18.38%, as the military view's bar's tooltip says; a guess of 100 000 to the whole read 6.02%.
  - `republican_tradition` (`0x2c05`) at `+0x1094`, written when not 0; `devotion` (`0x31c0`) at `+0x1430` and `meritocracy` (`0x35d4`) at `+0x1434`, each written when not 100.000 (`cmp dword ptr [rdx], 0x186a0`).
  - `+0x1090` is written as `legitimacy` (`0x2b51`), or as `horde_unity` (`0x32e8`) when the government is a horde (`0x2dfb2a`–`0x2dfb43`: `0x40a6d0`(country), then `0x7ac7f0`, and `cmovne` of the token): **a monarchy's legitimacy and a horde's horde unity are one field**.
- **Which kind a government has**: strengthening the government (the power spending `strengthen_government`, its check `0x3f78e0`) asks, in this order, `0x7ac7f0` (a horde: horde unity, read by `0x362990`), `0x7ac710` (meritocracy, `+0x1434`), `0x7ac1a0` (a monarchy: legitimacy, read by `0x362b70`), `0x7ac3a0` (a republic: republican tradition, `+0x1094`) and `0x7ac6e0` (devotion, `+0x1430`), and refuses each kind already at 100.000 (`0x3f7a1e` `81 bf 34 14 00 00 a0 86 01 00` and the like), with `STR_GOV_ALREADY_MAX_HORDE_UNIT`, `…_MERITOCRACY`, `…_LEGITIMACY`, `…_REP_TRAD`, `…_DEVOTION`; a government none of them answers yes for has none. The court view's tooltips of each kind (`0xb874f0`, `0xb877f0`, `0xb87d00`, `0xb88710`, `0xb88a70`) name the same five.
  - Each of these takes the government's reforms, which `0x40a6d0`(country) finds: the government's `+0x38` (the government at country `+0x1420`), or a default's. `0x7ac3a0` is a republic without meritocracy (`0x7ac710` no, and `0x7ac3e0` or `0x7ac230` yes); `0x7ac6e0` is `0x7ac600` without meritocracy. Several first look at globals and the thread's own flags (`gs:[0x58]`), presumably whether the reforms may be read on this thread now; the framework asks them on the main thread, as the interface does.
  - `0x362990`(country, out) answers `+0x1090` for a horde and 0 otherwise. `0x362b70`(country, out) answers `+0x1090`, but for a junior partner of a personal union (its subject type's byte `+0x118` set, `0x3d79f0`) whose overlord is a monarchy, the overlord's, recursively: a junior partner shows its senior partner's legitimacy.
  - The Celestial Empire's government has meritocracy in place of legitimacy: `0x7ac710` is asked before `0x7ac1a0`.
- **Tested** (2026-11-11, [the run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-country-rulers-legitimacy-modifiers.md)): in 1444 France, England, Lithuania and the Ottomans read legitimacy, Venice republican tradition, the Pope devotion, Ming meritocracy, the Golden Horde and the Kazakhs horde unity; France's 100 and the Ottomans' 76 in 1760 as the top bar shows them; the Ottomans' army tradition 34.0 and navy tradition 48.1 as the military view shows them. Corruption and absolutism were not compared with a panel.
- **What legitimacy adds to the country's modifiers** is made when the country's modifiers are made again ([modifiers.md](modifiers.md#a-countrys-modifiers)): `0x92e420` for meritocracy, devotion, horde unity and legitimacy, `0x92e810` for republican tradition.
