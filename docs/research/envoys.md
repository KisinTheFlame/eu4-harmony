# Missionaries, colonists, merchants, trade companies and centers of trade

Baseline 1.37.5. All addresses are RVAs. Read from eu4.exe on 2026-11-05 for part A8 of [player-actions.md](../player-actions.md); what was tried in the running game is in [Player actions A8](../prototype-log/2026-11-05-player-actions-a8-missionaries.md). What `core/src/game/` keeps of it is in `envoys.rs`, `missionaries.rs`, `colonists.rs`, `trade_companies.rs`, `centers_of_trade.rs` and `merchants.rs`, each address a dependency point there. Commands are posted as text through the command layer ([commands.md](commands.md)); keys left out take the command's defaults.

## The commands

The command classes were found from their vtables (the command layout of [commands.md](commands.md)), each class's `+0x50` answering its type token, whose name is its written form's key; the writer is the class's `+0x10`, its read `+0x20`, its check `+0x40`, its execute `+0x48`.

| `automate_missionary_command` | `0x1c53330` | `0x2bf7f0` | `0x2bf9a0` | `0x2bf770` |
| `send_colonist` | `0x1c641f8` | `0x445fa0` | `0x4462f0` | `0x445d90` |

- **Commands whose fields are a struct of their own** (`modify_center_of_trade`, `make_trade_company_investment`, `select_trading_policy`) write `members={ … }` (token `0x2aa9`) and then the struct through a descriptor (the class's `+0x50`; `0x1c383d0` for the center of trade, `0x1c38590` for the other two), every field under the key `dummy` (token `0xe6`, `0xcbff0` for a number), in the descriptor's order. So their written form is positional.

## Envoys

- **A country's envoys** are a pointer at `+0x1480` to its pools: colonists at `+0x00`, merchants at `+0x08`, missionaries at `+0x10` (the missionary command's check: `0xaab03e` `48 8b 81 80 14 00 00 4c 8b 48 10`; the merchant command's `0x2747d5`, `0x2747dc` `4c 8b 40 08`; the colonist check `0x34e7f0` reads `[+0x1480]` itself). A pool's envoys are a vector of pointers at `+0x20` (`0xaab06b` `4d 8b 41 20 49 8b 41 28`); an envoy whose byte `+0x18` is set is not free (`0xaab087` `80 78 18 00`): 1 travelling (`0x353d36` `80 79 18 01`), 2 placed (`0x274924` `41 80 79 18 02`).
- A pool whose byte `+0x14` is 1 names a country at `+0x16` (a tag); when that country's `+0x1764` is above 0, the pool has no free envoy (`0xaab049`–`0xaab060`). What this is was not worked out (the merchant check calls it MERCHANTS_TRADE_STEER_LOCKED: _inferred_, an overlord's lock).
- **An envoy** has its ID at `+0x44` (`0x274834` `44 39 52 44`), its name a `std::string` at `+0x20`, its mission at `+0x10` (null when idle; `0x2748d4` `49 8b 51 10`), a mission's destination province at `+0x38` (`0x2748dd` `48 8b 42 38`) and, for a merchant, its kind at `+0x9c`, 0 collect and 1 steer (`0x274257` `88 81 9c 00 00 00`).

## Missionaries

- **The province view's button** (`0x1362060`, a vtable slot; that it is the province view's is _inferred_) toggles: when the province has a missionary construction it recalls (`cancel` 1), otherwise it requires `0x3546b0`(player's country, province). It fills `country` with the province's **owner** (`0x136215c` `48 8b 82 68 04 00 00`, stored at `+0x50`) and `owned_by` with the player's tag (`+0x58`), so `send_missionary={ country="<owner>" owned_by="<player>" location=<id> }`, and `cancel=yes` to recall. `sound` (`+0x65`, written only when not 1) is left at yes. The confirm dialog `0xf98bd0` (`CV_SEND_MISSIONARY`) posts the same with `cancel` no.
- **The command's check** (`0xaaaf30`): the owner's harmonized religion rule; that whether the province has a missionary (`0xa1a320`) equals `cancel`; to send, a free missionary in the sender's pool and `0x3546b0`; to recall, that the missionary construction's country is `owned_by`.
- **`0x3546b0(country, province) -> bool`** says whether the country may send one: the province's owner among the countries `0x3544f0` lists (not worked out; _inferred_ the country and its subjects), no static modifier 2 (_inferred_ can_not_build_missionaries), a free missionary, and a territory rule (`[province+0x2e0]` and the tags at `+0x4a8`).
- **`0x354a00(country, out, province)`** writes why not, as the province view's tooltip does (`0x1379c1d`, called only when `0x3546b0` fails): it constructs `out` and writes nothing when every rule holds. Its lines: NOTYOURS, UNDER_SIEGE, the busy check's (`0xa1baf0`, MISSIONARY_WORKING and the rest), the static modifier's, OURRELIGION, OUR_SECONDARY_RELIGION, FOLLOWING_HARMONIZED_RELIGION(_GROUP), the owner's religion rule `0x3ec1a0`, MISCOLONY, MISSIONARY_RELIGIOUS_CENTER, NOMISS, TERRITORY_CONVERT_RELIGION_NOT_ALLOWED.
- **The upkeep**: `0x34c730(country, i32* out, province) -> out`, the yearly upkeep before the country's multiplier at `+0xedc` (thousandths); the send tooltip (`0x1206de0`, PW_SEND_MISSIONARY_COST) shows `out × [country+0xedc] / 1000 / 12` a month (`0x1206e80` `48 63 8e dc 0e 00 00`). A missionary at work uses its own factor at the construction's `+0x88` (`0x1379892`).
- **The missionary at work** is the construction `0xa1a360(province)` answers (null when none): the entry of the province's constructions (`+0x4d8`) whose `+0x70` answers 5. **Its country is at `+0x80`** (`0xaab0c5` `48 8b 88 80 00 00 00`), not at `+0x40` as other constructions keep it ([provinces.md](provinces.md)). The progress is `+0x894` of the province, 100,000 when done (`0x13797e3`).
- **Recalling** has no reasons function: `CAN_RECALL_MISSIONARY` (`0x34c510`) is the tooltip of a missionary that can be recalled, and the confirm dialog (`0x129d390`, CANCEL_MISSIONARY_DIALOG) posts `cancel=yes`.
- **Automatic missionaries**: `automate_missionary_command={ country="<tag>" value=yes }` raises the count at the country's `+0x14a0` by one, up to the number of missionaries; with `value` left out it lowers it by one, down to 0 (execute `0x2bf770`, `0x2bf79b` `41 8b 91 a0 14 00 00`). The check `0x2bf9a0` is the same two bounds, without reasons; the tooltip (`AUTOMATIC_MISSIONARY_TOOLTIP`) gives none and no cost. The religion view's raise button is `0xfe8050`, its lower buttons `0xfd9150` and `0xfd9280`.

## Colonists

- **The send button** (`0xe396f0`, the send envoy window's `send_colonist`) posts `send_colonist={ country="<tag>" location=<id> }` with `expulsion_province=0` and `settlement_growth_construction=no` (`0xe39872`–`0xe3988d`); its other variant (`0xe39910`) expels a minority and names the province. **Cancel** (`0xe39b40`) posts `cancel_colonist={ country location }`, first asking CONFIRM_ABANDON_COLONY unless the colonist's `+0xe0` answers so (not worked out).
- **`0x34e7f0(country, province) -> bool`** enables the button (`0xe333c5`): a free colonist and `0x34e5f0` (not read). **`0x34e890(country, out, province, _)`** constructs `out` with why not, which the tooltip asks only when the button is disabled (`0xe3560c`): alert_no_native_policy_delayed, CANT_COLONIZE_WHILE_CONCEDING, NATIVE_TRIBES_CANT_COLONISE, CANT_COLONIZE_WHILE_BANKRUPT, COLONY_TO_REMOTE, UNDERCONSTR, SIBHERE, NOCOLONISTS, NOCOLLANDLOCKED, NOPORTOTHERCONTINENT, COLONY_TOO_FAR_AWAY, NOCOLONISENONCITYADJACENT, NOCOLONISE_NO_SHIPS, NOCOLONIALCOLONISENONCITYADJACENT and a rule's text (`0xacccf0`).
- **Recalling** takes effect at the game's next day: the cancel's execute (`0x4464c0`) calls the colonist construction's `+0x58`, and the construction is gone a day later (tested).
- **A native policy** must be chosen before any colonist is sent (alert_no_native_policy_delayed, tested). `set_native_policy={ country policy }`'s check (`0x447160`) wants a colonist in the country's pool, and a policy below 4 other than the country's (`+0x286c`); 2 and 3 were taken.
- The command's own check `0x4462f0` is looser: the province not under construction and owned by none or the country (`0x255d90`).
- **The colonist at work** is `0xa1ba30(province)`, the construction whose `+0x70` answers 10, its country at `+0x40` (the cancel check `0x446850`).
- **Cost**: the send button's tooltip shows none when it is enabled: the effect `on_colonist_boosting_colony` and SENDCOLONIST (`0xe3563a`–`0xe35755`). A colony costs a colonial upkeep, which the budget's tooltip (`0xeb4aa0`, COLONIST_MAINT_COST, COLONIAL_MAINT_SETTLEMENT_GROWTH) shows for all of them together.
- **The colonial upkeep**: `0x34b7a0(country, i32* out, text or null, i32* factor, vector* extra or null) -> out` sums it over the country's colonies (its provinces, `+0x1338`, with settlers at `+0x3f8` and no city flag), the provinces with a colonist promoting settlement growth (a construction of kind `0x38a2`), and the provinces of the vector `extra` (a `std::vector` of province pointers, each counted once), in thousandths of ducats a month (tested: 2.00 for one colonist promoting settlement growth, as the budget showed): for each, from the define COLONIAL_MAINTENANCE_FACTOR (`[0x233d92c]`, from `0x4b0841`), a colony's modifier, and a share growing with the square of the count beyond the country's colonists (_inferred_ from the shape of `0x34bc40`–`0x34bd70`), times `factor`/1000 (`0x34c2c5`). The budget (`0xeb78c6`) and the ledger (`0x36a6ae`) take it with `factor` 1000 and no `extra`, times the country's factor at `+0xe14` (thousandths, `0xeb78bf` `48 63 9f 14 0e 00 00`); the AI (`0x1b4e27`) asks it with a province it weighs in `extra`. So the upkeep a colonist adds is the sum with its province in `extra` less the sum without, which `colonist.options` gives.
- **Expelling minorities**: the send envoy window lists the player's provinces (`0xe2e868`) that `0x264f50(province)` allows (a city, its `+0x8a0` not answering its `+0x40`, and the rule `can_expel_minority_from` of the game's rules, `[0x242bdd8]+0x288`, read by `0xacccf0`) and whose culture (`+0x440`) is not the country's primary culture (`+0x1228`, `0xe2e95d`) or whose religion (`+0x448`) is not its state religion (`+0xf30`, `0xe2eab6`), by minority; each item (`0xe3a090`) is enabled by `0x264ff0(province, reasons or null) -> bool`, which writes the first rule that fails: EXPEL_MINORITIES_NOT_CONTROLLED, EXPEL_MINORITIES_NOT_CORE (a core of the owner's and a state), EXPEL_MINORITIES_ALREADY_SHIPPED_OFF, EXPEL_MINORITIES_SEPARATISM, EXPEL_MINORITIES_CHANGING_CULTURE, EXPEL_MINORITIES_CHANGING_RELIGION, EXPEL_MINORITIES_TOO_MUCH_ZEAL. Choosing one (`0xe3c88b` → `0xe39910`) posts `send_colonist={ country location expulsion_province=<the province> }` (`+0x5c`; written under token `0x3916`).
- **The command's check for an expulsion** (`0x4462f0`, `+0x5c` above 0): a DLC flag, `0x264ec0(tag)` (not read: a DLC check and the country's own), `0x255d90(country, location)`, the expelled province's owner the sender, `0x264f50`, `0x264fb0(location)` (the location has no owner, and its `+0x8a0` answers its `+0x40`), and `0x264ff0`. So the framework lists expulsions only where `0x264ec0` and `0x264fb0` allow.
- **Settlement growth**: the province view's button (`0x137a031`) reads the province's construction of kind `0x38a2` (`0xa1ba90(province, kind)`): with none it offers START_SETTLEMENT_GROWTH, with its tooltip's reasons from **`0x262060(country, province, reasons or null) -> bool`** (silent: a DLC flag, the province the country's, controlled by it and a city; then the busy check `0xa1baf0`'s lines, a country modifier, and NOCOLONISTS without a free colonist) and SETTLEMENT_GROWTH_DESC; with one, STOP_SETTLEMENT_GROWTH. The confirm dialog (`0x136e7e0`) posts, on yes, `send_colonist={ country location settlement_growth_construction=yes }` (`+0x60`, token `0x38a2`; `0x13860d0`), or for the stop `cancel_colonist={ country location settlement_growth_construction=yes }` (`+0x5c`, `0x1386260`). The cancel's check (`0x446850`) takes any province when `+0x5c` is set, so the framework checks that the construction is the player's (its country at `+0x40`). The send command's check with `+0x60` set is `0x262060` without reasons.

## Trade companies

- **The province view's button** (`0xe39e20`, updated in `0xe33a60`) shows when the province's owner is the player, its trade company region (`+0x8a8`) answers its `+8`, and `0x9f8620(province, tag)` (not read). In a trade company (`+0x958` bit 4) it removes and is always enabled; otherwise it adds when `0x3ba790` allows. Its confirm dialog (`0x1369a80`) posts `add_to_trade_company={ country="<player>" province=<id> }` or `remove_from_trade_company` (`0x136a6a0`), `province_list` left empty (the execute takes `province` unless it is 0).
- **`0x3ba790(country, province, reasons) -> bool`** appends to `reasons`: the DLC check, then `0x3ba280` (same arguments; the first rule that fails: NO_TRADE_COMPANY_OWNER, NO_TRADE_COMPANY_STATE, APH_ALREADY_PART_OF_HRE, NO_TRADE_COMPANY_NO_REGION, IS_IN_TRADE_COMPANY, NO_TRADE_COMPANY_RECENTLY_REMOVED, TRADE_COMPANY_SAME_RELIGION, TRADE_COMPANY_SAME_SUPER_REGION), and when it allows it appends TRADE_COMPANY_ADD, what adding does. **`0x3ba830(country, province, text) -> bool`** allows removing when the region has a company of the country's (the region's `+0x28` vector, a company's owner at `+0x1c0`), and writes only what removing does.
- The commands' own check (`0x5dae80`) is only a DLC check: the rules are the interface's.
- **Investments**: the database is `[0x242be40]` (made by `0xb42860` from `common/tradecompany_investments`), its investments in index order an array of pointers at `+0x88` and their count, i32, at `+0x94` (the possible investments' tooltip, `0xb40b2b` `49 8b be 88 00 00 00 49 63 86 94 00 00 00`; tested: 15, the game's); an investment's index is at `+0x10` (the dialog `0x1343cde` `41 8b 7d 10`), its key a `std::string` at `+0x20`, its base cost at `+0xa0`. Its name for a country is `0xb3ddc0(investment, out, tag)`, which constructs `out` with the scripted parts filled in (tested: `settlements` is "葡萄牙定居点" for Portugal, where the localisation alone gives `[Root.GetAdjective]定居点`). A province's area is `[[province+0x28]+0x40]`.
- The confirm dialog (`0x1343c50`, CONFIRM_TRADE_COMPANY_INVESTMENT) posts `make_trade_company_investment={ members={ dummy="<tag>" dummy=<index> dummy=<province> } }` (descriptor's writer `0xc8440`: the tag, then `+4`, then `+0`; `0x1355080`).
- **`0xb40520(tag, investment, province, bool* made, reasons) -> bool`** (reasons the fifth argument, appended to): INVESTMENT_NEED_TRADE_COMPANY_PROVINCE, ALREADY_HAVE_INVESTMENT_IN_AREA (also for the investment's chain), its trigger's lines, INVESTMENT_CANT_AFFORD; `made` is set when the country has it in the area. The command's check calls it as well. The trigger's lines come under a heading ("要求：") and include those that hold, marked `£yes` (tested), so the framework keeps only the others.
- The trade company region at `+0x8a8` is never null: the button asks its `+8` (`0xe33d9f`). The framework lists the investments only for a province in a trade company, as the province view offers them.
- **`0xb3f580(investment, i32* out, tag, area or null, text or null) -> out`**: the cost in thousandths of ducats, the base less the investment it upgrades, times the country's modifier.

## Centers of trade

- **The province view's button** (`0x1395190`, COT_UPGRADE_DOWNGRADE) upgrades on a click and downgrades on a right click, each after a confirm dialog (`0x1392b90`, `0x1393120`), which post `modify_center_of_trade={ members={ dummy=<province> dummy=<level> dummy="<player>" } }` with the level now plus one (`0x1392dc4` `44 8b 41 14 41 ff c0`) or minus one (`0x13933c4`).
- The center is `[province+0x960]` (`0x1392d4f`), its level `[[center+8]+0x14]`.
- **`0x476ac0`, `0x476260`, `0x4760a0`** are each `(center, tag, reasons or null) -> bool`, writing every line with reasons and stopping at the first failure without: both ways (the owner the tag or its subject, else COT_THAT_CHANGES_OWNER_LOSES_LEVEL; COT_UPGRADE_DOWNGRADE_SIEGE, _OCCUPIED, _AT_WAR), the upgrade (COT_UPGRADE_NOT_STATE, COT_MAX_LEVEL, COT_NOT_ENOUGH_DEVELOPMENT, COT_NOT_ENOUGH_MONEY, COT_MAX_LEVEL_IN_COUNTRY, COT_MAX_LEVEL_IN_STATE), the downgrade (COT_DOWNGRADE_NOT_OWNER, COT_MIN_LEVEL, COT_NEGATIVE_PRESTIGE: prestige, `+0xcf8` of the country, below 0). The view asks both ways first, then the way.
- **The upgrade's cost**: `0x476d60(center, i32* out, tag) -> out`, the next level's cost times the modifiers, thousandths of ducats. **The downgrade's**: the execute (`0x474d80`) takes `[0x233e138]` prestige (the define COT_DOWNGRADE_PRESTIGE_COST, `0x4750e2`); the dialogs show a fixed 10.

## Merchants

- **Trade nodes**: the world's nodes are an array at `+0x21b0` of `0x138`-byte nodes, their count at `+0x21bc` (the steer command's execute, `0x5da508`–`0x5da517`); a node's own index at `+0x120`, its definition at `+0xa8` (key `+0x10`, name `+0x30`, index `+0xd8`, the province where it lies `+0xdc`, `0x101cb42`), its links downstream at the definition's `+0x98` (`0x78`-byte links, the definition each leads to at `+0x30`).
- **A merchant's mission** (made by `0x25aaf0`) keeps its node at `+0x80`; a recall (`0x25ba70`, from the recall's execute) sets its phase at `+0x98` to 2 (`0x25baf7`) and sends the merchant home, the mission staying until it is back (tested: a recalled merchant's mission still names the node). The node's entry says the merchant is there only once it arrived (tested), so the framework finds a merchant on its way by its mission.
- **A country's entry in a node**: `[node+0x18] + index × 0xc0` (`0x2741b4`): `+0xa8` the link its merchant steers along, `+0xad` its home node, `+0xae` a merchant of its there, `+0xb0` its trading policy and `+0xb8` the date it was set. `0xb58e00(node, tag)` answers whether the country collects there.
- **`send_merchant={ province=<the node's province> country="<tag>" name=<merchant's ID> type=0|1 }`**: 0 collects, 1 steers (`0x101cc0a`–`0x101cc10`; TRADE_MISSION_OFFICE and TRADE_MISSION_TRANSIT in `0x13d6570`). The trade view's merchant item names its merchant; sending the merchant already in the node changes its mission (the execute only switches the kind when the destination is the same; tested on one on its way). The check lets a merchant on its way count as free (tested: it allowed a send with none free), so the framework refuses then with the game's TRADER_FREE_ENVOY text, which the check writes when none is free. **`cancel_merchant={ node=<the node's province> country }`** recalls the placed merchant (`0x13cb560`). **`steer_command={ country to=<link> trade=<node's index> }`** has the steering merchant steer along that link (`0x13fd5f0`).
- **`0x3532c0(country, province, text, already there counts, needs a free one, _) -> bool`**, which the trade view calls as `(country, location, &text, 0, 1, 0)`, writes the rules, those that hold too: LACK_TECHNOLOGY_FOR_MERCHANT, TRADER_ALLREADY_THERE, MERCHANTS_TRADE_STEER_LOCKED, TRADE_ENROUTE, TRADER_NOT_DISCOVERED / TRADER_DISCOVERED, TRADER_FREE_ENVOY, TRADER_IN_RANGE. The view's own panel (`0x101ccf0`) adds TRADE_MERCHANT_ALREADY_COLLECTING, TRADE_MERCHANT_ALREADY_TRANSFERRING and TRADE_TRANSIT_NODE_CAPITAL (steering in the home node), which the framework checks itself with the game's texts.
- **`0xb5b290(node, tag, reasons) -> bool`** says whether the country's merchant may steer: STEER_HOME, STEER_NO_TRADER, and `0xb5c010`'s STEER_NO_POWER and STEER_LATER.
- **Trading policies**: `0xd38b60()` answers the database (`[0x242be78]`, made on first use from `common/trading_policies`), its policies an array of pointers at `+0x48` and their count, i32, at `+0x54` (`0xd397df` `48 8b 71 48 48 63 41 54`); a policy's key a `std::string` at `+0x10`, which is also the localisation key of its name (`0xd37c7f`), its index at `+0x50` (`0x13cba8f`), its trigger at `+0x348`, flags `+0x54` (center_of_reformation), `+0x55` (unique) and `+0x57` (cooldown). The first policy whose trigger holds is the country's default (`0xd39730`). A country's entry in a node keeps its policy at `+0xb0` (null for none) and the date it chose it at `+0xb8`.
- **The trade view's policy button** (`0x13cc540`) is enabled, for a policy other than the one the player follows, by **`0xb5c6a0(node, tag, policy, reasons or null) -> bool`**, appending to `reasons`; for the one followed, by whether the player's merchant is there. `0xb5c6a0` calls `0xb5dc80(entry, policy, node, 0, 0, reasons)`, which refuses the policy followed (when it has a cooldown) without a reason, writes TRADING_POLICY_IN_COOLDOWN with the date it ends when the policy followed has a cooldown and was chosen less than the define TRADING_POLICY_COOLDOWN_MONTHS (`[0x233f364]`, from `0x4ac368`; 12 in the game's defines) ago, and then writes the trigger's failing lines (`0xd36b80(policy, country, node, reasons)`, the trigger at `+0x348` in the node's scope); then UNIQUE_TRADING_POLICY_ALREADY_ACTIVE when a unique policy is another country's there.
- The button's click (`0x13cba10`) asks the same and opens CONFIRM_SELECT_TRADING_POLICY_DESC, which posts **`select_trading_policy={ members={ dummy="<tag>" dummy=<definition's index> dummy=<policy's index> } }`** (the command at `+0x50` the policy's index, `+0x54` the definition's, `[definition+0xd8]` `0x13cba84`, `+0x58` the tag). The command's check (`0xc2490`) is the cooldown and the trigger without the unique rule; its execute (`0xb5db70(entry, policy, node)`) changes the policy only where the country's merchant is (`+0xae`), and sets the date only when the policy changes.
- The trade view's list of policies (`0x13d58e0`) keeps those whose trigger at `+0x348` holds for the player's country without the node; `merchant.policies` lists every policy of the database instead, with the game's reasons for each.
