# What the game's message pop-up does with its Go to button and its shields: a static study

Date 2026-11-09. Subject: vanilla 1.37.5 `eu4.exe`, image base `0x140000000`. All addresses below are **RVAs**.

Method: static analysis only; the game was not started and no process was attached. Disassembly with capstone, `.pdata` function bounds, RIP-relative references, `E8`/`E9` calls and vtable slots scanned with scripts written for this study. What is marked **verified** was seen in the bytes; what is marked **inferred** is a judgement drawn from structure. All prototypes are tentative. The map manager (`campaign::world::MAP_MANAGER`, `[0x233fe78] + 0x1e00`, class `0x1cb8460`) is the object [extension-windows.md](extension-windows.md) and [notice-window.md](notice-window.md) call the idler.

It is the research for the pop-ups of [plans/extension-windows.md](../plans/extension-windows.md): a Go to that glides as the game's does, and shields that open the country's diplomacy view.

## Conclusion

- **Go to** (verified): the message pop-up's left button runs a callback the message was sent with; for the messages that name a place it is the map manager's method at vtable `+0x3c8`, `0x830150(map manager, message*)`. For a province it closes the views over the map (`0x818080`), may select the province (below), and calls **`0x80fe20(map manager, province)`** and nothing after it: the camera glides, as [camera.md](camera.md) says of the game's own moves. The framework's camera moves at once only because it calls `0x109cd80` after `0x80fe20`; leaving that out is the game's Go to.
- **A shield's click** (verified): the game's shields are wired by a helper the map manager keeps at `+0x58`, whose click handler `0x10b4720(helper, widget)` reads the country index from the widget (vtable `+0x1c0`) and calls **`0x821420(map manager, tag)`**, which opens the diplomacy view on that country.
- **A framework shield can take the same click** (verified for the slots, inferred for the whole): the slots the helper uses, `+0x218` (country), `+0x1b0`/`+0x1c0` (index), `+0x170` (clickable) and the click signal at `+0xc8`, are those of the game's button class `0x1d58218`, which the framework's pop-up shields (`guiButtonType`, `standarddialogshield`) already are; the framework's flags set `+0x218` and `+0x1b0` themselves. So either `0x10b4100(helper, widget, index, 1, 1, 1)` wires the framework's shield as the game wires its own, or the framework's own click handler calls `0x821420(map manager, tag)`.

## 1. How the pop-up is built and where its buttons lead

- **The factory** `0x8c1430` builds the pop-up for a message by its kind (`[[message + 8] + 0x58]`); kinds 0 and 7 get `DefaultPopup` (string `0x1cc0a68`, `interface/eu3dialog.gui`), the others the land and naval combat pop-ups, the colony dialogs, the heir naming and the new subject pop-ups. The message handler `0x8c07b0` (`messagehandler.cpp`) calls it and posts the dialog (`0x6ecae0`). The handler's third argument is a `std::function` (MSVC layout: 0x38 bytes of storage, the implementation pointer at `+0x38`; `+0x00` copy, `+0x10` call, `+0x20` delete), the action the message carries (verified).
- **The constructor** `0x8c1f80(pop-up, handler, message, std::function*, …)` (first bytes `4c 8b dc 4d 89 4b 20 49 89 4b 08 53 55 56 57 41 54 41 55 41`) calls the callback dialog base `0x10a03f0` with empty agree and decline callbacks, sets the vtable `0x1cc1df8`, keeps the handler at `+0x1b8`, copies the message to `+0x1c0`, and copies the action into its own `std::function` at `+0x2c8` (implementation pointer at `+0x300`). Three member callbacks (vtable `0x1cb80c8`: `+0x08` object, `+0x10` function) are kept at `+0x250` (`0x8cc340`, the action), `+0x4b8` (`0x8cc390`, the Settings button: the `MessageSettingWindow` through `0x1243500`) and `+0x530` (`0x8d1b80`, used by kind 6). Then the kind's filler runs; for `DefaultPopup` `0x8c4370` (verified).
- **The filler** `0x8c4370` writes `Header`, `Line1`..`Line6`, places the buttons, and decides them by the message's settings at `[pop-up + 0x1c8]` (verified):
  - byte `+0x60` clear: `AgreeButton` and `DeclineButton` hidden, `CenterOK` shown and closing the pop-up through the base's callback at `+0x90`, its text the settings' key (`+0x38`) with `_BTN1` (`0x8c5a6c`–`0x8c5b5a`).
  - byte `+0x60` set: `CenterOK` hidden, the two buttons' texts the key with `_BTN2` (agree, `0x10a29e0`) and `_BTN3` (decline, `0x10a2a80`); when the pop-up has an action (`+0x300` not null), `DeclineButton`'s click signal (`+0xc8`) loses the base's close (`+0x90`, signal vtable `+0x10`) and takes the action callback `+0x250` (`+0x08`) (`0x8c5a0f`–`0x8c5a64`). So the game's Go to is the **left** button, `DeclineButton` (z, Esc in `eu3dialog.gui`), and the agreeing button on the right closes.
- **The action callback** `0x8cc340(pop-up)` (`40 53 48 83 ec 20 48 8b d9 48 8b 89 00 03 00 00 48 85 c9 74`) calls the `std::function` with the message (`+0x1c0`); if it answers false it closes the pop-up (`[+0x1b0]`'s vtable `+0x10`) and marks it closing (`+0x114` = 1) (verified). The Go to below answers true.

## 2. Go to

- **The action.** Message senders such as `0x1af280`(handler, message, object, member function pointer) wrap a member function pointer into the action (`std::function` vtable `0x1c426b0`, call `0x1af4d0`: `this + adjustment`, then the function). The function the callers looked at give (`0x1adbbe`, `0x306fdd`, `0x703a8c`, and 26 RIP references in all) is **`0x1af510`** (`48 8b 01 ff a0 c8 03 00 00`: `jmp [vtable + 0x3c8]`), the object the map manager (verified for these three; that every place-naming message goes this way is inferred).
- **The map manager's `+0x3c8`** is **`0x830150(map manager, message*)`** (`48 89 5c 24 18 56 57 41 56 48 83 ec 20 4c 8b f2 48 8b f1 e8`), answering true (verified):
  1. `0x818080(map manager)`, which closes the views over the map ([extension-windows.md](extension-windows.md)).
  2. By the message type's name (`[message + 8] + 0x38`, a `std::string`) some types open a panel instead, through `0x820990(map manager, panel)` (for example `MERCHANT_ARRIVED`, `land_tech`, the improving relations messages).
  3. The rest look at a place: the province at `[message + 0x48]`, or, when it is null, the capital (`[country + 0x14b0]`) of the country whose tag is at `+0x28` (or `+0x30` when that tag is not valid). When the province's byte `+0x18` is 0, it calls the map manager's `+0x258` (`0x818080` again) and `+0x230`, `0x8181d0(map manager, province + 0x10)`, which adds the province to the selection at `+0x1b80` (inferred: so its province view opens; the byte `+0x18` was not worked out). Then **`0x80fe20(map manager, province)`** (`0x83065d`), and it returns.
- Nothing in this path calls `0x109cd80`, so the camera glides (verified), and the zoom with a panel open, `0x139c6a0`, is not used (verified: its four callers are the province view's and three others, none in this path).
- `0x80fd30(map manager, province ID)` is the map manager's `+0x278`: the province from the world's array (`+0x1ca8`, stride `0x2e10`), then `jmp 0x80fe20` (verified).

## 3. The shields and the diplomacy view

- **Setting a shield.** The filler calls `0x10a21e0(dialog, tag, faction or null, star, clickable)` for `LeftShield` and `0x10a24d0` (same first bytes, `48 89 5c 24 18 48 89 54 24 10 55 57 41 54 41 55 41 56 48 83`) for `RightShield`, the tags read from the message at `+0x210`/`+0x1e8` and `+0x218`/`+0x1f0`, the fifth argument 1 (`0x8c5b67`–`0x8c5c87`). Each finds its element by name in the dialog's table (`+0xa98`), then calls `0x10b44b0(helper, widget, tag, clickable, clickable, clickable, 1, 0)` with the helper `[map manager + 0x58]`; `LeftStar`/`RightStar` are shown when `star` is set (verified).
- **`0x10b44b0(helper, widget, tag, a, b, c, d, e)`** (`48 89 5c 24 08 48 89 74 24 10 4c 89 44 24 18 57 48 83 ec 40`): the widget's vtable `+0x218` with `{tag, d, e}` (the framework's `SET_COUNTRY`, `0x10b56b0`, which it calls with `{tag, 1}`), then `0x10b4100(helper, widget, tag's index, a, b, c)` (verified).
- **`0x10b4100(helper, widget, index, clickable, right click, tooltip)`** (`48 85 d2 0f 84 da 01 00 00 48 89 5c 24 08 48 89 74 24 10 48`): the widget's `+0x1b0` with the index (the button class's `0xfaab90`, `mov [rcx+0x138], edx`); when clickable, the helper's callback at `+0x278` subscribed to the widget's click signal `+0xc8` (signal vtable `+0x08`; `+0x10` unsubscribes when not); with a right click, the callback at `+0x358` and the widget's `+0x170` with 1 (the button class's `0x1593860`, `mov [rcx+0x161], dl`); with a tooltip, `DefaultShieldTooltip` (verified). The helper's constructor sets the callback at `+0x278` (vtable `0x1cb8150`, object `+0x280`) to **`0x10b4720`** at `0x10b3c31`, and the one at `+0x358` to `0x10b4970` (verified).
- **The click** `0x10b4720(helper, widget)` (`48 85 d2 0f 84 3e 02 00 00 53 48 83 ec 30 48 8b 02 48 8b ca`): the index from the widget's `+0x1c0` (the button class's `0xfaab80`, `mov eax, [rcx+0x138]`), the country's tag `[country + 0x20]` from the country manager (`0x233d8d0`, array `+0x118`); then, with the map manager and in a game (`[0x233fe78] + 0x7484`) and the map manager's `+0x388` answering yes (for the map manager's class it is `0x8f440`, always yes): for a valid tag of a kind other than rebels whose index is none of `campaign::globals::SPECIAL_COUNTRIES`, **`0x821420(map manager, tag)`**; for the rebels and an index of 0 or less, `0x8214e0` (the rebels' list, `revolters_listbox`). Out of a game it calls the map manager's `+0x280` with the country's capital (verified).
- **`0x821420(map manager, tag)`** (`48 89 5c 24 08 48 89 74 24 10 57 48 83 ec 20 48 8b 81 10 11`): the diplomacy view `[[map manager + 0x1110] + 0x20]`; `0xf15c30(view, 0)` (its `select_rivals_window` and `countryview_tabs`), `0x820990(map manager, 4)`, which closes the other panels and shows that one (inferred from its other uses: `0x830150` passes 0 to 3, 10 and 11), then `jmp 0xf01870(view, tag)` (`48 89 54 24 10 53 57 48 83 ec 58 48 8b f9 48 8b da 48 8b 0d`), which sets the country shown, next to the player's tag at `+0x148`, among the functions that fill `diplomatic_listbox`, `show_diploactions` and the trust and favors bars (verified that it is the diplomacy view's code; that the view then shows that country is inferred). It has nine call sites and is the map manager's way to open the diplomacy view for anyone.

- **`0x820990(map manager, view)`** (`40 53 55 57 48 83 ec 20 4c 89 7c 24 58 48 8b d9 44 8b fa e8`): first `0x818080`, then the view number kept at `+0x1108`, and, when the view is to show, each view of the vector `+0x1110`..`+0x1118` that answers open (vtable `+0x40`) hidden (`+0x38`, `+0x28`). It does not call `0x80c6e0`, so an observer on that function does not hear a view opened this way (verified in the game: the diplomacy view opened from a framework shield left an extension window that is a big panel open, until the framework observed this function too).

## Not verified

- Nothing here was run. That `0x821420` from a framework shield's click opens the diplomacy view, that a framework shield wired by `0x10b4100` behaves as the game's, and that `0x8181d0` opens the province view were not tried in the game.
- The byte `+0x18` of a province that decides whether Go to selects it, and what `0x820990`'s panel numbers are beyond 4 being the diplomacy view, were not worked out.
- Whether the helper's subscription is removed when a framework window is killed (the signal owning its subscriptions) was not looked at; a framework that subscribes its own click handler and calls `0x821420` does not depend on it.
