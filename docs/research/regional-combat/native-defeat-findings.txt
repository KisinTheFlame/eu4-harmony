Regular, overrun, and no-retreat cases
22BEA0 sums regiment strengths over both sides. Ratio thresholds choose:
 A. log 'Combat ended (regular)' at1C492D0; 239FF0(battle,index), then
    21EB30(loser). Direct retreat call22C054, return address22C059.
 B. 22B5A0(battle,winner,loser,index), call22C002/return22C007.
22B5A0 logs 'Combat ended (overrun)' at1C492E8, marks loser+169=1,
processes losses, calls239FF0, then21EF40(loser,...).
21EF40 copies the losing-side list, processes regiments, then calls
768E20(world,unit,...) to remove each unit. It is not a retreat-only path.
22ACB0 can call22B5A0 directly, without22BEA0: side0 wins at22B0AF
(return22B0B4); side1 wins at22B127(return22B12C). Thus a hook only at
22BEA0 misses immediate overrun/stackwipe. 22ACB0 first rejects battle kind2.
22C080 invokes22ACB0 both before normal fighting and in a later overrun test.
21EB30 computes each unit's retreat target through D4B270/D4A9E0. If no
retreat target is available, it calls768E20 and removes that losing army.
Other special unit+210 branches also remove units. The entry capture at
22BEA0 covers this no-route removal, even though no stable retreating army
remains in the following daily snapshot. Do not wait for flag203/204.
Already-empty-side22C080 returns1 early without either settlement. This is
not proof of a new defeat: previously removed units may already be gone.
A completely missing army should be retired by identity/session handling;
absence alone must not generate a fake defeat notification.

Exclusions: why generic hooks are not sufficient
21EB30 is also invoked from23FCE0 (siege path), call24036E/return240373.
239FF0 also has caller23B1D0. That function removes an arbitrary unit from
battle and, if it was its side's last unit, logs 'Combat ended (Unit removed)'
at1C49B18 and creates results. It is not intrinsically involuntary defeat.
D6B910 retreat command and A9E160 rebel-combat removal can set203/204.
Therefore status203, status204, side+205, or239FF0 alone cannot distinguish
active retreat, scripted removal, siege exit, and genuine land defeat.
Do not broaden the two verified settlement hooks to these generic helpers.

D5CF10 movement-command-execute has now been observed during automation too.
