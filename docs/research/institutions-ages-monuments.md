# Institutions, ages and monuments

Baseline 1.37.5. All addresses are RVAs. Read from `eu4.exe` on 2026-11-11 with a Capstone script over the PE file, the command vtables named by the token table ([commands.md](commands.md)), and `cargo xtask disasm`. What the framework builds on it is part M20 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md): the `institution`, `age` and `monument` groups. What was tried in the running game is in [the run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-institutions-ages-monuments.md).

## Institutions

- **The database**: its getter `0x855ad0`; a vector of pointers at `+0x10`, each institution's index an i32 at `+0x10` and its key a `std::string` at `+0x18` ([provinces.md](provinces.md#prosperity-devastation-manpower-supply-separatism-and-institutions-for-m6-of-mod-capabilities)). Whether one has appeared is the world's vector at `+0x22a0`, by index.
- **Whether a country has embraced one**: a vector (begin, end) of i32 at country `+0xc68`, 1 by the institution's index once embraced; the country's save writer writes it as `institutions` (`0x2dc992`).
- **The check** `0x3f6340(country, institution, reasons, with_cost) -> bool`, which the institutions view (`0x10167aa`) and the command's check (`0x2bc481`) call, the view with a `std::string` for its reasons and `with_cost` set, the check with none:
  1. the institution has appeared, or `INSTI_NOT_ACTIVE`;
  2. the share of the country's development that has it (`0x3f5510(country, out, institution, null)`, i32 thousandths into `out`; 1000 for one embraced) is at least 100 (10%), or `INSTI_NOT_ENOUGH_HOLD` with the share and what is needed;
  3. with `with_cost`, the cost (`0x3f6b50(country, out, institution, null)`, i32 thousandths of a ducat) no more than the treasury (`+0xbb0`), or `INSTI_TOO_EXPENSIVE`.
  It does not ask whether the country has embraced it already; the view does not offer it then.
- **The command** `embrace_institution_command` (vtable `0x1c53528`; write `0x2bc160`, read `0x2bc250`, check `0x2bc450`, execute `0x2bb3a0`): `country` is the tag's index (`%d` of the tag's bits 32–47, read back through the country manager), `institutions` the institution's index, `message` a bool the view's button sets (`0x10193e1`). As the button writes it: `embrace_institution_command={ country=<index> institutions=<index> message=yes }`. The execute pays the cost as a single-time expense `embrace_institution` and sets the flag.

## Ages

- **The ages' database**: its getter `0x8db70` makes it on first use (0x78 bytes) and keeps it at `[0x242b9e0]`; three maps by key with a vector of pointers each, the last the abilities of every age (`+0x60`, as the command's reader indexes it, `0x2bdb2c`), in order of their index; that the first two are the ages and the objectives is inferred from the constructor's layout and the reader's keys, not read.
- **The age the world is in**: a pointer at world `+0x2420`, its key a `std::string` at `+0x10`; the world's save writer writes it as `current_age` (`0x750a43`).
- **An ability**, made by the age's reader (`0x8c65a`): its index at `+0x10` (the count of the database's vector when made), its age at `+0x18`, its `allow` triggers an array at `+0x28` (count `+0x34`), its key a `std::string` at `+0x98`.
- **A country's abilities taken**: a vector of pointers at country `+0x30`, written as `active_age_ability`; its splendor an i32 of thousandths at `+0x48`.
- **The check** `0x3f8d30(country, ability, reasons) -> bool`, the command's check calling it with no reasons: no, without a reason, for an ability taken; each `allow` trigger, whose failing lines it writes; the splendor at least the define at `[0x233e4e4]` (thousandths), or `ADD_AGE_AB_NOT_ENOUGH_SPLENDOR`. It does not ask whether the ability is of the age the world is in.
- **The command** `age_ability_command` (vtable `0x1c535d0`; write `0x2bd9f0`, read `0x2bdae0`, check `0x2bdc60`, execute `0x2bd700`): `country` the tag as a string, `which` the ability's index into the database's vector: `age_ability_command={ country="FRA" which=<index> }`. The ages view makes it at `0xde9a57`.
- **The golden era** is the command `enact_golden_age` (token `0x357b`, vtable `0x1c531e0`: writer `0x2a4d40`, the country alone; check `0x2bde20`; execute `0x2bdd20`, which calls `0x3f9570` with the country): `enact_golden_age={ country="FRA" }`. The command's check: no while the country is in one (its start, i32 at country `+0x2788`, after `0x29c77f8` and its end not passed), no once it has had one, and the country's `+0x28` at least 3. The ages view's button's tooltip is `0x3f9f00(country, text) -> bool`: `WE_ARE_IN_A_GOLDEN_ERA` with the day it ends (`UNTIL`), `ALREADY_GOLDEN_ERA`, `GOLDEN_ERA_NOT_NUF` with `NUM` 3 (the age's objectives the country has completed, so the `+0x28` is _inferred_ to count them), or `GOLDEN_ERA_ENACT` with the years; each with the golden era's effects (`EFFECTS`). The end is the start plus the define at `[0x233e2d0]` years of 8760 hours, then `0x14c8030` (adding months) with the months at country `+0x278c`. Tried in the game ([the run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-defender-and-golden-era.md)): the day read matched the tooltip's after the game console's `golden_age` started one.

## Monuments

- **The great projects' manager** at world `+0x25b0`: a map by the definition's token (`+0x18` end, `+0x28` buckets, `+0x40` mask; `0x7d3060`) to an index into an array of the game's own, its data at `+0x58` and its length (i32) at `+0x64`, each project 0x5e8 bytes (`0x92d949`, which adds the projects of a country's provinces to its modifiers).
- **A project** (vtable `0x1cb4428`, its save writer at `+0x10`, `0x7d0a50`): its definition at `+0x10`, a pointer whose key is a `std::string` at `+0x10` and whose kind, u8 at `+0x1d36`, is 1 for a canal and 2 for a monument; its province, i32 at `+0x5e0`, 0 for one the world does not have (`+0x58`, `0x7d08c0`); its tier, u8 at `+0x5e4`, from 0 to 3; when it was built and destroyed at `+0x5d8` and `+0x5dc`.
- **The upgrade check**, its vtable's `+0x60`, `0x7d1560(project, country, flag, reasons) -> bool`: no for one the world does not have; at tier 3, `CANNOT_UPGRADE_GREAT_PROJECT_ALREADY_AT_MAX`; then the definition's check for the next tier (`0x7d5f30`), which writes its requirements' failing lines (`MACRO_REQUIREMENT_NOT_FULFILLED`) and the cost against the treasury (`BUILD_BUILDING_COST_TOO_HIGH`). The command's check calls it with no flag and no reasons, after the builder owns a province.
- **The cost**, its vtable's `+0x70`, `0x7d17a0(project, out, country, explanation or null)`: i32 thousandths of a ducat into `out`, through the definition's `0x7d7430` for the next tier.
- **The command** `upgrade_great_project` (token `0x3b64`, vtable `0x1c4e148`; write `0x267df0` through `0x26a7a0`, read `0x26b420`, check `0x26b690`, execute `0x267e20`), as the monuments window's button makes it (`0x139777c`): `great_projects` the definition's key, `province` the province's ID, `builder` the country's tag, written when its last byte is set: `upgrade_great_project={ great_projects="<key>" province=<id> builder="FRA" }`. `constructgreatproject` (vtable `0x1c4e1f0`) shares its writer and reader; it and the other commands are the next section's.

## Great projects' other commands

Found on 2026-11-11 from the command vtables (writer `+0x10`, reader `+0x20`, check `+0x40`, execute `+0x48`), the tokens their writers write, the monuments window's buttons that make them and the checks their tooltips ask; offered as `monument.buildable`, `monument.build`, `monument.evaluate_move`, `monument.move`, `monument.constructions`, `monument.cancel` and `monument.boost` (part M20 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md); [the run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-great-project-actions.md)).

- **The window** keeps its project at `+0x1c0` and its province at `+0x1c8`; one button sends `upgrade_great_project` when the project's `+0x58` says the world has it, and `constructgreatproject` when it does not (`0x13976d9`); the move button sends `move_great_project` (`0x1396db2`); each with the definition at the command's `+0x50`, the window's province's ID at `+0x58` and the player at `+0x5c`.
- **Building a great project** (a canal): `constructgreatproject` (token `0x3b65`, vtable `0x1c4e1f0`), writer and reader those of `upgrade_great_project`: `constructgreatproject={ great_projects="<key>" province=<id> builder="FRA" }`. Its check `0x26b540` wants a definition, a province above 0 and a builder with provinces or of kind 7, then `0x7d49d0(definition, country, province, reasons, flag) -> bool`, which writes its reasons into a `std::string`: none, and no, for a project the world has (its `+0x58`) or one not enabled (`+0x40`); then `0x7d4800`: unless the definition may stand anywhere (u8 at `+0x1d34`) its site (i32 at `+0x1d30`, 0 for none) must be the province, or `CANNOT_BUILD_GREAT_PROJECT_WRONG_LOCATION`; the country owning the province (`0x32a920`), in its control (`MACRO_NOT_CONTROLLER`), not under siege (`MACRO_UNDER_SIEGE`), not a colony (`MACRO_IS_COLONY`); the cost, `0x7d7b80(definition, out, country)` in thousandths of a ducat, against the treasury (`BUILD_BUILDING_COST_TOO_HIGH`); the requirements (`MACRO_REQUIREMENT_NOT_FULFILLED`). For a canal (kind 1) it also asks `0x9751b0`, _inferred_ to be the expansion's.
- **Moving a monument**: `move_great_project` (token `0x3b9c`, vtable `0x1c4e298`), the same writer: `move_great_project={ great_projects="<key>" province=<target> builder="FRA" }`. Its check `0x26b800` finds the project by its definition in the manager (`0x7d3060`), asks its `+0x58` and then its vtable's `+0x98`, `0x7d1ef0(project, country, target province, flag, reasons) -> bool`, the reasons a `std::string` in the fifth argument: one the definition does not let move (`+0x1d34` clear), `CANNOT_MOVE_GREAT_PROJECT_TOO_BIG`; one being moved, `CANNOT_MOVE_ALREADY_IN_PROGRESS`; a target the country or its subject does not own, `MACRO_NOT_OWNER_OR_SUBJ_OWNER`; from a subject of the types the definition forbids, `CANNOT_MOVE_GREAT_PROJECT_FROM_SUBJECT_TYPE`; no route, `CANNOT_MOVE_GREAT_PROJECT_NO_PATH`. The cost is its vtable's `+0xa8`, `0x7d2550(project, out, target province)`: the distance (`0x2d2520`) times the definition's days per unit of distance (i32 at `+0x1d2c`, thousandths), times the define `GREAT_PROJECT_MOVE_COST_PER_DAY` (`[0x233f6dc]`), in thousandths of a ducat; 0 where there is no route. The tooltip `0x7d26f0` explains it (`MOVE_GREAT_PROJECT_EXPLANATION`).
- **The constructions**: a great project's construction is in its province's list (`+0x4d8`), of kind 8 (its vtable's `+0x70`), its token `great_project_construction`; what it makes, at `+0x80`, is the project's definition, its builder a tag at `+0x88`, its start and end at `+0x20` and `+0x24`.
- **Cancelling a construction**: `cancel_great_project` (token `0x3b68`, vtable `0x1c4dab8`): its writer `0x272810` writes `country` (the tag, at `+0x50`), `province` (`+0x60`) and `great_projects` (the definition at `+0x58`): `cancel_great_project={ country="FRA" province=<id> great_projects="<key>" }`. Its check `0x272c80` finds the province's construction of kind 8 (`0x272cc0`) and compares its builder with the country; it writes no reasons, and the window's cancel button (`0x1398a53`) shows only what is cancelled (`CANCEL_GREAT_PROJECT`, `CANCEL_GREAT_PROJECT_UPGRADE`, `CANCEL_GREAT_PROJECT_MOVE`).
- **Boosting a construction**: `boost_great_project` (token `0x3b69`, vtable `0x1c4df50`: writer `0x26c850`, `great_projects` (`+0x50`), `province` (`+0x58`), `type` (a byte at `+0x64`, the window's `+0x1d0`) and `builder` (`+0x5c`); check `0x26cc70`, a construction of kind 8 in the province; execute `0x26b8e0`). The execute's `type`: 0 pays the define `GREAT_PROJECT_MANPOWER_REQUIRED_FOR_CONSTRUCTION_BOOST` (`[0x233da2c]`, men, against the builder's manpower at `+0xdb8`) once, 1 the define `GREAT_PROJECT_MONEY_REQUIRED_FOR_CONSTRUCTION_BOOST` (`[0x233da30]`, thousandths of a ducat, against the treasury) once, each bringing the start and the end forward by its define's days (`[0x233e354]`, `[0x233e358]`, thousandths); 2 and 3 do the same over and over while the builder can pay and the construction has not ended. The window's checks are `0x25faf0(construction, reasons)` for manpower (`CANNOT_SPEND_MANPOWER_TO_BOOST_PROGRESS_NOT_ENOUGH_MANPOWER`, `CANNOT_BOOST_PROGRESS_FINISHED`) and `0x25fbf0` for money (`CANNOT_SPEND_MONEY_TO_BOOST_PROGRESS_NOT_ENOUGH_CASH`), the reasons a `std::string`. What sets the window's `+0x1d0` to 2 or 3 was not read.
