## Personal deities

- **The database**: a singleton at `[0x242bca8]`, 0x28 bytes, made by its getter `0x7e6910` (`0x7e691a` `48 8b 05 87 53 c4 01`): a name table (count `+0`, 511 buckets `+4`, the buckets `+8`, each node the deity at `+0` and the next node at `+8`; looked up by `0x7e6830`, hash `(h + c) × 61`), then the deities in file order, a `std::vector` of pointers at `+0x10` (`0x7e6961` `48 89 7b 10`).
- **A deity** (its reader of one key, `0x7e5c10`): its key a `std::string` at `+0x10` (the command's writer, `0xaae096` `48 83 c3 10`); its modifiers `+0x70`; `effect` `+0x1c8`, `removed_effect` `+0x200`, `ai_will_do` `+0x238`, `sprite` `+0x274`, `potential` `+0x278` and `trigger` `+0x2f0`, content triggers as a decision's ([triggers.md](triggers.md)). **A trigger's reader is its part at `+0x38`**, which the deity's reader is handed: `potential` is read at `+0x2b0` (`0x7e5cd0` `48 81 c1 b0 02 00 00`) and `trigger` at `+0x328` (`0x7e5c39` `48 81 c1 28 03 00 00`), while the constructor `0x7e58a0` makes both blocks (`0xb89710`) at `+0x278` and `+0x2f0`, their conditions a small vector at the block's `+8` with its count at `+0x14`, as the verdict `0xb98200` reads them. The policies' reader shifts the same way (`potential` read at `+0x1b8`, the block at `+0x180`). Read at the reader's offsets, every `potential` held in the game (the run).
- **No deity**: the game's `Null` deity, at `[0x233fe90]`, made by `0x7e56c0` (`0x7e5766` `48 89 1d 23 a7 b5 01`), which the lookup `0x7e6830` answers for a key no deity has; it is in the database's vector, first.
- **The country's deity**: country `+0xfa0`, a pointer to the deity, the no-deity while it follows none; the setter `0x3d6bf0(country, deity)` (`0x3d6c12` `48 8b 89 a0 0f 00 00`) runs the old one's `removed_effect` and stores the new one.
- **Whether a religion has them**: `0xa7eda0(religion)`: the religion's byte `+0x117` (`personal_deity = yes`), and the expansion that brings them.
- **The command's check** `0xaae300`: the deity's own `+0x40`, the country's tag valid (`+0x57`), and its state religion (country `+0xf30`) having deities. **It reads neither the deity's `potential` nor its `trigger`.**
- **The view's button** (`0xfddb00`) asks the same and posts; whether the player may follow another deity now is `0x3d6e70(country, text)`, which the view asks for the button (`0xff1874`): false during a regency (the ruler's `+0x78` set), with `CANT_CHANGE_DIETY_REGENCY` in the text, and true otherwise, also while a deity is followed ("last until your monarch changes", `RV_NO_DEITY_DESC`).
- **What the framework offers**, as the view's list does (_inferred_ from the files' `potential`/`trigger` pairs and the decisions' and policies' views, the list itself not read): the deities whose `potential` holds, each allowed when `0x3d6e70` allows it and its `trigger` holds, with the regency's line and `trigger`'s failing lines as the reasons.

## Cults

- **The cults**: a `std::vector` of pointers of the game's own at `0x2328a78`, its end at `0x2328a80` (the getter `0x7faf20`, `0x7faf40` `48 8d 05 31 db b2 01`; the destructor `0x1b5ec60` walks it, `0x1b5ec79` `48 3b 1d 00 9e 7c 00`), in file order; a name table follows (`+0x18` count, `+0x1c` buckets, `+0x20` the buckets).
- **No cult**: an object of the game's at `0x245b190`, made on first use by `0x7fae20` (`0x7fae40` `48 8d 05 49 03 c6 01`), which a country that has cults follows while it follows none; its key is `nocult`, and the cults' vector holds it among them (seen in the game).
- **A cult** (`0x7fad10`): its key `+0x10` (`0xaae5a6` `48 83 c3 10`), `ai_will_do` `+0x78`, its modifiers `+0xb0`, `allow` `+0x200` (read at `+0x238`, `0x7fad78` `48 81 c1 38 02 00 00`, as a deity's triggers are), `effect` `+0x278`, `removed_effect` `+0x2b0`, `sprite` `+0x74`, `default` `+0x2e8`. Its `allow` holds the religion, the technology group or `has_unlocked_cult`, and the primary cult's rule (`common/fetishist_cults`).
- **The country's cult**: country `+0xfa8`, the cult or no cult; the setter `0x3d6f00(country, cult)`.
- **Whether a religion has them**: `0xa7ee00(religion)`: the religion's byte `+0x118` (`fetishist_cult = yes`), and the expansion.
- **The command's check** `0xaae6a0`: the cult not no cult, the tag valid, the state religion having cults. It does not read `allow`.
- **Whether the player may embrace one now**: `0x3d7850(country, text)`: false during a regency, with `CANT_CHANGE_CULT_REGENCY`; otherwise true only while the country follows no cult (a cult "last[s] until your monarch changes", `RV_NO_CULT_DESC`), false without a line while it follows one, and false with a null pointer. The framework gives that last refusal its own line.
- **What the framework offers**: every cult, while the religion has them, each allowed when `0x3d7850` allows it and its `allow` holds (_inferred_ as for deities).

## Church aspects and blessings

Read on 2026-11-11 from the code and the running game's memory (the 1700 save, as Sweden and Ethiopia); what the framework does with it and what the game showed is in [the run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-religion-aspects-blessings.md).

- **The aspects' database** (`common/church_aspects`): a singleton at `[0x242bcb8]`, 0x60 bytes, made by its getter `0x7f6ef0` with a name table as the deities' (`+0` count, `+4` 511 buckets, `+8` the buckets), then three `std::vector`s of pointers: every aspect with the game's no-aspect first (`+0x10`; `noaspect`, the object at `[0x2458be0]`), the aspects a church chooses among (`+0x28`: the Protestants', the Hussites', the Jewish), and the blessings (`+0x40`, no-aspect first: `is_blessing = yes`, the Copts' and the Zoroastrians'). An aspect's key is a `std::string` at `+0x10`; its `cost`, i32 thousandths of church power, at `+0x358` (`0x7f8fe2` `8b 87 58 03 00 00`; 100000 for every Protestant aspect, the files' `cost = 100`).
- **A religion's lists**: the aspects its church offers at religion `+0x730` (what `0x7f8270(church)` answers, `0x7f82a5` `48 05 30 07 00 00`; Protestantism's 16, `anglican_aspect` among them), and its blessings at `+0x760` (`0x37adeb`; the Copts' five). Every other religion's are empty in the 1700 save.
- **A country's church**: country `+0xf68` (`0xaae7c7` `48 8b b8 68 0f 00 00`), an object with a vtable whose `+0x40` says it is valid: its church power, i32 thousandths, at `+0x10` (Sweden's 200000), its country at `+0x18`, and its slots, a `std::vector` of pointers at `+0x20`, three in the 1700 save, the no-aspect in a free one.
- **A country's blessings**: country `+0xf70`, a `std::vector` of pointers to the blessings taken (`0x37aef6`); Ethiopia's two.
- **`change_church_aspect`**: `{ index=N aspect="K" country="T" operation=B }`, `index` written only when not negative. The view's button (`0xfdc0f0`) keeps the aspect (`+0x1c0`), a slot (`+0x1c8`) and the operation (`+0x1cc`), asks the same as the command's check and posts. The check (`0xaaee40`) wants the aspect valid, the country's state religion one with church power (`0xa7e6b0`, its byte `+0x116`), the church valid, the slot below the slots' count (so `-1`, no slot, passes), and by operation:
  - **0, take into a free slot**: `0x7f8e70(church, aspect, text)`, which writes the view's lines into the `std::string`: a free slot (`CHANGE_ASPECT_TOO_MANY`) and the church power (`0x7f8fe2`: the aspect's cost against `+0x10`, with the power's name from the religion, `0xa7e910`/`0xa7e9f0`). The execute (`0xaae93f`) takes the slot given if it is free, and otherwise finds a free one (`0x7f81f0`), sets it (`0x7f7f30`) and takes the cost from the church power (`0x7f7ce0`).
  - **1, give up a chosen aspect**: the aspect among the slots, and `0x7f9610(church, text)`, whether the aspects may change now: stability at least a define (`[0x233e664]`) less 3, with `CHANGE_ASPECT_TOO_LOW_STAB`. The execute finds the aspect's slot itself and frees it.
  - **2, replace the aspect in a slot**: `0x7f8ba0(church, aspect)` (the power, the aspect not chosen, the religion's list); the execute also takes stability by the define (`0xaae816`).
- **`change_blessing`**: `{ aspect="K" country="T" }`. Its check (`0xaaf3d0`), after the multiplayer guard, is `0x37adb0(country, aspect, text)`: the aspect valid, `0x37aea0(country, text)` (the country's religion keeping its holy sites, with `LOST_HOLY_SITES` and how many of the blessings last taken lapse), and the aspect in the religion's blessings.

## The defender of the faith

Read from `eu4.exe` on 2026-11-11 with `cargo xtask disasm` and scripts over the PE file (the command vtables, the token table, the strings' references), for the second half of M20 of [mod-capabilities.md](../plans/mod-capabilities.md); what was tried in the game is in [its run](../prototype-log/2026-11-11-defender-and-golden-era.md).

- **The command** `claimdefender` (token `0x2925`, vtable `0x1ce0e78`: writer `0x2a4d40`, the writer every command of the country alone shares, `country` (`0x289d`) only; check `0xaacbf0`; execute `0xaac190`): `claimdefender={ country="T" }`.
- **The check** `0x300b40(country, _, text) -> bool`, the religion view's button's tooltip, writing its lines into the `std::string` text when it is not null; the command's check calls it with none (`0xaacbf0`; its second argument is not read). In turn: the religion's group allows no defender (`+0xd8` of the religion's `+0xf30`, `+0x98`; `RELIGION_GROUP_NOT_DOF`); too few technology (`NOT_TECH_ADVANCED_ENOUGH_DOF`); the treasury (`+0xbb0`) below the define at `[0x233e274]` in whole ducats, times 1000 (`NEED_MONEY`, `MONEY`); a subject (`IS_SUBJECT`); a regency (`HAVE_REGENCY_COUNCIL`, the ruler's `+0x78`); a female ruler (the ruler's `+0x74`) where the religion does not allow her (the religion's `+0x11b`, `RULER_IS_FEMALE`); the country the defender already (`IS_DEFENDER_OF_FAITH`); less prestige than the defender (`NEED_MORE_PRESTIGE_THAN_DEFENDER`); a date before which it may not be claimed again (`UNTIL_DATE`, `DATE`); a revolutionary (`IS_REVOLUTIONARY`); then the scripted function at `+0xa8` of `common/scripted_functions` (through its getter `0xacd3a0`), whose failing lines `0xaccac0` writes into the text.
- **Who defends a religion**: the world's `+0x25a8` points to an object whose `+0x30` is an array of 0x50-byte records, one for each religion by its index (the religion's `+0x70`) less one (`0x3012c1`); a record's `+0x34` is the tag of the country that defends it, valid while its last byte (`+0x3b`) is set. A defender with no provinces (`+0x1344`) and of no kind 7 counts as none (`0x30132d`).

## Religious reforms

Read on 2026-11-11 from the code (`common/religious_reforms`; `PS_BUY_RELIGIOUS_REFORM = 0` in `common/defines.lua`).

- **The database**: a singleton at `[0x242bda8]`, made by its getter `0xa82ee0`, 0x50 bytes, laid out as the idea groups' ([government.md](government.md), "Ideas"): its reform groups and its reforms, `std::vector`s of pointers. A reform group is an idea group (`nahuatl_reforms`, `inti_reforms`, `mesoamerican_reforms`): its reforms at `+0x30`, `can_buy_idea` at `+0xc0`, its category, the kind of monarch power its reforms are spent in, at `+0x570`. A reform is an idea: its key at `+0x158`, its group at `+0x198`, its index in the database's reforms at `+0x1a0`; its vtable's `+0xa0` says it is a reform.
- **A country's reforms**: country `+0x1450`, a `std::vector` of 0x20-byte entries, one for each reform group of its religion: the group at `+0`, and at `+8` a `std::vector` of i32, one for each of the group's reforms, nonzero once passed (`0x3321aa`, where adding an idea that is a reform finds the group's entry and sets the reform's flag to 1). Reforms are passed in any order.
- **The button** (`0x139d930`, the reforms window's item, the ideas window's own item): `0x84f750(group, tag)`, the group's `can_buy_idea`; then a spending of kind 31 when the idea is a reform (kind 0 otherwise), its skill the group's category (`0x139d9bb`), its data the reform's index (`0x139da22`); its price against the points of the skill; then a confirmation window. **The spend_power check's kind 31** (`0x94f33e`) asks the same `0x84f750` and nothing of which reforms are passed; its price (`0x9528b0`) is the define `[0x233f628]` × 1000, 0 in the unmodded game; its execute (`0x94fd35`) adds the idea (`0x331b90(country, reform, 1, 0)`).
- **Reforming the religion**: `reform_religion_command` `{ country="T" }`, the window's `reform_religion` button. Its check (`0x2af4b0`): the country not `+0x28a7`, its religion's byte `+0x58b` (it has reforms), `0x9759c0` (a guard of the game's threads), every reform's flag set, and `0x3cd850(country, tag out, i32 out)`, whom the country would learn from, whose tag's valid byte must be set (`0x2af581`). `0x3cd850` walks the countries the country knows (`+0x1ac0`) and the technology levels; the window's lines for it (`REFORM_RELIGION_EXPL_DET_2` and its kin) were not followed, so the framework words that refusal itself.

## Fervor

- **The database** (`common/fervor`): a singleton at `[0x242bc20]`, made by its getter `0x735500` (`0x73550a`), 0x30 bytes: a name table, then its effects, a `std::vector` of pointers at `+0x10` (the loader `0x7362f0`, `0x7363c7`), in file order.
- **An effect** (class `0x1cae138`, made by `0x735120`): its key a `std::string` at `+0x10` (its vtable's `+0x48`, `0x9a920`, answers it), its number, its place in the list, at `+0x30` (`+0x50`, `0x8b0c0`), its cost in fervor, whole, at `+0x5c` (`+0x68`, `0x735030`), its `potential` at `+0x210`, another trigger block at `+0x288`; its `+0x40` answers yes (`0x8f440`).
- **A country's fervor**: country `+0xf60`, an object with the effects on at `+0x10` (a `std::vector` of pointers), its fervor at `+0x28` in thousandths, at most `[0x233f480]`, and its country at `+0x30`.
- **`toggle_fervor_effect`** `{ country="T" effect=N }`, `N` the effect's place in the list: its check (`0x9bfa40`) is the multiplayer guard alone; its execute (`0xaadc30`) is `0x736f60(fervor, effect, 1)`, which turns the effect off when it is on, and otherwise takes its cost × 1000 from the fervor (no lower than 0) and turns it on. Several may be on.
- **The view** (`0xff97e0` lists its entries; `0xfe2a20` makes one; `0xfe2d90` updates its button; `0xfe2f00` posts): it lists the effects that are on and those whose `potential` holds, in the game's own scope of the country; an effect that is on has its button enabled, to turn it off; one that is off when `0x736a40(fervor, effect, text)` holds, the check that writes the view's lines (its conditions at `+0x210` and the rest). The religion's part of the view is shown while `0xa7b5c0(religion)` holds (its byte `+0x111`).

## Harmonization

- **`harmonize_with_religion`** `{ country="T" religion=N }`, `N` a religion's number (religion `+0x70`, `0xe40641`); `-1` stops the harmonization going on, which the check allows while the country's `+0x1040` (the religion) or `+0x1048` (the group) is set.
- **The candidates**: `0x3ec460(country, list, i32 out or null, flag)` fills a small vector of the game's (class `0x1c40840`; data `+8`, capacity `+0x10`, count `+0x14`, its storage inline after it, ten entries in the check's own) with 0x18-byte entries: a religion of the player's religion's group as `{ religion, null }`, another group as `{ null, group }`. It walks the country's provinces (`+0x1338`), leaving out the state religion, the religion at `+0xf38`, the religion and the group harmonizing now and those harmonized already (`0x3ec320`, `0x3ec3c0`), and weighs their development; its order and its development test were not followed. The check (`0xab0b90`) wants the religion harmonizing (`0xa7eed0`) and the target among the candidates; a religion's group is at `+0xd8`, and a group starts with a `std::vector` of its religions, the first of which the view's button (`0xe40530`) names for a group.
- **The reasons** the view shows for a religion it does not offer (`HARMONIZE_NOT_ENOUGH_DEVELOPMENT` and the rest) are written by a widget's tooltip (`0xe3d060`), not by a function of the country, so the framework offers only the candidates, each allowed.

## Icons and metropolitans

- **Icons** (`commission_icon` `{ country="T" icon=N }`, token `0xb5`): the check (`0xab1790`) wants the religion to have icons (`0xa7ec60`), `N` a place in the religion's icons (a `std::vector` of pointers at `+0x7b8`), and `0x404f20(country, icon, text)`, which writes the view's lines: the icon commissioned already (country `+0xf90`, `ORTHIC_NOT_CURR` with the icon's name, a `std::string` at `+0x18`), too little patriarch authority (country `+0xf88`, thousandths of the whole, against `[0x233dc28]`, `ORTHODOX_ICON_AUTHORITY_COST`, `ORTHIC_NO_AUTH`), and the icon's own conditions (`+0x1b8`).
- **Metropolitans** (`consecrate_patriarch_command` `{ country="T" province=N }`, token `0x2843`): the check (`0xab0ee0`) takes the province's state (province `+0x2e0`) and asks `0xabf060(state, text)`, which writes the province view's lines (`STATE_PATRIARCH_ALREADY`, `STAPAT_LOW_DEV`, …).

## Piety

Read on 2026-11-11 from the code (`NReligion`'s `PIETY_*` defines in `common/defines.lua`; the `PIETY_*` lines of `localisation`). Piety is a country's i32 at `+0xf9c`, thousandths of the scale's half: 1000 is wholly Legalism (`positive_piety`), −1000 wholly Mysticism (`negative_piety`); a religion uses it while its byte `+0x114` is set (`0x3e5596`).

- **The commands** `use_high_piety` (vtable `0x1c3a1a8`) and `use_low_piety` (`0x1c3a250`) share the writer `0xc11a0`, which writes `members` (`0x2aa9`) as a tuple of one, the tag the command keeps at `+0x50` (the tuple's vtable `0x1c38490`, its writer `0xc8ce0` writing `dummy`, `0xe6`): `use_high_piety={ members={ dummy="TAG" } }`. The view's buttons (`0xff7210`, `0xff7590`) put the player's tag there and post.
- **Their checks**: `use_high_piety`'s (`0xc1370`) wants the religion's byte, the multiplayer guard, piety at least the define `[0x233dcd0]` (`PIETY_ABS_THRESHOLD`), and no entry of token `0x3789` in the country's cooldowns (`+0xeb8`, a `std::vector` of 8-byte entries, a token and a date) later than today. `use_low_piety`'s (`0xc1250`) is `0x3e5560(country, null)`.

## Islamic schools' scholars

Read from `eu4.exe` on 2026-11-11 with `cargo xtask disasm` and scripts over the PE file (the view's button, the command's writer, reader and check, the school lookup and the willing-country check), for `religion.invite_scholar`.

- **The schools** are a religion group's (`religious_schools` in `common/religions`): the group's `+0xa0` points to their database, null for a group with none (the view's button `0xfd9fb0` falls back on an empty one at `[0x2458ce8]`); the database's `+0x20` and `+0x2c` are the game's own array of the schools, a pointer each, which the lookup `0xdacf90(database, key)` walks. - **The school a country follows** is a pointer at country `+0x28b8`.
- **The command** `invite_scholar` (vtable `0x1ce0548`, its type token `0x3723`, which the button sets) carries the country at `+0x50` and the school at `+0x58`.
