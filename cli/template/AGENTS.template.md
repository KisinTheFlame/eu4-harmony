# Template Mod

A Harmony mod: Lua the framework runs in the game, in a sandbox, through its capabilities.

- Before changing the mod, read `eu4-harmony skills` and the guide for the work in front of you; `eu4-harmony skills mod` walks through making one.
- What a capability takes and answers is in `eu4-harmony wiki`; the handlers and globals in `eu4-harmony wiki lua`, the manifest in `eu4-harmony wiki manifest`.
- `eu4-harmony.exe` is built from Harmony's source (its README, Building from source); `cargo xtask install` puts it in the game's `eu4-harmony\` folder.
